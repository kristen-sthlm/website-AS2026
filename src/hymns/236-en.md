# 1.
Sweet moment, when we may sit down
Here at Thy feet, beloved Lord!
To the green pastures Thou dost lead us,
And for us Thou art the fountain of life.

# 2.
This rest Thou dost gladly grant us
Amid the toil, sorrow, and strife of time,
Still flow the wells of bliss
Of infinite love, grace, and peace.

# 3.
Thy words strengthen and comfort us,
They are light upon this dark earth,
Our hearts rejoice when Thou dost speak,
And we feel the power of Thy word.

# 4.
Lord Jesus, enlighten our eyes,
That we may see the wonders in Thy word!
May Thy Spirit work unhindered,
Grant to each one a rich blessing!
