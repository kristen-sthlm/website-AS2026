# 1.
To Thee we now bring the sacrifice of praise,
Our Father, Saviour, and God.
Our hearts would honour Thee
And magnify Thee aloud with the sound of lips,
With reverence think upon Thy grace,
Thy power, Thy wondrous counsel.

# 2.
Our God and Father, Thee we praise,
We rejoice in Thy Father’s name,
And the heart’s thanks as an offering
We bring through Christ.
Thou, who art worthy of power and glory,
We render Thee our homage.

# 3.
Thou hast established the order of things,
Thou hast set a limit to the trial,
But for our hope, our blessedness, none,
For Thou, O God, art love.
Thou bearest all in the Father’s hands
And knowest every soul’s desire.

# 4.
Therefore, O Father in the highest,
We commit our welfare to Thy hand.
We know that Thine gracious eye
Beholds with joy the faith of Thy children.
On the path of sorrow or of joy
Thou ever leadest us home to Thee.
