# 1.
Lord, we praise Thee, Thou who hast been dead,
But now livest forever in heaven’s height.
Lord, we worship Thee, Thou hast redeemed us.
In spirit we have reached the home Thou hast won for us;
For salvation we have found through Thy blood.

# 2.
O God, we confess Thy glory’s greatness;
For of Thee, and through Thee, and to Thee are all things.
How rich is Thy goodness, how great is Thy salvation!
In spirit and truth we may worship Thee,
Our God and our Father. Amen and Amen!
