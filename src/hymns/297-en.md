# 1.
We long, Lord, to see Thee soon
In glory and splendour,
That all the world may clearly behold
Thy greatness and Thy power.

# 2.
When Thy triumph, Thy victory shall
Be clearly manifest to all,
And praise and homage be given to
Him who is now despised.

# 3.
We know, and it is our joy,
That God hath highly exalted Thee,
But we long that Thou, highly uplifted,
Shalt be revealed to every one.

# 4.
When every opposition is gone,
And Thou alone art great,
That then to Thee, beloved Lord,
Shall be the desire of every soul.
