# 1.
O what a Saviour, Jesus, Lamb of God!
Well may His holy ones praise His name!
For He has redeemed them from judgment by His blood,
Saved them for eternity and brought them to God.

# 2.
Now He will give from the height of His glory
Peace to consciences and to every heart its joy,
Awaits in grace to set free each one,
Who in sin is held oppressed.

# 3.
Thousands have fled to His saving grace,
All welcomed, none despised,
Weary, burdened, all have received comfort,
Blessedly they rest at the Saviour’s breast.
