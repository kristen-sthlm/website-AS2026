# 1.
Let us await our God
Despite the world’s scorn and threat!
If the Lord is on our side,
Who can stand against us?
His power He grants
To those who walk in His commandments,
All things serve for good
For those who love God.

# 2.
Do not strive for riches and honor
In this world;
Let sorrow not consume you
For this brief time.
It is for other battles
The Lord has called you;
It is for other times
He has reserved your reward.

# 3.
Here it is a matter of working
His work, who has sent us,
With prayer and the Word to strengthen
The life His Spirit has kindled.
Here it is a matter of seeking
First His righteousness,
And of increasing His praise
In time and eternity.
