# 1.
All eyes shall behold the Lord,
When He comes in His glory.
Say, what does His coming bid thee:
Eternal woe or salvation?
Behold Him now and receive life,
Which in God’s Son alone is found,
The blood, which once was shed,
It has power to wash thee clean and pure.

# 2.
The Lord’s voice one day thou must hear,
Thou, who hast never hearkened to it yet.
What message shall it bring to thee,
Shalt thou know it as from a friend?
Or shall His thunderous voice resound
Into the depths of thy poor soul,
And with irresistible might command thee:
“Depart from Me!” O, consider well!

# 3.
Jesus’ name shall thy knees also bow,
If not before, yet on the day of judgment.
O that day, how it shall reveal
Crimes and sins of every kind!
Wouldst thou not flee from the judgment seat?
Jesus’ name offers thee salvation still.
Bow now, in faith receive the Son,
The Judge would be thy best Friend.

# 4.
Every tongue shall one day confess:
Jesus is Lord over all.
Then His wrath shall burn each one,
Who has refused Him homage here.
Consider thee: life and death are at stake;
Seek thy salvation, while yet there is time.
Death’s sickle may soon fell thee;
Flee to Jesus, He will give thee peace.

# 5.
Eye, ear, heart, mouth, and tongue,
All are His, who bought thee with His blood.
O, why wilt thou not begin to sing
His praise already now with joyful heart?
Soon the morning of glory shall dawn,
Which knows no cloud or darkness.
Shall thy voice be blended in heaven’s songs?
Shalt thou share in this glory?
