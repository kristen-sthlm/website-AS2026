# 1.
Our Jesus, we are reminded,
How Thou hast suffered,
How the dark waves of judgment
Have passed over Thee here.
In death’s deep waters
Thou wast baptized, when Thy blood
Thou didst shed for us, O Lord,
And stood in our place.

# 2.
For sin Thou once didst suffer,
That we might not suffer,
And Thou art now risen;
Thy glory we shall receive.
As the firstfruits from the dead
Thou didst burst the grave’s gate,
Thou hast overcome death,
A glorious work accomplished.

# 3.
With Thee we have died, O Christ,
We were baptized unto Thy death;
With Thee, our life, we are risen
Unto life in abundance.
From sin, the world, Satan
Thou hast loosed us by Thy blood,
And unto all truth leadeth
Us now Thy good Spirit.
