# 1.
No distance between Christ
And His redeemed bride.
Himself on the cross He removed
All that separated us from God.

# 2.
He Himself has sanctified Himself for us,
Sanctification for us He won,
Promised to be in the midst of us,
When we gather unto His name.

# 3.
He has given us of His Spirit,
Brought us into His liberty.
The bride can say from the heart:
“I am His, and He is mine.”

# 4.
Ever may He be our confidant,
He who is our heart’s Friend,
That we may apprehend His will
And with joy do it.
