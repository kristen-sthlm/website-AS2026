# 1.
Eternal, eternal glory to the Lord Jesus give!
Proclaim aloud His victory, how He gave His life!
He willingly left His home above,
Came in grace and love to die upon the cross.

# C.
Eternal, eternal glory to the Lord Jesus give!
Proclaim aloud His victory, how He gave His life!

# 2.
He came down from heaven, suffered in our stead,
Firstborn from the dead, Him now adore!
Jesus, meek and lowly, death has crushed,
He came here to save what was lost.

# 3.
Lord of glory, worthy all the praise,
Him now acknowledge, He shall reign!
Sing aloud, proclaim how He yet loves,
Joy now and eternally in the Saviour!
