# 1.
Thou tender Father-heart
That watchest over me,
How shall I be able to love,
How be able to praise Thee?
Thou warnest me so faithfully
Against all hidden snares,
And hidest under Thy wing
Thy timid, fearful child.

# 2.
Thou feedest and Thou clothest,
And guardest me so well,
And carest every day
For both body and soul.
I should soon be undone,
If Thou didst not do so.
O, could I but love,
But love Thee still!

# 3.
Grant that with holy obedience
I may ever give Thee right!
Make Thy commandments dear to me,
Thy burden sweet and light!
And when a danger threatens,
A need alarms me,
Let me in the first place
But ever flee to Thee!

# 4.
Thy Father-heart breaketh
With compassion,
Thou seest whatsoever I lack,
Thou knowest all my woe.
By night and by day
Thine eye open standeth,
And the Father-hand reacheth,
As far as heaven extendeth.
