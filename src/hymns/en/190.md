# 1.
Up, my soul, to praise thy God
For His great grace,
For His Son, His precious gift,
For His comfort and counsel!
For His comfort and counsel!

# 2.
Christ has blotted out my sin,
Granted me peace and light;
Through the cross’s death He has opened
The way to the Father’s house,
The way to the Father’s house.

# 3.
Pure and free I now am become,
Sonship I have received;
Blessed already here on earth:
What a glorious portion!
What a glorious portion!

# 4.
Nothing can condemn me,
Free from guilt I am.
God can never forget me,
I am dear to Him,
I am dear to Him.

# 5.
Though in clouds He seems to hide,
Yet He is near to me;
Though trials may follow me,
|: It is only love. :|

# 6.
Rejoice therefore, O my heart,
Raise a joyful sound!
Rejoice amid all thy sorrow,
|: Rejoice in thy God! :|
