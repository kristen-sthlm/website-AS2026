# 1.
We are not at home on the earth,
But journey to the land of bliss,
And Jesus our Shepherd has become,
Who leads us there with His hand.
Should the world drive us away from itself
And close its doors to us here,
No one can take our home from us,
For it is in heaven.

# 2.
Soon we shall enter into the joy
And sing the song of the blessed,
When Jesus meets us on high
And brings us to the many mansions.
How sweet with the company of the saints,
When we receive the crown and the robe,
It will be to be with the Lord
Forever and no more to depart.

# 3.
O, hold us then, Lord, by the hand,
The time we yet may dwell here,
Until there on the heavenly shore
In sight our faith is exchanged;
And help us to follow Thee faithfully,
Whatever way Thou mayest lead us!
Thou wilt conceal us in Thy pavilion,
Until we reach Thee and Thy heaven.
