# 1.
Thy night of sorrow is ended,
An eternal sun has risen,
Our Lord Jesus Christ, for Thou
Thy glory hast attained.

# 2.
That day shines forth,
Whose sun never sets,
For the Son of Man is now exalted
In the Father’s glory.

# 3.
Thou wast the grain of wheat
That fell into the earth;
A harvest Thou didst bring forth to Thy God,
Alone Thou art no more.

# 4.
And now around Thyself
Thou gatherest Thy company,
To lead it in the Spirit’s power
Into the Father’s presence.

# 5.
In Thy counsel, O God,
Thou hast laid this plan,
Thy work, our beloved Lord Jesus,
The whole hath accomplished.
