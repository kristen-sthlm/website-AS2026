# 1.
Safer can no one be
Than God’s little child-gathering;
Over it He has compassion,
Bears it in His Father’s arms.

# 2.
No distress and no fortune
Shall from His hand us snatch,
He, our Friend beyond other friends,
His children’s cares knows.

# 3.
Rejoice then, thou little gathering!
God His own shall preserve;
Before His will must all
Enemies to the earth fall.

# 4.
What He takes and what He gives,
The same Father He remains,
And His aim is but the one—
The child’s true welfare alone.

# 5.
Praise be to Thee, our God and Father,
That our song of praise Thou dost receive!
Grace’s riches we have tasted,
Which Thou never dost withdraw.

# 6.
And at last, when hence we journey
Through heaven’s bright clouds,
Shall we Thee better praise
For Thy great gift of grace.
