# 1.
Lord, Thou art our strength,
This we perceive.
In days of weakness here,
Strength in Thee we find.

# C.
Near Thee, near Thee,
Help us to abide,
Till with the great redeemed throng
Glory to Thee we give.

# 2.
Jesus, Thou who for us died,
Thou art the fountain of life;
Freely, open to each one,
Grace’s streams do flow.

# 3.
Water in the desert is not found,
But Thou dost grant us
With joy to draw therefrom
The wells of salvation.

# 4.
Lord, Thou art our praise,
Strength in the weak,
Forward in the world of light
Thou wilt draw Thy people.
