# 1.
Thus God loved the whole world
That He gave forth His only Son,
That whosoever believes on Him
Shall have eternal life.

# 2.
For therein consists the love,
Not that we have loved God,
But therein that He loved us
And gave us His Son.

# 3.
Yea, therein is God’s love great
Which was made manifest to us,
That for atonement for all our sin
He has sent His Son.

# 4.
He has sent us Jesus Christ,
That on Him we should believe,
That through Him we may live
And love for His sake.
