# 1.
Soon we shall dwell with Thee,
Our Jesus, where Thou art;
Thou who art Thyself our heart’s joy
And all our soul’s desire.

# 2.
Our blessed hope now is,
O Lord, to see Thee
Descend to go to meet
Thy bride in the clouds.

# 3.
Yet even now in faith
We may in heaven dwell
And enjoy in our Father’s bosom
An unutterable rest.
