# 1.
O God of all grace, who can measure Thy grace,
The depth of Thy heart’s love, when Thou didst give
Thine only Son to lay down His life here!
He was obedient, He suffered the death of the cross,
He who in the earth’s desert, barren and dry,
Was “the fresh tree” in Thy sight.

# 2.
“I lay down My life and take it again,
For this commandment I have received of My Father.”
We thank Thee, our God, for this commandment;
Thanks that Thy Son has died and has risen.
Thou didst not permit Him, Thy Holy One,
In the dark grave to see corruption.

# 3.
Thy glory, O Father, hath raised Him
In the power of an incorruptible life.
He liveth now and beareth fruit unto Thee.
Grant our hearts more to see His beauty!
For “the tree of life in God’s paradise,”
To our God be glory, eternal praise and honour!
