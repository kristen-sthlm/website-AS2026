# 1.
Sinners’ Saviour, Jesus,
Saviour is He for me also.
I, who in sin was bound,
Through His grace am now free.

# C.
Saviour is He,
Sinners’ Saviour here.
He gave Himself a ransom,
He now my Saviour is.

# 2.
I know that I am forgiven,
Happy, righteous, and free,
Saved by my beloved Lord,
Saviour is He for me.

# 3.
Just as I was, He received me,
When from judgment I would flee.
Now no condemnation remains,
Saviour is He for me.

# 4.
Loved with love unending,
I receive blessing rich.
How shall I His praise sing?
Saviour is He for me.

# 5.
Soon dawns the glorious day,
When His face I shall see.
Sing, O my heart, in joy,
Saviour is He for me!
