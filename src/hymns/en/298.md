# 1.
O Lord, come
And take Thy people to Thyself,
That Thy flock, so scattered here,
With Thee may be gathered there;
Come, Lord, come!

# 2.
Come, Lord, come!
For he who has the bride,
It is the Bridegroom, and Thou
Art He whom we now long for,
Come, Lord, come!

# 3.
Come, Lord, come!
And during the waiting hour,
Grant us to stand girded,
With lamps clear, burning;
Come, Lord, come!

# 4.
O Lord, come!
Thou art our heart’s hope;
With sweet voice Thou hast told us,
“I come quickly,” we answer gladly:
Yea, Lord, come!
