# 1.
O our Father, we would bring Thee praise
In a manner worthy of Thy name,
For Thou seekest worshippers even today,
And a worship spiritual and true.
All worlds were created through Thy Son,
Through Him Thou hast spoken unto us;
It is He who upholds all by the word of His power,
And all moves only as He wills.

# 2.
Now with one accord we may with one soul and one mouth
Worship Thee with the sound of praise,
And in Thy gathering Thy beloved Son
Himself praises Thee, our Father and God.
He has taken us up for Thine honour, and now
Wills to lead continually our song,
And thus bring us into the joy of Thy sanctuary,
That we may praise Thee among many brethren.
