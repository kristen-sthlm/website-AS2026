# 1.
In heaven, in heaven,
Where God our Father dwells,
How glorious shall the bliss be,
How inexpressibly great,
There face to face
We shall eternally, eternally God behold
And Jesus Himself, the Lamb of God.

# 2.
In heaven, in heaven
What brightness, high and pure!
Not even the sun resembles it
In its noonday splendour.
The sun that never sets,
But stands eternally unclouded,
Is the Lord Jesus Christ.

# 3.
In heaven, in heaven
No tears are seen;
No death, no corruption
Shall there ravage more.
An eternal joy and glory
And peace and incorruptibility
From the Lord are given us.

# 4.
In heaven, in heaven
A bliss Thou hast prepared,
Which no ear has heard yet
And no eye has seen;
Yet now revealed it is
To those who have the Lord dear,
Our Lord Jesus Christ!
