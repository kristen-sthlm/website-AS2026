# 1.
Our Lord, Thee we now praise,
Teach us to appreciate more
Thy love for the gathering,
Which Thou didst dearly purchase here.

# 2.
Thine beloved for evermore
Thou bearest upon Thy heart,
And cleansest in the washing of the word
On the wilderness path here.

# 3.
Soon without spot or wrinkle
Thou wilt present to Thyself
The gathering in snow-white array,
Holy and without blame.
