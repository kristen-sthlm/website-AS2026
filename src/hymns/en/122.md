# 1.
“One spirit with the Lord,” O Lord most great,
With Thee already here on earth!
What heavenly light, what power divine
That dwells within that word!

# 2.
In Thy resurrection
Thou art not there alone:
The church Thou unitest with Thee,
With Thee, her Head, on high.

# 3.
With joy we behold Thee,
Glorified with God.
Thou lovest well the church,
Thy body and Thy bride.

# 4.
United now with Thee
In heaven's blessèd rest,
Soon to Thyself Thou bear'st us home,
With Thee to dwell for aye.
