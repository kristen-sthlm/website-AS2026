# 1.
Peace, true peace, amid earth’s sin and strife?
Yea, Jesus’ blood gives the heart rest and peace.

# 2.
Peace, true peace, amid pressing toil and care?
On Jesus’ will’s path all unrest flees.

# 3.
Peace, true peace, when all feels hard and heavy?
At Jesus’ heart, there is stillness, calm.

# 4.
Peace, true peace, where friends are not?
Yea, Jesus bears us and our loved ones.

# 5.
Peace, true peace, when unknown future dawns?
We know Jesus, He our vessel steers.

# 6.
Peace, true peace, in death’s dark domain?
Yea, Jesus has subdued death and its power.

# 7.
It is enough for us: soon earth’s strife shall end
And Jesus calls us to eternal peace.
