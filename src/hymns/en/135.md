# 1.
God in love the Son hath given
For a world in sins sunk down;
He on the cross was hanged,
Many sins there He bore.

# 2.
Rule shall death no more,
Jesus liveth, life He giveth;
Prince of life Christ is,
Life’s crown He bestoweth.

# 3.
Lion of Judah’s tribe,
Jesus, God’s risen Lamb,
Now in glory’s height
Is He God’s chiefest joy.

# 4.
All who on Christ believe
Have eternal life and rest,
And they shall Him see
Come soon in the clouds.

# 5.
O, what glorious grace-light
Shineth forth in the Saviour
To proclaim to the sinner here:
God is both light and love.
