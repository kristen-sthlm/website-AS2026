# 1.
On Golgotha it was so dark,
For us no shadow remains,
For Thou art our righteousness,
O Jesus, crowned with glory.

# 2.
In Thee before our God we stand,
To partake of His full favour;
When Thou didst descend into death’s depths,
A great company Thou didst gain as reward.

# 3.
Thou art exalted, yet still we see
The love that endured the cross’s woe.
Thou, who hast borne death here,
Now livest for Thine own there.

# 4.
Our Lord Christ in heaven’s height,
We praise Thee with holy joy;
Yea, before Thee Thy ransomed host
With reverence raises a shout of gladness.

# 5.
Thy power knows no bounds,
Thy love is of eternity,
Thy truth shall stand as the rock,
When earth and heaven shall pass away.

# 6.
Thy love turns not back,
Yesterday, today the same still,
Son of God, who hast laid earth’s foundation,
Lamb of God, who hast brought full atonement.
