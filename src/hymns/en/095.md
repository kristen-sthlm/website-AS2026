# 1.
Thanks be to God, who has given us the victory
Through our Saviour, mighty and good!
Thanks be to God, that we have been cleansed
Through the holy blood of our Passover Lamb!
Thanks be to God for the living hope,
Which has risen above death and the grave
Through the victory of the Risen One!

# 2.
Praise Thee, O Christ, for light over the tomb;
Praise for Thy promise, Thy holy peace!
Praise Thee, who speakest to us through the scriptures,
Strengthening our heart in the time of trial!
Praise Thee, that opened eyes we receive,
That we may see Thee and recognise Thee,
Thou, who wast dead and who livest!
