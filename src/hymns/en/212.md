# 1.
When the Lord is my Shepherd,
What can be lacking to me?
All, all that I need,
I shall receive from Him.
In the green pastures of the Word
He feeds His lambs,
To the fresh springs of life
He Himself leads them forth.

# 2.
And if from the flock strayed
A single sheep, He will not
Leave it behind in the desert
A prey for the wolf, no;
He seeks it in love,
The faithful Saviour,
And faithfully restores it
To the right path again.

# 3.
The little lambs He shall carry
And shelter at His breast,
Bind up the wounded,
Bring comfort to the sorrowful.
In the good grazing land
His flock may lie down,
He richly blesses it
Now and eternally.

# 4.
Thus I sing from my heart:
The Lord is my Shepherd;
With joy I follow Him
The way that leads homeward.
Only grace, yea, pure goodness
Shall ever follow me,
And I shall in His dwelling
Dwell eternally.
