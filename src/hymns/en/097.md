# 1.
Father in the glory,
We behold Thy beloved Son,
Exalted at Thy right hand,
Richly rewarded for the earth’s scorn.

# 2.
On Thy throne, O God, our Father,
He who as man is seated crowned,
He who bore the crown of thorns
And here endured reproach.

# 3.
Him the angels admire,
He who fulfilled Thy will here,
Every tongue shall confess:
Jesus is Lord of all.

# 4.
God and Father, our hearts
Bring Thee the sacrifice of praise
For Thy Son, whom Thou hast given.
Praise be to Thee eternally!
