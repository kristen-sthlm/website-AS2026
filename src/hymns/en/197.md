# 1.
As pilgrims and strangers
We now walk here;
Our home is not on earth,
Nay, above the skies it is.
There above dwells our Father
In glory and light,
There we also shall be
In our Father’s house.

# 2.
There above reigns Jesus,
Our Saviour so gracious,
Who went into death for us
And took upon Himself our guilt.
Now sin shall not rule,
For it is condemned indeed,
It may still grieve us here,
But not at home there.

# 3.
And soon we shall rest
With God eternally.
Our Lord Jesus comes
And takes us to Himself.
Then we shall blessedly behold
What here we believed,
And to the glory of our Lord
Strike the golden harp.
