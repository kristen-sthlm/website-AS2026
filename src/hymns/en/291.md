# 1.
Our Lord, though afar, yet near,
Though unseen, we worship Thee.
Thy name above all names is
Our refuge on the pilgrim’s way.

# 2.
Come, Lord, and fetch us home,
O, leave us here no longer,
Let the morning’s light break in,
The trumpet proclaim Thee nigh!

# 3.
Above among the number of saints
No fear, no wavering faith,
Naught from the vale of tears
Shall disturb our blissful rest.

# 4.
And should we yet remember it,
The memory shall not distress our soul,
But only increase our praise to our Friend,
To Him who hath done all things well.

# 5.
Yet while we still walk here,
All darkness in light is dissolved,
If only we see the Unseen One,
Who Himself is our life and our hope.
