# 1.
God in grace the His own chose
And from "the distant land" took.
He to us gave life and salvation,
When He us to the Son drew.

# 2.
The Father’s counsel soon shall be fulfilled,
Sonship He already has bestowed,
All He has said shall be wholly accomplished,
All shall appear as He has purposed.

# 3.
We admire here His love,
Which so high and deep is shown.
Christ is God’s power and wisdom,
Life in abundance He gives.

# 4.
Lord Jesus, soon Thou comest,
All Thy grace we shall behold,
Not in part alone, nay, its fullness.
Then shall we clearly understand.

# 5.
Thee we await, Lord Jesus,
Thou our true hope now art.
The creation in bondage sigheth,
Freedom soon Thou to it wilt impart.

# 6.
This world, where Thou hast walked,
Also we now pass through.
Fix our gaze on Thee more and more,
Till Thy blissful home we attain.
