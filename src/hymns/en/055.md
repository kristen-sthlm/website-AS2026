# 1.
Lord Jesus, Thee we behold
In Thy glory exalted,
And to Thee our heart’s praise
Ascends now with holy joy.

# 2.
Every trace of scorn and mocking,
Heaped upon Thy head here,
Every depth of sorrow and pain
Has been recompensed with glory there.

# 3.
On Thy cross Thou wast alone,
No comforter was near.
Exalted now in glory
Thou art acclaimed by heaven’s host.

# 4.
Though Thy God forsook Thee,
Hid Himself from Thy woe,
In Thy countenance once smitten
Now His glory we see.

# 5.
With admiration Thee we behold,
Lord Jesus, on Thy path.
Thou, Lamb of God, alone art worthy,
Earth and heaven acclaim Thee.
