# 1.
O Jesus, Thy name is a fortress in distress,
Our staff and our stay and our mountain-firm stronghold,
Thy name is the surest antidote against death,
Oh, knew but, oh, knew but all the world it!

# 2.
Thy name is a poured-out ointment that heals
And soothes and restores the wounded soul;
Thy name is our strength, a power that does not fail;
Thy name is our refuge in woe and in weal.

# 3.
Thy name is a rock in the hour of trial,
A tower which none can cast down,
A light that dispels the thickest of mists,
A spring that gives us living water.

# 4.
Thy name has proved also upon my heart
The life-power that lies hidden therein,
And stilled my unrest and eased my pain,
And made me free from the terror of death.

# 5.
Well may we the glorious name extol,
O Jesus, as long as we await Thee here,
And then for eternity with heart and tongue
Proclaim how great and how worthy it is.
