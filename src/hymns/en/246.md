# 1.
Secure we rest upon that Rock,
Where no foe us reaches,
Rest in that strong fortress,
Which in all storms stands.

# C.
Upon that lofty Rock we build
Our hope, our salvation;
It stands firm since former times,
It stands firm in eternity.

# 2.
Rise yet around the lofty Rock
The waves their wild threat,
Nothing there us frightens,
For they hush at its foot.

# 3.
O, what joy the lofty Rock
Can grant each moment!
O, what peace and power there flow
Abundantly from its depths!

# 4.
Only in that Rock, Christ,
Life and salvation we have;
Flee also all earth’s treasures,
He, our heart’s treasure, remains.

# 5.
The thought of that lofty Rock
Fills us with blessed hope;
Heart, soul, and spirit are lifted
To the blissful realms above.
