# 1.
Christ lives for us,
He who was crucified.
Though all may vanish, He remains.
To be one with Christ,
What a blessed portion!
We have received our all, yea all in Him.

# C.
Glory, praise, and honour, O Jesus Christ!
Thee may we praise first and last.
Here and in heaven our song shall be of Thee,
Of the love that gave itself.

# 2.
Jesus has loved us,
He loves us still,
For He is and ever shall be our Friend.
Never shall He weary
Of doing us good
And sanctifying us, both body and soul.

# 3.
This flame of love
Cannot be extinguished,
Which burned for us in the darkness of the cross.
Just as brightly it burns
Beyond death’s shore,
For our Jesus is in the land of life.
