# 1.
The wanderer is home,
In the world he wanders no more.
The lost son is dear to the Father
In Thee, God’s precious Lamb, God’s Lamb.

# 2.
My soul, now saved from sin and death,
Shall no more suffer hunger,
It is satisfied with God’s own bread
In Thee, O Thou God’s Lamb, God’s Lamb.

# 3.
No more separated from my Father,
Though I wasted His possession,
I am embraced by Him gently
In Thee, God’s precious Lamb, God’s Lamb.

# 4.
And the festal robe He brings forth,
He prepares a ring and shoes for me,
And teaches me the children’s song of joy
Of Thee, O Thou God’s Lamb, God’s Lamb.

# 5.
And now I stand in God’s favour,
But I cannot fully comprehend
The love I enjoy
In Thee, God’s precious Lamb, God’s Lamb.
