# 1.
Holy Father!
Gratefully we sing Thy praise,
Thou who hast prepared
For us a path in the wilderness.

# 2.
Thanks that Thou dost lead
Ever with Thy faithful hand,
And dost deliver us
From every hindering bond!

# 3.
Thou who didst speak
Once in fire and in judgment,
Now dost speak to us
Through Thy beloved Son.

# 4.
Sweetly Thou dost speak,
Speakest in grace, O our God;
Weakest hearts
Are strengthened by the sound of love.

# 5.
Of old on the stone
Thou didst write Thy holy law,
But now Thou hast written:
Grace is my heart’s delight.

# 6.
And in the minds
That here hearken to Thy voice,
Thy will’s ways
Thou yet dost desire to inscribe.

# 7.
May then our heart
Be a tablet so pure,
That with Thy Spirit
Thou mayest write more and more upon it.
