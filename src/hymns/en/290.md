# 1.
Thou, who in grace hast been able to call us friends,
Art in our midst, every heart Thou knowest.
Though we here from one another may be parted,
Thou Thyself art near us, art ever near us.

# 2.
Soon shall we hear the heavenly message,
The Bridegroom cometh to lead home the bride,
The marriage shall be celebrated, O joy without compare,
Jesus cometh soon, yea, He cometh soon!

# 3.
O, how swiftly our days vanish,
Soon shall we stand there, where tears shall not flow,
Sing with unburdened and jubilant tongue
The song of God and the Lamb, the song of God and the Lamb.

# 4.
Jesus, our Lord, lead us with Thine eye,
Till we Thee see in the high abodes!
Teach us to know Thee better on the way;
Know more Thy grace, all Thy counsel of love.
