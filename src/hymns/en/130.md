# 1.
Our God, we thank Thee for the word we have received,
Which declares that all Thou hast done is well.
Behold, the ransom is accepted, and all is now good,
And saved is every believing soul.

# 2.
So take us, our Shepherd, now Thyself by Thy hand,
And lead us and nurture us also.
Seek out and gather the sheep and the lambs,
Who still go scattered abroad!

# 3.
One day when Thou comest to fetch Thy bride
From this foreign land,
Then shall she stand forth in the snow-white robe,
Which she has received from her Saviour’s hand,

# 4.
And then in eternity sing Thy praise
With a tongue set free and unbound.
O, think what joy in heaven’s court,
Where it shall be blessed and sweet!

# 5.
The goal draws nearer with every minute,
And time hastens so swiftly;
Behold, the day of salvation shall one day end,
And heaven’s gate shall then be closed.

# 6.
For the blood, sinners may now come freely,
As many as will be with Thee.
O man, consider now, the decision is thine,
Follow on the living way!
