# 1.
O God, shall I be made like Thy Son,
Fruit of Thy grace, so unspeakably rich?
God of all grace, how deep Thy sea of love,
Thou, who didst give Thine only Son for us!

# 2.
Thou gavest Him, and He in love took
Our guilt upon Himself and patiently suffered and died.
Now satisfied, O God, Thou dost rejoice
In the Son, who for us hath sacrificed Himself.

# 3.
Thy thought great, O God, from eternity
Was to bring us to glory,
Yea, for Thy Son to have there a bride,
For Him adorned in glorious, snow-white raiment.

# 4.
In the Lamb’s bride Thou now, O Father, seest
The reward He gained for the cross’s anguish here;
For her sake His soul hath laboured
And found no rest until all was accomplished.

# 5.
We praise Thee, our Lord Jesus Christ!
Grace, nothing but grace we see from first to last,
Joint-heirs with Thee in glory,
Thy image to reflect in eternity.
