# 1.
I am a stranger, I am a pilgrim,
Only an evening, only an evening do I dwell here.
With feet shod, out of the bondage of slavery
I go to find the goodly land.
I am a stranger, I am a pilgrim,
Only an evening, only an evening do I dwell here.

# 2.
To that land my eye gazes;
Eternally shines, eternally shines the sun there.
There no one weeps, there want ceases,
There sin dwells not, thither death reaches not.
To God’s Canaan my eye gazes,
Eternally shines, eternally shines the sun there.

# 3.
The Spirit of grace sweetly comforts us
With a foretaste, with a foretaste of His rest.
Not the leaven of sin will we desire to taste;
Onward we hasten, look not back.
The Lamb’s supper we soon shall celebrate
In an eternal, in an eternal eternal rest.

# 4.
Gracious Shepherd, Thy grace we praise,
Follow and lead us, follow and lead us ever Thou!
That we may ever hasten to meet Thee,
Until we rest in Thy bosom.
Gracious Shepherd, Thy grace we praise,
Follow and lead us, follow and lead us ever Thou!
