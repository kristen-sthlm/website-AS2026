# 1.
The word of God’s love I hear,
That He looks upon the blood;
He looks upon the offering that has died,
And this gives me peace.

# 2.
It is an eternal peace,
As sure as His word,
As steadfast as His firm faith;
It was made at the cross.

# 3.
My love is small,
My joy has variation;
His peace knows no change,
It remains as it was.

# 4.
I often change,
But Jesus is ever the same;
His love is my place of rest,
His truth makes me rich.

# 5.
And He Himself is my peace,
My distress has reached its end;
For I am assured, it was for me
He died and has risen.

# 6.
At God’s right hand
He now sits exalted,
I rest in His faithfulness,
His love is my joy.
