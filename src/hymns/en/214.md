# 1.
Sing, with all the heart sing
And give praise to the Father!
His way becomes clear and manifest
To those who see Jesus.

# 2.
Through the heavens
Our great High Priest has gone.
In Him we, a company set free,
Have received a glorious standing.

# 3.
Though still upon the earth
Amid need and toils here,
We walk in peace to dwell with God,
Our home His presence is.

# 4.
And strengthened by our God
In the day’s labour and strife,
In the desert’s sand and barren land
We journey onward in peace.

# 5.
Our way ever upward bears
To life and salvation;
There upward we go to partake
In our Lord’s glory.

# 6.
Forever like the Lord,
Forever near to Him,
We shall clearly see His face
And be where He is.
