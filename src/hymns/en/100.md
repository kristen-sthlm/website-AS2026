# 1.
The sacrifice of thanksgiving we now bring to Thee,
O Thou our highly praised Father and God!
For all that Thou hast given us in the Son,
We would praise Thee with the sound of the song of praise.

# 2.
Though He was the Son, yet He learned obedience
Through the anguish He suffered for us here,
And through His blood entered into Thy sanctuary
To now present Himself there for our good.

# 3.
O Christ, our High Priest, Thee we praise,
The great Apostle of confession Thou art,
Thou who before the hostile powers of the earth
Made the good confession here.

# 4.
So high, yea far higher than heaven Thou hast become,
Well may we exalt Thy holy name,
Thou who through death led us to the Father
And won for us unspeakable salvation.

# 5.
Our Lord, who can fully declare and comprehend
What Thou as High Priest dost for us,
How mighty, how gracious the service Thou performest,
When Thou dost bring the soul into God’s sanctuary.
