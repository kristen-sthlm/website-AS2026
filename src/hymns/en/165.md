# 1.
Blessed assurance, Jesus is mine!
He is my shepherd, He calls me His own.
Blessed assurance, all is well now!
Jesus has redeemed my soul for eternity.

# C.
|: He is my joy, He is my song,
I praise Him all the day long. :|

# 2.
I gave up all, and I gained all,
The day of eternity has dawned, glorious.
The Spirit teaches me God's eternal plan,
With whispers of love, with whispers of grace.

# 3.
Blessed assurance in the strife of life,
In Jesus I have joy and peace.
Awaiting Him, I lift my eyes to heaven,
Even now He gives me the fullness of His love.
