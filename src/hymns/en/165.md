# 1.
Blessed assurance, Jesus is mine!
He is my Shepherd, calls me His own.
Blessed assurance, all is now well!
Jesus has redeemed my body and my soul.

# C.
|: He is my joy, He is my song,
Him I praise all the day long. :|

# 2.
All I surrendered, all I have gained,
The eternal day has gloriously dawned.
Heaven is open, angels from God
Bring to the earth the message of salvation.

# 3.
Now I can blessedly sing His praise,
My soul now enjoys heaven’s food.
I am but dust, yet cleansed by God,
Jesus is mine, and I am His bride.

# 4.
Baptized with God’s Spirit according to His word,
Blessed assurance, heaven on earth!
Fire on my tongue, fire in my breast,
He has filled me with love so warm.
