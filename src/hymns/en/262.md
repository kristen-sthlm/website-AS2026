# 1.
Art thou in distress,
Hast thou no support,
Dost thou feel forsaken,
The Lord is rich,
Ever the same,
Never is His door shut.

# 2.
Rich in grace,
He knows a way,
Tell Him what ails thee;
Great or small,
Only tell Him,
He will share His help with thee.

# 3.
Though many a friend
May yet fail thee,
The Lord is the sure Rock.
Faithful He is,
Holds thee dear,
On Him thou canst build.

# 4.
Help He has,
Ready and near
For thee in the right time.
The day of trial,
With God’s good pleasure,
Shall shortly pass away.

# 5.
Only take courage,
The Lord is good,
He acts in love.
The bread of tears,
The hardest distress,
He turns into joy.

# 6.
Jesus, Thy name
The safest haven
Is in all changes;
Comfort in distress,
Life in death,
The light even at the grave.
