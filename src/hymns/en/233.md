# 1.
O precious Lord Jesus! On the desert path here,
Teach us to better comprehend how great Thy love is.
What more, if Thou for us through trial severe,
Only our hearts know, we walk in Thy footsteps,

# 2.
Only if thereby we perceive Thy love rich and warm,
Understand how Thou dost lead us with gracious and mighty arm.
The way we here are given to walk, that way Thou hast gone before,
And now Thou goest before us still unto the journey’s end.

# 3.
Ah, teach us to better know Thee Thyself, our great Shepherd,
And draw thus all our longing to the home where Thou dost dwell!
Teach us in the storm to hear Thy sweet, gentle voice,
Which bids us to rest wholly secure at Thy breast.

# 4.
For when we have grasped somewhat, that all which reaches us here,
Is intended for our nurture by our loving Father,
Then is all our unrest stilled, and content in Thy embrace,
We thank Thee, who teachest us to know the Father’s name.
