# 1.
A fair and glorious morning we await,
A joyful morning with the Saviour there at home,
When we, glorified, His wounds shall see
And hear the sound of His beloved voice.

# 2.
Eternal dwellings are opened there,
Which He has prepared for His redeemed hosts,
There we shall see our Lord as He is
And give thanks for the perils we have passed through.

# 3.
Yet even here He walks with His own,
A mighty pillar of fire before us in the night,
And the Spirit’s sword, His precious word, endures,
And the ark of promise passes over deep waters.

# 4.
Soon we shall sing around the Lamb’s throne,
Where there is no end to joy, the Sabbath rest.
Even now, when the heart hears a homeland tone,
With uplifted courage we press onward in the conflict.

# 5.
Wait for the Lord, the time is so short;
Though the journey be stormy, soon we shall reach the haven.
Behold, the morning dawns and the night withdraws,
An eternal rest the Lord Jesus has won for us.
