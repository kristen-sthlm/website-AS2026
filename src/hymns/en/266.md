# 1.
As God’s Israel in days of old,
May we be led solely by the Lord’s word!
The Spirit still, and the word, guides us,
And His arm has wrought great wonders.
Let us encamp wherever He commands,
Yet also willingly set forth again,
If His voice to break camp once more resounds
Through mist or storms anew.

# 2.
Never was the morrow’s care laid
Upon the day that was—it rested on God;
For they hearkened to what from the Lord was spoken,
Tarried or journeyed at His bidding.
And whether the cloud arose by night
Or by day, yet onward it bore them
Through wilderness or deep waters,
Where no path, no track was seen.

# 3.
The Lord led them, He knew the way,
And each day’s need, and never yet
Has the King of kings been at a loss,
Or shut again the door of His goodness.
The Lord led them, and they depended
On their God from day to day in all;
Himself in the tent with Israel He dwelt,
In His care they had committed themselves.

# 4.
Let us ever cast every burden
Upon our God in faith upon His name,
And hasten onward in Jesus’ footsteps,
Till we reach the rest in heaven’s haven;
For whatever each day may bring us,
It is our Father’s good will.
May His praise alone resound more among us,
And may He Himself become ever dearer to us!
