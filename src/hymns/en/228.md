# 1.
Though our path be hard and thorny,
And Thou seemest to hide Thyself,
Yet we press onward in faith,
And thus find a blessed rest.

# 2.
All power in heaven and on earth
Is given Thee, as Thy word teaches;
And all things here serve for good
To those who love the Lord.

# 3.
A peace which surpasses knowledge,
For around Thy throne, O God, it reigns,
Sinks down into our heart,
When our desire we have made known to Thee.
