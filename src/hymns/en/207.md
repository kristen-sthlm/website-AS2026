# 1.
O Thou, who hast said: No one can
Take Mine from Me,
Thee against all things we cry unto,
That would draw us from Thee.
Lead us on the path of life with those,
Whom to Thy Father’s blessed home
Thou hast promised to conduct.

# 2.
Teach us now to know Thy voice
And go where Thou wilt call.
The "little flock" Thou lovest,
Thou hast numbered all the sheep.
Thou seekest him that goeth astray,
The weak one Thou takest in Thine arms,
Upholdest those who fall.
