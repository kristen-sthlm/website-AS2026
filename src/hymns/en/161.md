# 1.
A straying sheep,
I wandered about,
And of the good Shepherd’s voice
I knew nothing.
My own way I went
Far from my Father’s house,
I knew not His great love,
Nor the home’s peace and light.

# 2.
But the Shepherd sought wholly
His straying sheep,
And the Father followed faithfully, tenderly
The straying one’s path.
And behold, His sheep He found
Upon the path of ruin,
Bound up my wounds, and sweetly
By name He called me.

# 3.
That Shepherd is Jesus,
He has saved my soul,
He has cleansed me in His blood
And made all things well.
He sought me so wholly,
He sought until He found,
And bore me home to Himself with joy;
All was done by Him.

# 4.
A straying sheep,
Who is found again,
I now know my Shepherd’s voice
And willingly follow it.
Lost son, who went
From his Father’s house,
I am now found by Him
And rejoice in His light.
