# 1.
O, God’s wondrous love,
Holy, boundless, tender, and free!
All my anxiety has vanished,
For I know, He loves me.
Love, source of all bliss,
Grace so rich, immeasurable,
Willingly gave the holy Lamb
To win us for Himself.
|: God’s wondrous love, :|
God’s wondrous love was manifested at the cross.

# 2.
O, the wondrous cross,
Where I see God’s counsel of love;
For in Jesus’ death are declared
His righteousness and grace.
The cross shows God is holy,
When He sacrificed His Son.
Light and love there proclaim,
How God can say: “Come!”
|: My sin He there atoned, :|
My sin He there atoned, when He died on the cross’s tree.

# 3.
Now I gaze up to heaven
And behold Him there.
The beautiful crown of glory
He now bears upon His brow.
He who suffered for us, righteous,
For the unrighteous,
Is exalted at the Father’s side;
Praise and glory we give Him.
|: O Jesus, Thou art worthy, :|
O Jesus, Thou art worthy, Thou, who once died for me!

# 4.
There is rest for weary souls,
If only they look upon Him there,
Eternal peace to continually rest
In God’s Son, who is exalted.
We may rest securely in Him,
As we pass through the world.
Heavenly peace and joy and love
We in Christ may enjoy.
|: Praise, glory, oh give to Him, :|
Praise, glory, oh give to Him, now and for all eternity.
