# 1.
Soul and tongue may praise
God, our Father’s, holy name,
He His only Son to us sent
As a spotless sacrificial Lamb.
He from the grave, according to Scripture,
Now in glory stands forth.

# 2.
He to us has become the light, the life;
He is our righteousness.
He His Spirit to us sent,
And brings us to glory.
Christ has won, that we have found
Sons’ place in eternity.

# 3.
We are redeemed, the serpent is crushed,
The enemy’s and death’s power
Is bound from that moment,
When all is subjected to Thee.
Christ the life for us has given
And God’s grace in the light brought.

# 4.
Gladly our tongue may praise
The Son’s power and glory
And bring home to the Father glory
For so great mercy.
God we praise for His gift
Now and in all eternity.
