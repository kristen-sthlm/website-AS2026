# 1.
O come, Lord Jesus, we await
To soon go to Thy meeting!
Hear, the Spirit and the bride they cry,
That Thou, Lord, must come.
Thy love for the bride we know,
Thy love unutterable.
With all our heart we long:
O, were Thy bride with Thee!

# 2.
And in Thine own gathering
Now is heard ever more clearly,
How the Spirit and the bride still cry
And say: “Come, Lord, soon!”
Behold, darkness now covers the earth,
And night is over the world,
Yet hast Thou still here Thine own,
The flock which Thou holdest dear.

# 3.
And in the midst of the darkness we discern
The dawning of the coming day,
We see Thee as the morning star,
Which heralds a new morn.
Thy promise, that Thou shalt soon come,
Is light upon the path here,
And draws with power our hearts
To heaven, where Thou Thyself art.

# 4.
O, grant us to hear the trumpet,
Already now to understand its sound,
Before we see Thee upon the clouds
As the Bridegroom for Thy bride.
Thy word and Thy Spirit, Thy love,
Our pledge of Thy coming are.
Thy mighty hand preserves us,
For faith Thou art ever near.

# 5.
How sweet is Thy word: “Behold, I come!”
Thou sayest: “I come soon!”
Thy bride, who hath long awaited Thee,
Perceives Thy voice clearly.
With the Spirit Thou hast sealed us,
Thou Thyself art our blessed portion.
Thou never shalt lose any one
Of those Thou hast received from the Father.
