# 1.
Image of God’s being, and yet
A Son of Man, that every soul
To God might return,
Thou, Jesus, camest with peace and hope,
The lost to seek out
And save the wretched;
Hast fought, hast suffered death’s pain.
Now our heart has found peace
And an opened heaven gained.

# 2.
Thou didst shed tears as we,
Our weakness seest and standest by us
With the power of Thy Spirit,
Declarest to us Thy Father’s counsel
And the sweetness of Thy rich grace
In the cup of sorrow blendest;
Love, faithfulness to us showest,
Sorrow easest. Praise and honour,
Lord, we now to Thee bring home.
