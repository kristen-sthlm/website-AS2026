# 1.
Thou art the Son of God,
The only-begotten of Thy Father,
The Man of God’s counsel of love,
His soul’s chosen One,
Thou art the Son of God.

# 2.
Thou art our peace,
For peace Thou hast established
With the blood of Thy cross,
Jesus, our Mediator,
Thou art our peace.

# 3.
Thou art our life,
With Thee hid,
We await now Thy day,
Thy manifestation.
Thou art our life.

# 4.
Thou art our hope,
The long-expected One.
Come and soon deliver
Thy whole creation!
Thou art our hope.

# 5.
Thou art our sun.
The glow of Thy mighty love
Disperses all clouds
In both life and death.
Thou art our sun.

# 6.
Thou art our joy,
All heaven’s joy Thou art,
And the Father’s good pleasure
Ever rests upon Thee there.
Thou art our joy.
