# 1.
O Lord, to be with Thee,
Forever like Thee,
So near Thyself, when Thou comest again!
Then we shall live wholly in Thy grace
And behold Thee may,
For the journey is ended, when Thou comest again.

# 2.
O Lord, Thy countenance to see,
Once marked by woe,
What joy it shall be, when Thou comest again!
Then, Lord, we shall always Thee see,
Our heart worships,
All sorrow shall end, when Thou comest again.

# 3.
O Lord, exalted on Thy throne,
Thou sittest, Son of God,
With Thee is Thy bride, when Thou comest again.
But more than all glory there,
Thy nearness to us is,
For, Lord, Thou Thyself hast then come again.
