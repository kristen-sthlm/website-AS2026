# 1.
A little time! Then He shall come,
Who is to come, and take us home
To the Father’s mansions, where He
Has prepared an eternal place for those
Who are His; then we shall see
And stand before His face.

# 2.
A little time! How short it is!
May we then diligently use it
And serve Him faithfully here,
Who has loved and still loves.
Our joy, when He beholds His joy,
Our sorrow to grieve Him.

# 3.
A little time! How swiftly it passes!
Oh, may we take up the cross here
And willingly follow in His steps,
All else is only harm,
And the suffering in this time
Shall soon be exchanged for joy and peace.

# 4.
A little time! O Lord, come
And take to Thyself Thy beloved bride
To sing there an eternal song
In unblemished, snow-white robe,
To render Thee full worship
And ever behold Thy face.
