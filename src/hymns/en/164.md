# 1.
O depth of mercy, Lord, with Thee,
That blood on Golgotha hath cleansed even me!
No sin and condemnation reach me more,
Complete before God in Christ I stand.

# C.
O, sing of His great grace,
|: Sing of His great grace, :|
Sing of His grace!

# 2.
O depth of mercy, He hath love for me!
From judgment’s terror ransomed I am free,
And certain of my salvation I can praise
His grace, which in the wilderness sought and found me.

# 3.
O depth of mercy, wonder of grace!
For all He hath healing, ever knows the way;
Each bleeding heart-sorrow He grants comfort,
And tears are dried close by His breast.

# 4.
O Jesus, my Saviour, Lord and God,
For Thee I will raise the sound of praise,
And though through death’s vale I must go,
I will yet rejoice in Thy great grace.
