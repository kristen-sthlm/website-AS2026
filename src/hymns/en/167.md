# 1.
Rock, Thou who wast cleft for me,
Let me hide myself in Thee.
Water, blood, which forth do flow
From Thy heart, O Lamb of God,
Be to me a double cure
Against sin and law’s dread threat.

# 2.
With my pious toil I ne’er
Can fulfil Thy law’s demand.
Were I in zeal not weary,
Wept I both by night and day,
Still the stains of sin remain.
Only in Thee have I salvation.

# 3.
Naught can I give Thee.
To Thy cross I cleave.
Naked, Thee for clothing I entreat,
Helpless, to grace I look up.
Let me flee to the fountain now,
Saviour, O cleanse Thou me!

# 4.
At each fleeting breath I draw,
And when I shall die one day,
When to an unknown land I go,
When before Thy judgment I stand,
Rock, Thou who wast cleft for me,
Let me hide myself in Thee.
