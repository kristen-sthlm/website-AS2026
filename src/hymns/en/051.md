# 1.
He is risen. What light, what joy!
O death, where is thy victory?
He is ascended to heaven’s height;
All power and glory He possesses.
He, who took upon Himself our sin and need,
He, who on the cross for us became dead,
He is risen, He lives!

# 2.
“He is risen, He is not here,”
The message the angel brings.
In glory He is exalted;
May we on the wings of worship
Praise-sing God, who to us the victory gave,
When the stone was removed from Christ’s grave
And He was raised to life.

# 3.
He is risen! That “grain of wheat,”
Which was laid down into the earth,
He bore much fruit unto His God
And the first-fruit sheaf has come.
No more “alone”! In eternity
His joy, His power, and His glory
With His redeemed He shares.
