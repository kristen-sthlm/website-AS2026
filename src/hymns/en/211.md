# 1.
My Shepherd is the Lamb,
The living One who died;
All good He richly grants to me,
That I have sufficiency.
My heart He satisfies
In pastures green, beside
The fresh waters, where I may
Enjoy repose.

# 2.
Should I go on a wayward path,
He gently restores me,
To the right way, and teaches me
What I have forgotten.
In the dark valley of the world,
He is ever near to me,
With rod and staff, with words of comfort,
He cheers me there.

# 3.
Now before the enemy,
A table He prepares,
And with the holy oil He
Anoints me,
And filled with bliss,
A cup He gives;
His goodness and mercy
Each day I see.

# 4.
When faith and hope shall pass away,
But love remains,
I shall behold Himself,
Whom I have loved,
And dwell in the Lord’s house,
All eternity content,
And behold Him as He is,
In eternal joy.
