# 1.
For Israel Thou didst formerly
Thyself call: "I am,"
And still Thou art the same,
But also our beloved Father.
Thy holy name,
Thy open arms,
Thy faithful, tender love
Our refuge now is,
Jehovah, who didst call Thyself
In former time: "I am."

# 2.
Our Father of Jesus Christ,
Our God from eternity,
Thou for us through the wilderness
With grace and faithfulness.
With tenderness sees
Thine eye below
And shields us from dangers,
Of which we know nothing.
So lovingly Thou leadest us
Home to Thy glory.
