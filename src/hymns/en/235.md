# 1.
Jesus, Thou my shelter
And my heart’s delight,
At Thy faithful breast
I have eternal comfort.

# 2.
All that burdens my soul
Upon Thee I cast,
Who upon the way here
Bearest all my burden.

# 3.
Toil, want, and tears,
All that wounds the heart,
Sin and sorrow and anguish
Here in the valley of death.

# 4.
Shall not separate me,
For such is Thy will,
Lord, from Thee,
Because Thou lovest me.

# 5.
That Thou mightest eternally win me
Thou didst let Thy blood flow,
Didst die for me, Lamb of God,
Upon the tree of the cross.

# 6.
Eternal life Thou givest,
Thine I am and remain;
Nothing shall separate me,
Lord, now from Thee.
