# 1.
How deep the peace which Thou, O Jesus, givest,
The peace which sweetly stills each conflict,
Which causes the desert path to become level
And the time of estrangement to be but "a little while."

# 2.
A little while to struggle at Thy side,
During which Thou dost whisper: "thou art Mine;"
A little while to sow Thy seed and await
The day when the sheaves shall be gathered in.

# 3.
A little while, with the deep longing of hope,
To drink here in faith from the river of life,
And then in joy, surpassing all desire,
An eternal fullness beside the fountain itself.

# 4.
A little while to keep the lamp ready,
A little while to wait for Thee yet,
And then an eternal jubilation: "Thou art worthy
To have power and glory, Thou alone!"
