# 1.
Come! saith the Spirit, calling,
Come! saith the Bride in one accord.
Lord Jesus, Thou shalt come,
Clothed in glory’s splendour, Lord.
All creation now doth groan,
Waiting for Thee, Thou our God.
Lord Jesus, come, we pray,
Hallelujah! Come, O Lord!
