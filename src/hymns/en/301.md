# 1.
Soon the morning star shall rise,
Then all our hope shall be fulfilled
At the Lord’s sweet meeting;
When the bride, raised above the earth,
Shall be brought in holy joy
Up into her Bridegroom’s bosom.
The time, the conflict soon shall end,
Peace be enjoyed,
The crown be won,
The goal of bliss be attained.

# 2.
No more with dim and feeble gaze
Shall we behold the features of God’s countenance
In the mirror of the word portrayed;
But the Lord’s unclouded glory see,
See Jesus’ sweet face
With glory radiant,
Blessedly, blessedly shall we experience
All the fair,
True, exalted,
That here was glimpsed by our eye.

# 3.
O blissful day, Thee we await;
We stand ready, already clothed in
The white festival garment.
Come, Jesus! “Yea, I come quickly,”
Thou dost answer us; we hear clearly
Thy voice in the morning watch.
Amen! Amen! Thou shalt come;
Thy godly ones
Shall greatly rejoice
And with thanksgiving and praise come forth.
