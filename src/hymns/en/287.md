# 1.
How sweet many a time
That thought stands before us:
Today we are nearer
Our homeland than yesterday.

# C.
Nearer home, nearer yes,
Nearer home today, today,
Than we have been before.

# 2.
Yes, nearer the strand of peace,
Where the sword is laid down,
And the cross is exchanged for the crown,
Which God gives to His own.

# 3.
Ah, nearer today
Our Father’s blessed house,
With all the dwellings there,
Filled with God’s light.

# 4.
Yes, nearer that moment,
When we shall see our God,
When the Bridegroom shall fetch home
His redeemed bride.
