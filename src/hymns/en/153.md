# 1.
Venture boldly,
Believe that Jesus has done all for thee!
Thus was God’s counsel of salvation eternally,
Man is saved by grace alone,
Jesus has died for all,
Jesus has died for all.

# 2.
Venture boldly,
Believe that Jesus has done all for thee!
The victory for thee He has won,
Crushed the serpent, who was mighty,
Opened for us the gate of heaven,
Opened for us the gate of heaven.

# 3.
Venture boldly,
Believe that Jesus has done all for thee!
Thou hast nothing, only sin and shame,
Praise only God’s precious Lamb,
Thou mayest now look upon God’s Lamb,
Thou mayest now look upon God’s Lamb.

# 4.
Venture boldly,
Believe that Jesus has done all for thee!
Oh, what joy and what power has he,
Who has received all in the Saviour,
Blessed and holy he is,
Blessed and holy he is.

# 5.
The hour strikes, time passes,
Soon thy soul before the Lamb shall stand.
If in the blood thou hast received cleansing,
Fair, heavenly shall be thy lot:
Christ in heaven’s height,
Christ in heaven’s height.
