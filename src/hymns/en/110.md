# 1.
As the hart longeth after the water brooks,
So longeth the soul after God,
Who has heard His divine voice,
The almighty sound of His love.
Though thou couldst measure the depths of the sea,
And number the small raindrops,
God’s eternal love, so boundless,
Thou couldst not measure still.

# 2.
God’s love is now manifested,
In Jesus, an offering for sin.
He knocks yet upon thy heart,
And pleads: O soul, let Me in!
God’s righteous judgment I have borne,
Upon the cross My life I gave.
God will forgive thy sins,
And sink them in the sea of love!

# 3.
Ah, see Jesus’ hands and feet,
The wounds which thy sin therein smote;
There are seen the paths so heavy,
Which He in His love then took.
If thou castest a splinter into the fire,
Thou seest no trace thereof more,
So the sinner also disappears,
Disappears in the Saviour’s grave.
