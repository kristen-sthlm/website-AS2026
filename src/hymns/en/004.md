# 1.
How great Thou art, when I behold the world
Which Thou hast created by Thy word of almighty power,
How Thy wisdom directs the threads of life,
And all beings are satisfied at Thy table;

# C.
|: Then bursts the soul forth in songs of praise:
How great Thou art! How great Thou art! :|

# 2.
When I consider the wonders of heaven on high,
Where golden worlds plough the blue ether,
And sun and moon measure the moments of time,
And alternate, like two clocks going;

# 3.
When I in the Bible behold all the wonders,
Which the Lord has wrought since the time of the first Adam,
How full of grace He has been at all times,
And helped His people out of the sin and strife of life;

# 4.
When, pressed down by the guilt of sin, I fall down
At the Lord’s foot and pray for grace and peace,
And He guides my soul in the right way,
And saves me from all my sin and strife;

# 5.
When at last all the veils of time shall fall,
And my faith is changed into sight,
And the clear bells of eternity call
My redeemed spirit to its sabbath rest,
|: Then the soul breaks forth in sounds of praise:
Thanks, good God! Thanks, good God! :|
