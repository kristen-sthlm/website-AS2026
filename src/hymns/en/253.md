# 1.
Jesus, our Saviour,
Sweet is Thy name;
Firm is our anchor,
Secure our haven.
Now the light of life has risen,
Thou art our great portion,
Rest our heart has found
Within Thy bosom.

# 2.
Thou wast the Son of Man,
Yet the Son of God.
Thy life Thou hast given,
Jesus, Son of God.
None on earth here
Exists whose love is
Like that which Thou bearest to us,
Jesus, Son of God!

# 3.
Delivered by Thee
From bonds of slavery,
We walk in Thy path
In the wilderness.
What is there to fear here?
No toil, sorrow, or trouble;
For Thou art ever near
With mighty hand.

# 4.
Soon Thou comest again,
Blessed hope!
Takest us to Thy home
Among the clouds above.
There is only joy and peace,
There we, who on Thee believe,
Eternally with Thee shall dwell,
Jesus, Son of God!
