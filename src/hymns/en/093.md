# 1.
Praise be to Thee, Lord and King of all kings!
To Thee our souls with reverence in faith now call.
Praise be to Thy name,
Praise for Thy opened arms!
High may our song of praise now resound.

# 2.
Thou hast in Christ, O God, in Thy grace received us,
Us hast Thou by the Spirit and the word so mightily drawn,
And from our guilt
Hast Thou, merciful and gracious,
Washed us white as snow.

# 3.
Father, teach us from the heart to praise and honour Thee,
Through Jesus to bring Thee thankful offerings of praise!
Praise be to Thy counsel,
Praise be to Thy glorious grace,
Praise be to Thee far and near!
