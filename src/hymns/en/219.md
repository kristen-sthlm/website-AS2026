# 1.
Bring all doubt to Jesus,
Take His promise as it stands.
Never shall he come to shame
Who in faith to Him goes.

# C.
|: I trust, fully trust,
Yes, I rely on His word. :|

# 2.
Bring all your sin to Jesus,
He will clothe you in festal robe.
In His word He will cleanse you
And bring you home to God.

# 3.
Lay all care on Jesus,
He gives rest to the weary.
Though your path goes through dark valleys,
The sun of grace never sets.

# 4.
Bring all your joy to Jesus,
He gives life a new form.
He is Lord of all worlds,
With Him you find all.

# 5.
Give yourself, your all to Jesus,
Both spirit, soul, and body,
What you have and what you gain,
All your longing and your hope.
I trust,
etc.
