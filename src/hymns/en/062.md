# 1.
O my soul! And now let Jesus
Fill thy gaze entirely;
See, He is in glory,
Where thy sin can never reach.

# 2.
See in the highest glory,
Seated there for us He appears,
The blood that was given for atonement,
Is thy praise to be there.

# 3.
All thy sins He bore,
Bore them on the cross’s tree;
God laid the burden on Him,
Through faith thou art now free.

# 4.
God now for thee to His dwelling,
Spreads a richly laden table,
Bids thee come, and tells thee,
All thou there from Him shalt receive.

# 5.
There surrounded by His favour,
The Father’s love’s wide embrace!
Blessedness to rest undisturbed
Eternally in His safe haven.

# 6.
What a blessed word “for ever”!
Yea, for ever there above!
Nought can the redeemed
Sever from their Saviour.
