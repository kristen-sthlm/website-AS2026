# 1.
Sing to the God of our strength,
Who hitherto hath helped us!
He at last will bring us home
In His grace so wondrous.
Trust His word, which never faileth,
Fear not, His power never faileth.

# 2.
Sing to the God of our strength,
Sing of His love so great!
Who comprehendeth its length and breadth,
Who in heaven and on earth
Can its depth and height conceive,
Who its riches can esteem?

# 3.
Sing to the God of our strength,
He ever with us goeth!
We fear not the journey’s length
Nor him who against us standeth.
Soon shall the enemy vanish,
And the goal of our journey we reach.
