# 1.
Down into a death of nameless woe
Thy love could go,
To save us from the judgment,
To cast down the strong one.

# 2.
Our guilt, yea all upon Thee, Lamb of God,
In this hour was laid;
Thou didst bear the weight of our sins,
God’s judgment and Satan’s power.

# 3.
The storm that passed over Thee here
Is forever stilled,
And we may enjoy eternal peace,
Thou bearest the crown of victory.

# 4.
Thy praise we may sing eternally
In the Father’s house one day;
On earth, where Thou didst suffer and die,
We already raise our song.
