# 1.
With Jesus’ blood all our sin is washed away,
And the judgment we deserved is removed from us;
Jesus’ blood has delivered us from sin’s distress,
Yea, He has saved us from an eternal death.

# 2.
With Jesus’ blood the handwriting is blotted out,
With Jesus’ blood the way to heaven is opened,
By Jesus’ blood, which flowed from His wounds,
We receive life and peace and salvation.

# 3.
By pure grace we are borne home to heaven,
For the blood’s sake we are eternally blessed,
And the Lamb Himself is the theme of the song,
Which we one day shall sing before the throne.
