# 1.
Just as I am, without a straw
Of my own ground to build upon,
Only on that ground, that Thou for me
Hast died and Thyself didst invite us to Thee.

# 2.
Just as I am, ere I have
Accomplished what eternally was vain—
To cleanse myself from one spot—
To Thee, whose blood alone doth this.

# 3.
Just as I am in this moment,
So poor, cold, and evil in nature,
To find all that I lack,
In blessed abundance with Thee.

# 4.
Just as I am, Thou dost rejoice to see
Me come and give me all,
Forgive, cleanse, heal me;
I believe Thy word, and safe in Thee.

# 5.
Just as I am, Thou hast loved me,
For Thou hast bought me, as I am;
Thou didst well know how evil I was,
When Thou didst bear my sins here.

# 6.
Just as I am, yet now I believe
And would praise Thy great love,
Whose breadth and length and depth and height
I shall sing in eternal joy.
