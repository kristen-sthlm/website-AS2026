# 1.
Say, was it the Righteous One,
God’s own beloved Son,
Who, that He might show us God’s grace,
Came down from heaven’s throne?

# 2.
Yes, Jesus came and for our sake
In death gave Himself up.
We shall not now come into judgment,
Our judgment He took upon Himself.

# 3.
Our sin He bore, our guilt He took,
Such was God’s counsel of love.
Our punishment He suffered, our death He died,
O, wonder of grace!

# 4.
O, teach us better to thank Thee,
More praise and worship to give
For all the riches of that love
We see in the word.
