# 1.
Father, Thou hast in Thy love
Called us sons.
Thou hast loved us ere the world
Was founded.

# 2.
The Son hath come, He who is called
“Wonderful” and “Counsellor;”
Of His fulness Thou hast given us
“Grace upon grace.”

# 3.
Freely Thy great grace now reigneth
In righteousness,
And we possess a perfect
Salvation.

# 4.
May we then by the Spirit be led
Here unto Thy pleasure!
Short is the time, soon breaketh in
“The day of Christ.”

# 5.
The sons then at home shall be,
At home each one,
Thee to everlasting praise, O Father,
In Thy home.
