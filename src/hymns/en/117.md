# 1.
Love, vast as the ocean,
Grace and goodness like a river!
Jesus, to reconcile us,
Freely gave His precious blood.
For His death, that wondrous one,
Sing His praise with heart’s joy!
The song of divine love
Fills all heaven’s height.

# 2.
The cross speaks of God’s love
And of His righteousness,
Love, which is offered to all,
Full and free mercy.
Each one who receives it,
Obtains the Father’s kiss and favour,
Grace and truth, peace and bliss
From God’s heart continually flow.
