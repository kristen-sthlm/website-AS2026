# 1.
Lord Jesus, Thou hast won us
In the depth and height of Thy love.
God in Thee has found His rest,
Thou art all the joy of heaven.

# 2.
Around Thee, O Lord Jesus,
Now Thou gatherest Thine assembly,
To bring it into the Father
In the joy of Thy heart.

# 3.
Lord Jesus, as brethren
We may share Thy joy,
And in liberty before the Father
We stand in Thy fragrance.
