# 1.
A holy Father in His care here
Does not weary to watch with vigilant eye,
If now His children bear the fair fruits
That befit their high calling.

# 2.
He ever takes heed of our state and knows
If aught may dim His love’s joy,
Sees all that hinders or separates us
From what is our portion in heaven’s height.

# 3.
What great love, that cannot forget them,
Who are the objects of His tender care,
And if He chastens here in the land of sin,
It is but for warning and their protection.

# 4.
He forgets not, but ever nourishes them
With many tokens of His great love,
Preserves and delivers them from evil,
Till they dwell with Him in His heaven.
