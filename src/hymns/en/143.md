# 1.
Yet in grace the Lord delays to come
To fetch unto Himself His gathering of the godly.
Is it upon thee He waits, thou weary soul?
He will save thee,
He will save thee.

# 2.
Oh, how He longs to enfold thee
And pour out His love into thy heart,
Longs to tenderly bear thee home in His arms
To His Father’s house,
To His Father’s house.

# 3.
Let Him open thine ear to hear
How He longs to make thee blessed,
To help and shield thee from all that can harm thee
In the evil world,
In the evil world.

# 4.
He has gone away to prepare a place
For those who here let themselves be led by His Spirit,
And who now have received cleansing from sins
In His precious blood,
In His precious blood.

# 5.
Abroad in the world there is only death and danger,
Come then to Him who can keep thee!
Lay hold of the hand that so surely can lead thee—
Death shall not reach thee! Death shall not reach thee!
