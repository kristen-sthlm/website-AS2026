# 1.
O Jesus, Thou Lamb of God,
In Thee our salvation is,
For Thou didst drink the bitter cup of death
Upon the cross here.

# 2.
Merciful priest of God,
Our Saviour and Friend,
To the journey’s end Thou shalt follow us
With Thy love still.

# 3.
Our soul Thou shalt feed,
Lead us, give us strength,
Until at last in Thy glory
We may see Thee.
