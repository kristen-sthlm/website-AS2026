# 1.
What comfort it gives each soul,
How sweet, that the heart knows:
For those who believe all is well
In time and eternity.

# 2.
In all things they stand secure,
Whatever may yet meet them,
For the Saviour shall go with them,
His eye follows them.

# 3.
All is well, if here they must
Yet tarry for a time,
And if through death they pass
To heaven’s rest and peace.

# 4.
All is well in the narrow vale
As on the mountain’s height,
All is well in joy and song,
In all distress they have hope.

# 5.
All is well, when song ascends
And the path appears bright,
So also all is well in severe trial,
In sorrow and temptations.

# 6.
Well, when with sounds of joy
To the heights they go,
And all is well, so God accounts,
When in the fiery trial they stand.

# 7.
And last, how wondrous,
When Jesus’ word reaches them
And they hear the trumpet clearly,
They shall meet Him!
