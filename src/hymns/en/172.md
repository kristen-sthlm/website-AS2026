# 1.
God’s only Son, Thou woman’s Seed,
Who on the cross for us didst suffer
And bore sin’s burden;
We praise Thee, who hast conquered the power of the grave,
Laid death low,
And reconciled us!
And reconciled us!

# 2.
We sing of Thy precious blood,
Which to every believer gives courage
And a purged conscience.
Through it we may draw near,
Freely enter the sanctuary
Unto God, the Holy One.
Unto God, the Holy One.

# 3.
Thou hast declared to us Thy Father’s name;
As sons we now draw near,
With joy to praise Thee.
Praise be to Thee in eternity!
All glory, might, and majesty
Are Thine forever.
Are Thine forever.
