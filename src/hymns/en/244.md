# 1.
We have in heaven a High Priest,
Who ever can have compassion,
Who saves completely, preserves from fall,
And strengthens us here on our path.

# 2.
And He Himself was tempted in all things as we are,
All suffering He knows well,
And stands by us in every trial,
To help the tempted soul.

# 3.
We praise Thee, Christ, our High Priest!
Now outside the camp we go,
For within the veil is our place,
Where we ever may worship.
