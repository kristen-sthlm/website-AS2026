# 1.
Not yet do we see all things
Subjected to our Lord,
Not yet is earth’s glory
Brought to His feet.
But He who once was made lower
Than all the angels,
Risen from the power of death,
Our Jesus now we see.

# 2.
We await that morning
When faith shall be exchanged
For sight and clear vision
At the close of the wilderness journey;
Yet even now in that blessedness
May our souls dwell,
And amid the changes of time
Enjoy heavenly rest.

# 3.
Soon shall we, O Jesus,
Behold Thee as Thou art,
Help us to more patiently,
More faithfully witness here
Of Thee, who in a sinful world
Wast God’s faithful witness,
And thus for them follow Thee
A path here hast prepared.

# 4.
We praise Thee, our Lord,
As Thy Spirit teaches us,
That Thou hast opened the way
For us to the Father’s world.
Thy desire is to have us
In spirit with Thee there,
That in us may be perfected
Thy joy already here.
