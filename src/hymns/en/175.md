# 1.
I belong to heaven’s free children, for Jesus has made me free
From Satan’s bonds and the law’s judgment and sin’s slavery;
And since He, out of pure grace, has loosed my poor soul,
I will be a “freeman of the Lord” and no man’s slave.

# 2.
What wonder, if my longing now ever more stretches forth,
My Lord and my Saviour, upward, upward to Thee!
My origin is, O God, from Thee, and I am a stranger here,
In heaven is my citizenship, and there I am at home.

# 3.
Now I may stand in the same grace that sought and found me,
And trust in His faithfulness who allowed Himself to be offered.
In Him I am white and clean, righteous, holy, acceptable,
As surely as the Father sees no spot in the Son.
