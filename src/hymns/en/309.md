# 1.
What shall it be, O beloved Lord,
With Thee to reign, with Thee to dwell,
When in Thy love already here
We find such sweet repose.
What comfort that word contains:
“Ye shall be where I am.”

# 2.
When nevermore our gaze grows dim
And flesh no longer rises up,
When ended is the long night
And we, O Christ, behold Thee,
Then free from earth’s constraint,
We shall praise Thee with a better song.

# 3.
Thou shalt come: what sweet hope!
Ah, soon we go to meet Thee,
Soon ended is our pilgrimage
And we shall have eternal rest.
Glorified, we shall see
Thee face to face.
