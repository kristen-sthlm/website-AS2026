# 1.
O what a home, our Father’s house,
Where love has found its rest,
Where Christ’s redeemed bride shall dwell
With her Bridegroom blest!
O depth of love without a bound!
Our great portion is this,
To share with God’s beloved One
His Father’s joyful home.

# 2.
O what a home, by love built up,
Prepared from eternity,
Where the only-begotten Son
In the Father’s glory dwells!
Abides in His bosom, knows full well
All that moves therein,
And came that this blissfulness
To proclaim to us here.

# 3.
O what a home, where love so deep,
Infinite, rich, and tender,
In light’s expanse flows forth
Like a mighty stream.
Toward this home we hasten on,
Where all the Son has gained,
He shares with His heart’s bride
In love without measure.
