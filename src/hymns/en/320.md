# 1.
Our Jesus, Thou who art God Himself,
Art worthy of power and glory,
Thou delightest in the sound of our song of praise
And the heart’s act of homage.

# 2.
May all that dwelleth above the sky,
In air, in sea, on earth,
Sing Thy glory, ever new,
Though eternally, eternally traced.

# 3.
And the earth’s circuit from pole to pole
Shall bear offerings of praise!
In eternal power upon God’s throne
Shall God’s Lamb reign.

# 4.
How blessed are they who then shall see Thee,
Who stand in the throne’s radiance!
Many thousands, thousands are they,
But the joy is but one.
