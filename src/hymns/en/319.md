# 1.
O, how sweet that thought is,
To meet Him who suffered here
To reveal God’s grace,
When He shall come here again
And take all the saints
Up with Him into heaven
To be there eternally!

# 2.
To stand forever before the throne,
In holy joy and worship to
Fall down before the Lord;
Not only to see Him on the throne,
No, face to face,
When He shall show His glory
To all His saints.

# 3.
If even now the heart knows not
Of any greater blessedness
Than that He is near;
If heaven’s foretaste already it is
To know the Lord Jesus here,
What will it then be to be there
And bear Him praise at home!

# 4.
What joy to hear the Lord’s voice
Address us with sweet comfort
And mete out praise and reward!
How great our gladness to see
Our Saviour glorified there,
And the Lamb, who was slain, give
Praise, glory, and honour!
