# 1.
Jesus, my Lord, to Bethlehem Thou didst come,
Laid in the manger in deep poverty,
Wonderful grace and mercy, which
Came thus for me, for me!

# 2.
Jesus, my Saviour, praise be to Thy name!
My sins Thou didst bear on the tree of the cross,
Hanging there as God’s spotless Lamb,
Thou didst die for me, for me!

# 3.
Jesus, my Shepherd, Thy love doth reach me,
Faithfully Thou hast loved Thy lost sheep,
Patiently Thou didst seek it year after year,
Calling me, yea me!

# 4.
Jesus, my Shepherd, ascended Thou art,
Glory and honour crown Thee there.
Faithful Saviour, my heart now teach
To look only on Thee, on Thee!

# 5.
Jesus, my Lord, how blessed Thy comfort,
Soon shall I hear Thy lovely voice,
Lean me securely against Thy gracious breast
At home with Thee, with Thee!
