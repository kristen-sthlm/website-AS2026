# 1.
God loves without compare
And loves each one,
To poor and rich
He has the same love.
His fatherly heart yearns
For every human soul,
Not the least child does He forget,
But provides for its well-being.

# 2.
And He in grace turned
To poor sinners,
He sent His only Son,
True God and Saviour.
And now He faithfully knocks
On the heart in every breast,
He shall not lose any,
Of those He has redeemed.

# 3.
His love toward us burns,
It is so rich and full,
It constantly overflows
Only for His own sake.
We cannot earn
A single drop thereof,
But God in Christ Jesus
Freely gave it to us.

# 4.
No voice from earth
Bade Him descend
To this poor world,
Where all suffered from the fall.
Sent by the Father, He came
Down here from heaven’s height
To transform all our need
Into eternal peace and joy.
