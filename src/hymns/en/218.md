# 1.
A childlike spirit of obedience
Is not found in everyone
Of those who here confess
Themselves to believe in the Saviour;
For the sweet yoke of Jesus
One must take upon oneself,
Subdue the self-will
And find rest for the heart.

# 2.
If suffering and reproach
On the way meet us here,
Ah, remember it is God’s love
That thus teaches us obedience,
And deeper becomes the joy
To obey the Lord’s commandments,
If we on the altar-height
Have received testimony from God.

# 3.
Surely Jeremiah laments
That he is shut in
And in the days of sorrow
Pressed on every side;
But every trial becomes
A high Jacob’s ladder,
On which the soul ascends
To God with all its need.

# 4.
Have comfort then, pilgrim band,
For we are reminded indeed
That the water in Marah
Was bitter before as now;
But see, who is that tree
Which was cast into its stream?
Yes, it is Jesus Christ,
Who for us suffered and died.

# 5.
And mark, His obedience He learned
Through what He suffered;
God set value thereon,
He became the “Captain of salvation.”
At the thought of His love,
Which has suffered and died,
Affliction, sorrow, and reproach,
Our Marah-water, becomes sweet.
