# 1.
Teach us to know Thy love more fully here,
Thou who hast died,
Our Lord Jesus, who on the cross’s tree.
Thy blood was shed.
Thy pierced side, Thy hands now here
For our weak faith Thou yet dost show.

# 2.
Through Thy death the Father’s heart-law
Is wholly manifest.
Thou hast risen, and now from heaven’s throne
Shines clearly down
Thy Father’s love freely upon us here,
That love which ever on Thee there doth rest.

# 3.
Thy flesh is truly meat, Thy blood,
Shed here,
True drink; of Thee the living bread of God
Our soul doth feed.
In spirit now in Thee we rest may find
In life’s home, where sin and death ne’er come.
