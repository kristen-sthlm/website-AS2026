# 1.
Verily, saith Jesus,
Every one who heareth My word
And believeth on Him who sent
Me hither down to this earth,
He hath eternal life and cometh
Not into judgment; for he hath
Passed from death unto life.
What a wondrous salvation!

# 2.
Hear yet more: an hour shall come,
Yea, it is already come,
When the dead shall hear
The Son’s voice, and they who here
Hear the voice shall live;
God be praised, thus saith He,
Who is the truth and the life
And who cannot lie.

# 3.
Lord Jesus, let those words
Become life to every soul!
Come and from the prison loose
Many a bondman of sin’s thrall!
Thy truths, O Jesus,
In our hearts inscribe;
For the words which Thou dost speak
Are spirit, are life.
