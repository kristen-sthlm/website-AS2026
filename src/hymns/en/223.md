# 1.
Through storm and cloud
God shows our way.
He has His time, and the night’s strife
Soon turns to day.

# 2.
He governs all in grace,
All serves but His might.
Whatever He does, to us brings grace,
His path is light and power.

# 3.
When He has stretched out His arm,
Who can withstand His work?
When it is He who pleads for us,
Who can go against Him?

# 4.
To Him we leave
To choose for us here.
Admiring, we soon shall see
How wise, how strong He is.

# 5.
We cannot grasp Him.
His earth, His heaven’s veil,
Yet show God in sovereign robe
Rules all and makes all well.
