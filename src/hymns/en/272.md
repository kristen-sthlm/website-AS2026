# 1.
There is rest in the tender love,
Which has walked our wilderness path,
Which has given us a place in its own home,
And which takes our sorrow upon itself.

# 2.
In the severe trial there is rest,
For it shows His hand of love.
How sweet it is that we may dwell in His love,
When we walk through the land of sorrow!

# 3.
There is rest in the sweet yoke,
When we have proved His good will,
When we have learned from His word and the path He took,
What is the patience of Jesus.

# 4.
And the path He has gone before
Is the way which we may walk.
It leads us to the home where all sorrow ends,
Where we reach our blessed goal.
