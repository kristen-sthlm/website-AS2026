# 1.
Oh, to be like Christ,
In that which is wonderful,
To in the brightness of faith sing
Of all the depth and height of His love;
To ever behold His beauty
In an unclouded clarity,
And free from earthly bonds
Forever enjoy the Lord’s joy.

# 2.
Yet blessed to be here
And in sorrow and danger
To gladly proclaim His virtues,
Who has poured out His blood here,
To clearly shine for Him
As a light in the darkness of the age,
And with joy in our hearts
Offer thanks and praise to God.

# 3.
Now we may press onward
And take possession of our land,
Which flows with milk and honey
And bears sweet fruits,
To experience the Lord’s grace and power,
And with the beautiful grapes of Eshcol
Revive weary souls
To rejoice already here.

# 4.
The Lord Himself is near us,
Well may we bring home
The offering of our redeemed souls
In the sound of holy songs.
How sweet to the Father’s ear
To hear the name Jesus
From the lips of the little children
On the journey home to God.
