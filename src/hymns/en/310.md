# 1.
Hear, the blessed throng of millions
Sing the Lamb’s praise, oh hear!
Thousands upon thousands answer,
Like an echo that ne’er will die.

# 2.
“Praise the Lamb!” thus they are singing,
The song fills heaven’s expanse;
High and wide, each tongue is raising
Praise to the gospel’s advance.

# 3.
That same song of praise there sounding,
Eternal ‘round the Father’s throne,
The Lamb His offering is bringing
In full, harmonious, united tone.

# 4.
All to the Son homage render,
Even as to the Father’s name;
Seen in the Son’s own brightness tender,
The Father’s glory stands proclaim’d.

# 5.
By God’s Holy Spirit learning,
Saints perceive that Jesus bears
All divine power and glory turning,
He who ever is and was and there.

# 6.
Hear those wondrous tones ascending,
How they praise Him there on high!
“Amen!” heaven’s heights are sending,
“Amen!” earth’s deep valleys cry.
