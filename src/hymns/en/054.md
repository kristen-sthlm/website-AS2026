# 1.
He liveth, He liveth, our hope of salvation,
He liveth in heaven’s height.
Our eye unto Him in faith upward gazes,
And the heart is filled with joy.

# 2.
He liveth, now shall we also live
A life in resurrection’s power.
The faithful word shall eternally stand,
For Himself once hath said it.

# 3.
He liveth, He liveth, the same who once died
For our sins upon the cross.
We live in Him, He dieth no more,
We sing resurrection’s song.

# 4.
He liveth and soon cometh again in the clouds;
This hope now our comfort doth yield.
From graves shall arise a rejoicing host
At the Lord’s awakening voice.

# 5.
With this we shall be caught up in the clouds
To meet and behold our God,
To see fully accomplished the assembly’s hope,
When the Bridegroom meets His bride.
