# 1.
No silver, no gold has acquired my salvation,
No earthly treasures have freed my soul.
No, the blood on the cross is the price I have cost,
The only-begotten Son was given for my good.

# C.
Redeemed I am, yet not with silver,
Delivered, yet not with gold.
O what a price! With precious blood
God has blotted out all my guilt.

# 2.
No silver, no gold has erased my debt,
No, for this my debt was too great and too heavy;
Only the blood on the cross could remove it,
Only therein do I receive deliverance from sin.

# 3.
No silver, no gold could, when I was afar,
Win me access to God and His grace;
But the blood on the cross, it brought me near.
What depth and what height in the counsel of the Eternal!
