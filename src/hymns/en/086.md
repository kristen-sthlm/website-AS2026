# 1.
Lift up now your hearts
And rejoice in the Lord,
Sing praise, sing praise with a joyful voice!
Our God is almighty,
And the Lord is King,
And the bride, His beloved, He gives comfort.
Eternal is His love,
He Himself has been offered up,
The prince of death overthrown.
Hallelujah!

# 2.
Thou didst ascend, O Lord,
God’s Champion, who hast conquered;
Ascended Thou art, and Thou shalt reign.
Thou shalt subdue the peoples
And win the nations,
And the earth shall be Thy footstool.
Sing, sing unto God,
Sing unto Christ, the Lord,
For His holy throne stands fast.
Hallelujah!
