# 1.
Ah, who can sufficiently praise,
Great God, Thy goodness here!
All Thy works testify
That it is inexpressible.

# C.
Thou art worthy to have the glory.
|: Praise be to Thee! Hallelujah! :|

# 2.
Who can describe Thy love,
Who Thy grace, O Father kind,
When we see Thee give Thy Son
To atone for all our guilt.

# 3.
Who can measure Thy faithfulness,
Jesus, Thou our Surety!
Can a mother forget her child,
Thou canst not forget us.
