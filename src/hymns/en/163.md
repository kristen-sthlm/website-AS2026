# 1.
Grace is a glorious word,
Blessed is he who rightly understands it;
Through the heavens, over the earth
Its mighty echo resounds.

# 2.
It was the God of all grace,
Who in His counsel of love
Gave the only Son for the world
In ineffable grace.

# 3.
And this same rich grace
Preserves us from falling,
It shall ever follow us
Along the entire way home.

# 4.
The grace that saved us
Now nurtures and teaches us
To gladly forsake the world’s vanity
And live godly here.

# 5.
By grace alone we receive
The crown God prepares,
By grace we enter into heaven,
Divine grace it is.
