# 1.
Glory, Lord, we give to Thee,
Thou art our Redeemer;
God our debt on Thee hath laid,
Ransom full for us Thou hast brought.

# 2.
All Thy work completed is,
Thou to the Father joy dost render;
He from death did bring Thee forth,
Gave Thee life eternal.

# 3.
At His side Thou sittest now,
Dost in patience wait there still,
Makest intercession good for us with God,
Till Thou dost fetch home Thy bride.

# 4.
Soon Thou shalt come again,
Take us to the Father’s home.
We Thy coming surely wait;
Amen, come, O Jesus Christ!
