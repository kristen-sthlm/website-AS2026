# 1.
Rejoice now, my blessed heart,
Greater joy was never found!
Now is ended sorrow and pain,
Jesus is mine and I am His.
Now is ended sorrow and pain,
Jesus is mine and I am His.

# 2.
Every wound of sin He has healed,
For He is so good and gracious.
With His Spirit He has sealed
Now His precious possession.
With His Spirit He has sealed
Now His precious possession.

# 3.
He has written my name in heaven,
O, what joy! I surely know:
I am His for all my life,
I am His in eternity.
I am His for all my life,
I am His in eternity.

# 4.
Now I can, in childlike spirit,
Cry: “Abba, dear Father!”
It is joy, He dwells within,
Such blessed company I now have.
It is joy, He dwells within,
Such blessed company I now have.

# 5.
Sin and world, farewell forever,
Never shall you have me again!
Jesus saves, saves gloriously,
Every moment He is my Friend.
Jesus saves, saves gloriously,
Every moment He is my Friend.

# 6.
Now with tones of praise so clear
Heaven gives back an echo,
And I hear my heart reply:
“Amen, amen, praise be to God.”
And I hear my heart reply:
“Amen, amen, praise be to God.”
