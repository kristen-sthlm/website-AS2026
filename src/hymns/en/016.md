# 1.
From His home in glory,
From His lofty hall of joy,
The Lord Jesus came to save
Sinners in the valley of death.

# 2.
There, by the Majesty’s side,
Yet He thought of us here,
And His heart’s deep longing
Was to have us with Him there.

# 3.
Trembling, a place we sought
Only at the door of His home,
But our room, our robe, and crown,
All was ready long ago.

# 4.
For ere a world was created,
Yea, from eternity’s prime,
He purposed to share with us
All His joy and glory.
