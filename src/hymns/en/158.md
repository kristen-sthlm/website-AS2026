# 1.
Hear, the Saviour calls, He seeks thy welfare,
He calls from heaven, He loves thy soul!
Long hast thou hesitated, ah, hear now His voice,
His blood He has shed, thy guilt He has loosed.

# 2.
Behold, the Father calls thee and pleads in grace!
O wanderer, come now, for the time is urgent!
He longs to save each one who will believe,
He gives to the weary, burdened one rest.

# 3.
Yea, believe now on the Son, in favour thou standest,
And in His atonement thou receivest full salvation.
The judgment we deserved, He bore, O how great!
Thou canst do nothing, for all hath He done.

# 4.
Soon the Saviour will fetch us all who believe,
And then His own shall dwell in glory.
Confess the Lord Jesus, thy Lord and God,
Then shall He clothe thee in righteous attire!
