# 1.
Thy name, O Father, we praise,
Like children taught by grace.
Help us in righteousness
And joy to stand before Thee!

# 2.
Sweet the confidence, peace, and joy
Thou dost bestow upon our heart.
Our spirit now has its refuge
In the light where Thou art.

# 3.
There, in Thy love’s purpose, He
A place for us hath prepared,
As sons with Him now,
He who bore our sorrow here.

# 4.
Though still here, our hope it is,
When the path is fully trod,
That He will take us up to Himself
Far beyond death’s power.

# 5.
Holy Father, keep us thus,
Within Thy great love,
That without fear we may walk here,
Until we dwell with Thee.
