# 1.
Beholding God’s Son on high,
Let our hearts rejoice,
See in Him the Father’s love,
Love deep, infinite.

# 2.
Lord Jesus, we admire,
Thank, praise, worship Thee,
Thou who here on earth hast trodden
The path of anguish of the cross.

# 3.
Men’s scorn, contempt, and mockery
Hast Thou patiently endured here;
All Thy sorrow is exchanged for joy
At Thy Father’s side there.

# 4.
But the deep wounds, Lord,
Which Thou bearest in Thy hands,
Show clearly in the glory,
That for us Thou wast crucified.

# 5.
God hid His face
In that hour of distress and woe;
The woe of the cross none can fathom,
Save Thou, God’s Holy One.

# 6.
The heavens give Thee glory,
We too worship Thee now.
Precious Lord Jesus Christ,
Thou alone art worthy!
