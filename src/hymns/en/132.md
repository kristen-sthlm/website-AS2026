# 1.
The blood of Jesus has opened for us
The way to God.
Happy is he who now receives
The call of grace.

# 2.
Only through this blood
Is there forgiveness.
God can now freely grant pardon
To all.

# 3.
Yes, the transgressor who in sin
Otherwise must die,
Through the blood of Jesus can become
White as snow.

# 4.
Now we draw near to the Holy One
With a holy boldness;
For to God Jesus has purchased us
With His blood.

# 5.
Who can comprehend the value of the blood,
This holy stream,
Which still proclaims God’s love,
Deep and tender!

# 6.
Lord Jesus, we thank Thee
For Thy precious blood,
Thou who gavest Thy life for the sheep,
Good Shepherd!
