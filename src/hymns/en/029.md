# 1.
It was the night of deepest woe,
When darkness round Thee closed,
When Thou for our transgressions didst endure,
And depths of waters round Thee flowed.
Thou didst command us, O our Saviour,
To celebrate Thy remembrance.

# 2.
How full, how bitter was the cup
That Thou for us didst drain;
How great the love that sought us out,
When we before God did hide.
Now may we gratefully remember Thee,
And freely may the heart rejoice.

# 3.
How precious is Thy thought,
How wondrous is Thy will,
That us, who were enemies,
Thou wouldest separate unto Thyself,
To praise and worship Thee,
And walk in the path of Thy will.

# 4.
Graced are we now
In Thee, who hast been exalted.
Thy soon coming we await,
The promise Thou hast given;
Until then our heart’s deep desire
Is ever to remember Thee.
