# 1.
Thou shalt come, and we shall hasten
To Thy meeting, beloved Lord,
To behold Thee, to thank, to praise,
To show Thee our love thus,
As we never could here.
Then shall we in glory
Bring forth the sacrifice of jubilation,
Hail Thee with shouts of joy,
O Thou holy Lamb of God!

# 2.
Thou shalt come, and we await
After Thee each moment.
While life’s hours hasten by,
Our hope may in security rest
On the anchor-ground of Thy love.
Day and time God alone knows,
But Thy word of promise abides,
The certainty of hope imparts courage,
The expectation of faith attains victory.

# 3.
O, what joy to see Thee reign,
Beloved Saviour, once.
Every tongue shall bring Thee
Praise and worship, and loudly shall resound
The song of jubilation to Thy glory.
O, what joy when only Thee
Earth’s ends shall homage give,
And when every people at last
Shall in Thee its King see!
