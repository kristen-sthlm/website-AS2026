# 1.
Here we gather, Lord Jesus,
We give Thee our heart’s thanks,
Thou, who in Thy love didst bid us
Keep Thy remembrance.
Thanks and praise, thanks and praise
For the holy life Thou didst yield,
The offering Thou didst present to God,
Thou our Redeemer.

# 2.
The bread, which here we may break,
Is fellowship with Thy body,
And from the cup we are reminded,
God hath received full atonement.
Thanks and praise, thanks and praise
For the peace Thou hast won for us,
For the precious blood that hath flowed
To atone for our debt!

# 3.
Thus may we keep Thy supper
During the wilderness journey,
And proclaim Thy precious death,
Until Thou shalt come again.
Thanks and praise, thanks and praise!
Thy words we still may hear,
What Thou didst bid us thankfully do
Now in remembrance of Thee.

# 4.
Thee with joy of heart we praise,
Jesus Christ, God’s Lamb of sacrifice,
And Thy God, who gave Thee forth,
The Son in the Father’s bosom.
Thanks and praise, thanks and praise!
Soon shalt Thou Thy bride proclaim,
Thyself with her manifest
In Thy Father’s glory.
