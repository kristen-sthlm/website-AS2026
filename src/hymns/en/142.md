# 1.
We speak to you of God’s grace,
So boundless, so free, and so rich,
But what shall it avail your soul,
If it is not received by you?

# 2.
We speak of the great salvation,
Which the Father in Jesus has made known,
But if you will live for God,
In faith you must receive it.

# 3.
We speak of the Saviour’s name,
By which God reaches sinners.
Lost you nevertheless shall be,
If on that name you do not believe.

# 4.
We speak of the Lamb, whose blood
Now makes you free from sins.
If you have not proved its power,
Eternally unclean you shall remain.

# 5.
We speak of great glory,
Of heavens of light and of peace,
But if you do not believe in Jesus,
You shall not, you cannot there dwell.
