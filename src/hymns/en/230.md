# 1.
Blessed is he who knows Thee, Lord,
Whose God and Friend Thou art.
Among all earth’s friends
There is no friend so dear;
And should all others
Here entirely change,
We yet shall walk securely,
For we ever have Thee.

# 2.
Who can Thy love extol,
O Lord, as is its due?
And who can show the care
With which Thou ever dost provide for us?
Thy goodness encompasses us,
Within Thy shelter we stand,
Thy arm remains our strength,
From Thee all good we receive.

# 3.
Though we walk in the dark valley,
In distress and need,
Thou art our light, our comfort,
Our strength and our bread.
From heaven’s rich bounties
Thou dost abundantly provide for us;
But greatest of all gifts
Thou Thyself, O Jesus, art.

# 4.
If already here on earth
Thou dost thus comfort the heart,
What shall I not enjoy,
When made blessed!
No tongue can utter,
No heart yet knows,
How Thou wilt gladden us
In Thy glory.
