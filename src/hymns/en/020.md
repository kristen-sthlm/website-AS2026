# 1.
O come, let us sing of Jesus,
Our precious and faithful Friend,
Who went to death for us all,
Who loved and still loves us!

# 2.
For who has given us greater
Proof of His love than He,
Who through His death for us all
Won for us an eternal redemption?

# 3.
He left the kingdom of heaven
And came to our sinful earth
To serve, to suffer and bleed,
And die according to the Scriptures’ word.

# 4.
He walked here so poor and lowly,
That none so poor here was found,
Though He was the Lord of Creation,
And heaven and earth were His.

# 5.
On Golgotha He won the victory,
And the enemy’s power is crushed.
He triumphantly rose from the grave,
And brought forth life in the light.

# 6.
And when He had completed the work,
He ascended to heaven again.
Now He is there above with the Father,
Our faithful Advocate still.

# 7.
O Jesus, Thy love we behold
In all that Thou didst and dost,
A love proved in deed,
Just when Thou diest for sinners.
