# 1.
Rest in the home with the Lord,
Far from conflict and strife,
Already in hope it imparts
To the believing heart peace.

# C.
Jesus, with Thee is found rest,
Eternal sabbath repose;
Ah, soon after the storm and the conflict
We shall with Thee dwell.

# 2.
The angel with the sword is gone,
Open stands Eden’s gate,
The eye, though tears may flow,
Yet sees that fair place.

# 3.
Over the suffering heart
Shines the bright day,
Permitting the tested child
To be satisfied with God’s good pleasure.

# 4.
The ear amid a thousand voices
Yet hears a gentle sound,
Hears how the Spirit proclaims
Sweet things concerning God.

# 5.
And of the heavenly treasures
Already Thou dost here give us,
Giving to hungry souls
Daily more and more.
Jesus, with Thee,
etc.
