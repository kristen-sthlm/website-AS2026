# 1.
O countenance, pale and bloodied,
How art Thou, O Son of God!
By men, in their pride,
Met with scorn and mockery!
Thy weary head Thou bowest
Now down toward the earth,
And Thy final sigh Thou raisest,
And to Thy Father prayest.

# 2.
Thy divine power is concealed,
Thou art wearied by sorrows,
Thy gentle eye is veiled
At last by death’s night;
Thy loving heart
Is pierced by the spear.
Where indeed is there a sorrow
Like Thine, O beloved Jesus?

# 3.
Unto all Thou wast made to suffer,
My sin was a cause.
Me Thou didst will to redeem,
When the weight of the cross Thou didst bear,
My whole heart gives Thee thanks,
Lord Jesus, ever kind,
For Thy deep suffering
And all Thy patience.
