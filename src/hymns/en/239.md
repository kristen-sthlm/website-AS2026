# 1.
O Lord, how measureless
Thy love to us is;
How great the grace and favour
Thou ever dost bestow upon us.

# 2.
Yet, Lord, what weakness
Is manifest to Thee
In us, whom Thou hast called
To walk in Thy path.

# 3.
Thy love, nevertheless, abides,
However we have acted,
And Thee we ever find
The same as Thou wast.

# 4.
O, that with Thee, our Lord,
We constantly walked,
Then would Thy glory
Ever be revealed to us.

# 5.
Far more of Thy fulness
We then should comprehend,
Thee with all the heart worship,
And walk in Thy ways.

# 6.
How sweet, however, then we find,
When clouds that veiled Thee
Have fled, Thou art the same,
And never dost change.

# 7.
Thy love can assuredly
Preserve us from falling,
Until before God blameless
Thy people Thou shalt place.
