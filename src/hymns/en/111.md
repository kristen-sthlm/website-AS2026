# 1.
Ah, teach us better to know Thee,
God’s holy Lamb, who didst take
God’s judgment and all our guilt upon Thee,
Thy grace for us is sufficient.

# 2.
O, may we ever count now
What we on earth have received
As of no value compared to Thee,
Our inheritance, our portion!

# 3.
Ah, tell how Thou hast loved us,
How Thou dost still love us,
And let us remember all the days
Thou art and wilt remain our Friend.

# 4.
Write this into our heart,
That we may not forget it,
But show that we belong to Thee
For time and eternity.
