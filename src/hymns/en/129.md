# 1.
Thou art the eternal Word of God,
God’s Son in the Father’s bosom,
God manifest, God seen and heard,
God’s spotless Lamb of sacrifice.
For Thee, God’s precious Lamb, for Thee
Shall every knee soon bow.

# 2.
O Jesus! Thou the bright effulgence
Of the Father’s glory,
Thou art the image of God, heaven’s delight,
Light and perfection.
For Thee, God’s precious Lamb, for Thee
Shall every knee soon bow.

# 3.
Thy being’s glory and power
Our praise far surpasses.
What the name “the Son” implies
Only the Father fully knows.
For Thee, God’s precious Lamb, for Thee
Shall every knee soon bow.
