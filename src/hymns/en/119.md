# 1.
Thou who hast loved us ere the worlds were,
Jesus, our Lord, our Saviour good,
Thou, who in love Thyself hast given,
And hast redeemed us to God by Thy blood.

# 2.
Thee our hearts sing and praise,
Rejoice that Thou now hast Thy glory received,
Rejoice that the suffering for Thee is ended,
And that with victory from the grave Thou hast gone.

# 3.
Thou, who hast prepared us a place in Thy heaven,
Since Thou on earth hast glorified God,
Thou, who art not ashamed to call us "brethren,"
Thee we now praise with the sound of praise.
