# 1.
Praise, worship, adoration, and glory
We bring home to Thee, O Jesus Christ,
Our God and Redeemer!
Obedient to the Father unto death,
Thou becamest our Deliverer in distress,
From eternal death our Saviour.
The grave did not hold Thee,
Though it was sealed;
To Thy Father Thou didst return,
And now Thou art
For us in God’s presence there.

# 2.
High at the Majesty’s side
We see Thee, Lord Christ, and await
With joy Thy appearing.
The Father hath crowned Thee with glory,
With the oil of gladness God hath anointed Thee,
Thou Firstfruits of the resurrection.
Yet heaven still holds
Thee, who wast abased;
But in glory and majesty
Thou shalt come
And reign over all the world.
