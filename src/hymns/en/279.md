# 1.
How sweet it is, O Lord,
That though still on earth,
Yet we have above,
With Thee our true home;
For where Thou now sittest,
In faith our rest we have found;
For us heaven is opened,
Since Thou hast gone from the grave.

# 2.
In spirit already there,
Soon we, who on Thee believe,
In body and soul perfected,
With Thee may eternally dwell.
Thy Father’s gracious care
Makes the path of thirst bright,
Thy countenance makes joyful
Our way to the Father’s house.

# 3.
The Comforter here below
Comforts us with Thy grace
And gives us already a foretaste
Of all Thy counsel of love.
With favour Thou dost cheer us
And ever surroundest us here,
Until the new name
Our brow shall once bear.

# 4.
Thy glory we await;
Our home is not here,
But among the redeemed throng
With Thee, our beloved Lord.
No place can rightly please us
Where Thou art not,
And to be with Thee
Is now our soul’s desire.
