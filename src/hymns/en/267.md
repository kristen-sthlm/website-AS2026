# 1.
Firm in a world of sin and strife,
We few enjoy a blessed peace.
Though the way be hard and perilous,
Yet, O our God, to Thee it leads,
And in Thy light we shall not err
As to the goal and our inheritance.

# 2.
Thy gentle hand is never empty,
With Thee there is no poverty,
And Thou art near us with Thy grace.
An inexhaustible rich store
Of strength and courage is given us,
And Thou Thyself bearest all our care.

# 3.
For every step that time takes,
Our soul tastes Thy goodness;
Thy arm of strength is our protection.
Soon the night is fled before the day,
When in sight our faith shall be exchanged,
And we shall dwell with Thee eternally.
