# 1.
Raise your voices in holy choir
And sing His great love,
Sing His glory!
He, who is exalted there,
Is the admiration of angels,
He bears many crowns,
Our Priest and King.

# 2.
A great reproach He bore;
The cup was bitter,
For us He took it.
God’s angels looked down
With astonishment at how He suffered.
My soul, fall down, worship!
For thee He died.

# 3.
His grace is rich and warm,
Strong is His arm of salvation,
He is God’s Son;
And soon, in light once more,
Throughout eternity’s long course,
We shall sing a new song
To Him there.

# 4.
Yet, though feebly, still
His praise we must sing
In childlike wise.
He is heaven’s joy,
He bears the crown of glory,
With joy we sing here
And there His praise!
