# 1.
Thou spotless Lamb of God, on the cross’s tree
Thou didst suffer death and smite our foe.
Now exalted in heaven, God’s delight,
God’s true bread, who once wast dead,
Thou livest still and comest soon again.

# 2.
His flesh, His blood our good Shepherd gave
For the sheep, and entered for them the grave;
But He, our life, our hope, arose;
And though He vanished in the sky,
He liveth still and cometh soon again.

# 3.
His little flock with the word of life
He faithfully feeds, and the lambs Himself doth bear.
Though we our Saviour now not see,
His supper now doth testify:
He liveth still and cometh soon again.

# 4.
“Until ye see Me once again,
With bread and wine proclaim here My death.
In the world ye shall have tribulation; My peace
I have given you, remain ye one.
I live still and come soon again.”
