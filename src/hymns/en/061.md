# 1.
Lord Jesus, Thee we honour,
Exalted on Thy Father’s throne,
The heart’s homage to Thee we bear,
God’s only begotten, precious Son.
Thee we praise,
Thee we worship,
|: Laud Thee in united tone. :|

# 2.
Lord of life! Unto death obedient
Here, Thy cross Thou didst bear,
Thou, who to Thy Father’s heart
Wast a perfect joy.
Thee we revere,
Thee we remember,
|: Thou, who wast the Offering Lamb. :|

# 3.
Anointed King, crowned with glory,
Lord of glory, Thou,
Once smitten and despised,
Now richly rewarded by the Father,
Thee we behold
And admire,
|: Thou joy of heaven’s spring! :|

# 4.
Royal robe shall soon adorn Thee,
Soon Thou shalt the diadem wear.
Thou God’s Anointed, the heart’s obedience
Gratefully we give Thee here;
Thee we adore,
Thee we hymn,
|: Lord Christ, where Thou art. :|
