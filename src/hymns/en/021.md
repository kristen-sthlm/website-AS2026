# 1.
Thou gentle Lamb of God, Thou precious Lamb,
Who camest down to earth,
And tookest upon Thee our sin and shame,
And didst endure anguish and distress;

# C.
Thou didst step forth in our place,
And didst die on Golgotha,
Thou didst die for us, O holy Lamb of God,
Praise Thee! Hallelujah!

# 2.
Thou wast wounded and smitten sore,
That Thou mightest set us free
From sin’s guilt and the grievous penalty,
And Satan’s tyranny.

# 3.
We walk by faith in Jesus Christ,
Toward heaven we go,
To receive at last our inheritance
In the city of the living God.
