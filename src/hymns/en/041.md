# 1.
Had we been beasts on a thousand hills
With earth’s fatness, sap, and marrow,
That could be given as an offering,
Vain would be their blood,
Vain would flow this flood;
Sin is not taken away thus.

# 2.
A better offering is required,
When the conscience knows itself guilty
And cannot approach
God with sin: it is His blood,
Which once stood in our place,
It is God’s spotless Lamb.

# 3.
He who was and is one with the Father,
Jehovah’s equal, the beloved Son,
Who created the world,
He Himself, when no other blood was found
That could cleanse us, even His,
Was the only offering.

# 4.
He took the form of a servant,
When He came to earth and died
And became our Saviour.
He has atoned for all our sin,
He Himself was our ransom,
He, the Righteous One.

# 5.
How sweet this message of grace!
In Christ we may draw near to God,
May behold the Holy One.
And all that once stood in the way
Has been removed by Jesus’ blood;
We have forgiveness.

# 6.
And before the Father we now stand,
Our sin He no longer remembers,
For it is taken away.
Thus we sing in childlike manner
Our Lord Jesus’ eternal praise
And begin already here.
