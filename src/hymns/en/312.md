# 1.
Jerusalem, God’s city,
In faith we behold thee.
At the thought of thy glory
Our heart rejoices.

# 2.
Thou secure haven of rest,
Where soon we shall rest,
Where freely our soul shall enjoy
All that here she longs for.

# 3.
Who can declare here
The joy that awaits us then,
When we with all the saints
Shall praise Thee, Lord.

# 4.
No tongue can tell,
What joy and peace it will be
In eternities’ eternity
To dwell with God and the Lamb.

# 5.
The “hidden manna” then,
Known in the wilderness here,
God’s Son, the living bread of God,
Shall ever nourish us there.

# 6.
God Himself and the Lamb then
Shall be light and temple,
And the redeemed hosts shall fully understand
God’s mystery therein.
