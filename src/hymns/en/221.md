# 1.
All I would leave to Jesus,
All to Him I gladly bear.
Him I would trust and love,
In His company walk here.

# C.
I now leave all.
Yes, I leave all.
All to Thee, my precious Jesus.
I now leave all.

# 2.
Jesus, all I surrender,
Give myself entirely to Thee.
Fill me with Thy power and love,
Send a shower of grace on me.

# 3.
All to Jesus I surrender,
Holy fervour now fills me.
For the full salvation’s joy,
Praise be to God eternally!
