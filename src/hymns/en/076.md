# 1.
O Lord Jesus, Thou art worthy
To have eternal praise and glory,
Thou who hast crushed the serpent’s head
And opened paradise for us.
Yea, Thou hast slain the enmity
And purchased us for God with Thy blood;
Thou hast gained victory without weapons,
When Thou didst stand in our stead.

# 2.
Praise, honour, thanks, and glory and dominion
And majesty belong to Thee.
In Thy death Thou didst overthrow death
And livest now eternally.
Thou art the Son, in whom the Father
Hath His delight from eternity;
Yea, Thou art He who fillest heaven
With light and joy and glory.
