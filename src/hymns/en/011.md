# 1.
O God our Father, good and gracious,
God of all grace and truth,
Who hast sent Thy Son, Thy true image,
To us with the message of grace.
He hath put the enemy to shame
And proclaimed to us Thy Father’s name.

# 2.
As Man He hath glorified Thee,
Magnified Thy name;
He hath offered Himself upon the cross,
A spotless sacrificial Lamb.
He hath thus fulfilled all Thy will,
That we might enjoy Thy presence.

# 3.
Thou hast shown the depth of Thy love,
Its length and height and breadth;
In Christ and His work for us
Thy wisdom now is seen.
Thy power and glory we behold
In Thy Son’s resurrection.

# 4.
Thou hast exalted Him who suffered here,
To the height of glory;
He is Lord of all lords,
And the joy of all heavens.
In the clouds He cometh soon again
And taketh His gathering home.

# 5.
Soon Israel and all peoples
Shall render Him homage,
And kings from every land
Shall bow the knee to Christ.
His glory shall then be widely spread
Among the children of men on this earth.
