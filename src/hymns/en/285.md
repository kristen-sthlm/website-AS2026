# 1.
The path of the righteous is like the light of the dawn,
Which grows in brightness more and more,
Until the day has reached its full perfection,
And no shadow of the night is seen.

# 2.
In a radiance so vast that darkness no longer reaches there,
And where death is brought to nothing,
The path of the righteous leads toward the home of love,
Where the Sun appears in glory.

# 3.
The Lord Jesus, God’s holy One, the slain Lamb,
Is the Sun in whose circle of light we stand,
And with joy we look onward to the coming day,
When we shall behold His face.
