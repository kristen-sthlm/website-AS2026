# 1.
Our Father, we come as happy children
Before Thee to give Thee glory.
For all that Thy Spirit has made known to us of Thee
From the heart we bring Thee praise
And rejoice to be near Thee.

# 2.
Thanks, Father, for Christ, Thy beloved Son,
Who came to reveal to us
Thy heart’s law, as it was of old
And as it ever shall be!
He came to declare all Thy grace.

# 3.
In the Son, who here in the light of Thy countenance
As the "Branch" has sprung up before Thee,
In Him, who builds the temple for Thee,
Thy love has found its rest.
To Thee has He won glory.

# 4.
What joy to see Him toward that day when His praise
Shall loudly through the heavens resound,
When jubilant hosts, who praise Thy name,
Worshipping shall fall down before Thee
And Christ shall be acknowledged by all.
