# 1.
Father, Thou who from Thy heaven
Lookest down upon Thine own,
Up to Thee from earth’s confusion
Trustingly our eye is turned.
We before Thy presence come,
Open stands Thy gracious embrace,
And to the throne of grace we approach
With our prayer in Jesus’ name.

# 2.
We ask not for earthly joy,
Nor for gold, O Father dear,
But that all Thou wilt so lead
That Thy name be honoured here.
Speak Thou to our hearts
Of Thy love’s depth and height,
That we may be wholly filled
With Thy Spirit’s peace and joy.
