# 1.
O Thou, who gavest Thy life for the sheep,
For grace and power we beseech Thee,
That henceforth we may follow Thee in Thy footsteps
On the path of holiness and peace.
Let Thy flock, so scattered,
Be more and more united in Thy truth’s light,
Until Thou comest to lead us
To glory in the Father’s house!
