# 1.
Comfort, my soul, in the Lord,
Forget not that He has thee dear;
Know that He is not far,
Know that thou art not forgotten!
Though thou here below must be
A stranger and a guest,
God nevertheless will deal with thee
As thou needest it best.

# 2.
Amid the changing times
Praise the Father who rules.
The night now swiftly advances,
Glorious the morning that dawns!
He who is loved of the Lord
Must stand under chastening,
Far be it from me
Thus to go sorrowing here.

# 3.
Soon shall our Bridegroom bring home
His dearly purchased bride,
Make us eternally happy,
Give us a robe without spot.
Yea, the Lord’s words are true,
On them we trust,
Sing thus gladly Hosanna,
Amen, yea, even so be it unto us!
