# 1.
Soon shall all the conflicts of the earth be ended,
Soon shall our heart enjoy an undisturbed peace;
Then shall we fully partake of God’s bliss,
Which we could not here in this time.

# 2.
Not yet is it, but the star of hope shines
And shows us a fair and lovely shore;
The sun of righteousness there gloriously shines
In the everlastingly, ever blessed land of peace.

# 3.
Yet here a little while we must contend,
Still stand in “the armour of God,”
And beneath the night await the Morning Star,
Until He who is the Sun shall rise for the world.

# 4.
Now we await soon to behold Christ,
When sight shall replace our faith.
God’s King shall reign in peace upon the throne
And give the peoples of the earth His peace and rest.
