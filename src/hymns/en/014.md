# 1.
Jesus, the true image of the Father,
The brightness of God’s glory,
Eternally shall Thy praise increase
Through time and eternity.

# 2.
Thou who camest from heaven’s heights
Down to the depths of sorrow at the cross,
To crush the power of darkness,
Thee, O Jesus, we worship.

# 3.
Sing, ye ransomed company,
Sing His love without measure.
He removed the sting from death
And hath risen from the grave.

# 4.
Sing His glorious ascension,
Victorious and wondrous,
Sing His majesty upon the throne,
His who was so humbled.

# 5.
Sing His return in the clouds
With all the redeemed saints!
Earth and heaven loudly proclaim,
“The Nazarene” shall reign.
