# 1.
Lord, in Thy supper now we behold
The token of Thy love unto death.
We, delivered from eternal woe,
Praise Thee, who hast endured the wrath’s glow.
No anxious fear now consumes our heart,
For a love without limit we behold here.

# 2.
Bread and cup proclaim Thy wonders,
Witness to a work that was accomplished
In those unspeakably dark hours,
When death’s hard bonds were girded upon Thee.
We praise Thee, Thou hast blotted out all our guilt,
Through Thee we know God our Father gracious.

# 3.
The rift is forgiven! Every barrier
Between God and us Thou hast broken down.
No bond of slavery now binds us,
Free children, our Father we worship.
Thee we now here praise with a holy joy,
Thee we soon shall laud in heaven’s height.
