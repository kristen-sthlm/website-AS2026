# 1.
Our faithful, good Shepherd,
In counsel one with God,
Thanks that Thou here didst accomplish
His great salvation-command!
Sent forth by the Father, Thou camest,
For the sheep didst lead and die,
That life they might possess
And have it abundantly.

# 2.
Since the God of peace from death
Thee, Jesus, hath restored,
Thou other sheep hast called
And they Thy voice have heard.
Even as Thou knowest the Father
And as He knoweth Thee,
So known of Thee is the flock
And their wilderness-path.

# 3.
How securely Thy flock may rest
In Thy Father’s hand!
With Thee it is united
By an indissoluble bond.
He loveth the whole flock,
Which He to Thee hath given,
He beareth it on His heart
And He with Thee is one.

# 4.
Thou great Shepherd of the sheep,
Soon Thou wilt be manifested,
And then for Thy faithful ones
A reward Thou hast with Thee.
Thou, who so tenderly hast led
And guarded the sheep’s steps,
Shalt come in clouds
With the Father’s majesty.
