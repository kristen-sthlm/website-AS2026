# 1.
The work is finished,
The blood of the Offering is poured out,
And Christ now presents
Our cause before His God;
Our Priest, our precious Surety,
Who can perfectly save us.

# 2.
Peace and forgiveness
In His blood we have received.
Our Minister,
He pleads well for us.
He, who endured the cross’s pain here,
Now bears us upon His heart.

# 3.
No great earthly temple
Is now His resting place,
In heaven’s height He dwells,
Our Priest forever there.
All that in the law was shadow,
Is fulfilled in Him, has passed away.

# 4.
He entered with His blood,
The rent veil is forgiveness;
We also may enter
And worship Him there,
Yes, in heaven itself
We behold Jesus, the Saviour.

# 5.
Though for a time He is
Hidden from man’s eye,
Soon the world shall see
That He has not forgotten His people.
From heaven He shall come
And gather all His saints.
