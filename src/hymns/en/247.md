# 1.
Lord, with power through the Spirit clothe me,
I am so powerless and weak in myself.
Let me not go where Thou canst not go with me,
Lead me to the river of grace and truth!

# 2.
Quench my thirst with the living water,
Of which the world knows not the least,
Be to me a shining pillar of fire by night,
Be to me a pillar of cloud when the day is hot!

# 3.
Be to me a defence against sin and vanity,
Thou art a stronghold where I safely may dwell,
Let me not seek my happiness in the world,
Let me not seek my rest in the world!

# 4.
Thou art the overflowing well of blessedness,
Thou art a living fountain for me.
It is my happiness ever to be found,
Christ, my Saviour, only in Thee!
