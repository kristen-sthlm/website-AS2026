# 1.
O, rejoice in the Lord, our Saviour is He,
Come, let us give thanks and praise His name!
Straying sheep, now found, He tends,
And the lambs He bears in His faithful bosom.

# 2.
O, sing in the Spirit, with heart perceive,
O, taste and see how gracious He is!
His glorious virtues and grace now remember,
The eternal Son’s, so dear to the Father.

# 3.
Behold Jesus, the pure, given up in death,
Our Lord, who victoriously rose again.
The eternal life for us Thou hast become,
Only Thou art our Lord, our peace, and our hope.
