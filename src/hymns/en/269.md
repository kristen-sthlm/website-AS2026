# 1.
Saviour, take my hand,
Train me so,
That in Thy footsteps
I may ever walk!
Lord, for each day
Draw me to Thy heart,
That in Thy truth I
Might firmly stand!

# 2.
The Rock, which was smitten for me,
Gives me water,
Manna for each day
Still falls down.
Jesus bears my care,
Ever He has me dear,
Yea, in all things here
Love I see.

# 3.
Jesus, in Thy footsteps
I will follow.
Keep me in joy and need
Only with Thee still!
What more can I desire?
Thou, who seest the need,
All that I lack, givest,
More besides!
