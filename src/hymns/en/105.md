# 1.
Father! We Thy love extol
And Thy counsel of salvation,
Who can know all Thy goodness,
All Thy grace!

# 2.
No forgiveness now remains hidden from Thee,
For it has been rent asunder,
All Thy grace is now in Christ
Manifest.

# 3.
He the great work has accomplished,
The toil is past;
In the love wherein He rests,
We rest.

# 4.
Jesus! Thou Thyself hast given,
Faultless Offering Lamb.
From Thy cross God’s grace and love
Flow forth.

# 5.
Yea, God’s love-sun now shines
Unveiled down.
Up, my soul, to praise the Father
And adore!
