# 1.
It is a name we love here,
Whose praise we will sing;
The sweetest music it is,
Our ear listens to.

# 2.
It speaks of a Friend so good,
Who for us suffered and died;
It speaks of His precious blood,
Which is sufficient for all sin.

# 3.
It shows how our Father here
Ever supplies us with help,
And though our way may be so dark,
That name gives sunshine.

# 4.
It names One who loves us,
Who understands the least distress,
Who bears all sorrows with us
And dries every tear.

# 5.
It gives peace and security,
It imparts comfort and courage,
In "gentle breezes" it speaks
Of how "God is good."

# 6.
And Jesus is the precious name,
Which is so dear to us here;
On earth no one can comprehend
What this name is worth.

# 7.
Its fragrance that name shall spread
On the path of our pilgrimage here;
And if we walk through the valley of death,
It grants sweet comfort.

# 8.
When at last in the Father’s house
In glory we stand,
Of Jesus’ name with many saints
May we yet sing.
