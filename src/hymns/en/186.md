# 1.
Father, we have wandered astray
In darkness far from Thee;
But Thy grace has overflowed,
Met us far from the homeward path.
Tenderly Thou hast kissed us, O Father,
Garment, ring, and shoes Thou hast given,
Slain for us the fatted calf,
And prepared a feast.

# 2.
Clothed in garments of honour,
At Thy table we are permitted to sit,
Rejoicing in Thy fatherly love,
Seeing how Thou art glad also;
Glad, for many, mourned as dead,
Thou hast restored alive,
Glad, for Thou hast found again
Many who were lost.
