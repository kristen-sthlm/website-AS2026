# 1.
Thy nearness, Lord, we seek, Thy support,
To Thee we flee from strife, care, distress.
Thy sympathy our weakness meets here,
And in Thy shadow rest for the heart is.

# 2.
Thou knowest the cause of every sigh,
Thou knowest fully Thy own’s sorrow, all oppression.
All anxiety Thou dost still, and in due season dost grant
Us heavenly light, which we securely follow here.

# 3.
Thy love, Lord, giveth us strength to stand
For the interests Thou dost entrust to us.
We count upon the grace which Thou wilt give,
And wait quietly with thanksgiving.
