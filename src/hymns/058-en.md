# 1.
Hail to Thee, risen Jesus,
In heaven’s lofty hall,
Who once didst descend
To us in death’s vale.
Hail to Thee! On the throne of dominion,
With which Thou wast rewarded,
We now see the Son of Man
With glory crowned.

# 2.
Thou, who in deep humiliation
Didst here in the world abase Thyself,
And to eternally redeem us
Once didst hang upon the cross,
On the Father’s throne Thou sittest,
And awaitest only the day
When He shall say to Thee:
“My Son, take Thy kingdom.”

# 3.
And yet for Thine own
Thou art the same still;
On the throne Thou remainest
Yet Jesus, the Saviour.
By Thy wounds we know Thee,
God’s Lamb, who wast slain,
Our good Shepherd, who life
For the sheep hast given.

# 4.
Our Friend, if no other
There is in all the world;
Our Bridegroom, who loveth us
And ever shall love;
Our God, who dost not condemn,
Though all Thou of us knowest;
Our Lord, who dost not forget
Us in Thy glory.

# 5.
Hail to Thee! Thou art the same,
Who didst suffer need and shame,
And didst die for us upon the cross,
God’s spotless Lamb!
Hail to Thee! Thou art the same,
Who, exalted, dost reign there,
Whom our hearts await,
Our beloved Lord Jesus!
