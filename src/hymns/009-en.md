# 1.
God gave a promise in Eden:
Salvation through the woman’s seed;
Four thousand years passed,
And God’s Son Himself came down here.
The Lord has come, the Lord has come,
He has saved us from death,
Given Himself, bestowed life upon us,
Opened heaven for us.

# 2.
The first time He came to earth,
Angels were heard praising God,
That His Son had become man
To accomplish all His command.
The Lord has come, the Lord has come,
He has saved us from death,
Given Himself, bestowed life upon us,
Opened heaven for us.

# 3.
Jesus was poor on earth,
Heaven’s riches are our portion,
He bore sin’s penalty on the cross,
God’s righteousness we have received.
The Lord has come, the Lord has come,
He has saved us from death,
Given Himself, bestowed life upon us,
Opened heaven for us.

# 4.
Since He accomplished the work of salvation,
He went back to heaven again;
But He has given us this promise:
“Soon I will fetch you home.”
The Lord is coming, the Lord is coming!
Earth’s conflicts shall end;
The bride is adorned, the wedding is celebrated
In an eternal glory.

# 5.
Then the Bridegroom shall present
To Himself His purchased bride,
When no spot, no wrinkle is seen,
She is clothed in glorious attire.
The Lord is coming, the Lord is coming!
Earth’s conflicts shall end;
The bride is adorned, the wedding is celebrated
In an eternal glory.
