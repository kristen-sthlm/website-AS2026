# 1.
To look up and see Thee there,
O Lord, what joy!
To contemplate Thy great love,
Its breadth and depth and height!

# 2.
To confess the power of Thy blood,
Which flowed for us all,
And weary hearts to revive
With the thought of God’s Lamb!

# 3.
What joy it is to see the path
Which Thou didst so humbly tread,
To learn to walk in Thy grace
And be led by Thy gaze.

# 4.
To look forward to that day,
When Thou Thyself shalt appear,
When we Thy lovely countenance
With unclouded gaze shall see!
