# 1.
Hast thou no room for Jesus,
Who His life for thee laid down?
He now knocks upon thy heart:
Dear soul, open unto Me!

# C.
Room for the Lord of glory,
O, open, bid Him in!
Blessedness He with Him bringeth,
Yea, His glory shall be thine.

# 2.
Room for pleasures, room for the world,
Yea, for all vanity,
But no room for Jesus only,
He who for thee death endured?

# 3.
Hast thou no time for Jesus,
When in grace He calleth thee?
Woe to thee, for He perchance
No more will let Himself be heard!

# 4.
Room and time now give to Jesus,
Soon the day of grace is past.
If thou thrustest grace from thee,
O, how shall thy end then be?
