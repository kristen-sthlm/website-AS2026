# 1.
There is a heart that yearns for thee,
Thou poor captive in sin’s bonds.
There is an eye that eagerly scans
Ever for the child in a distant land.

# 2.
There is an ear that bends down low
And hears the poor one who seeks help;
There is a tongue that speaks sweetly
To the wretched one who is sorrowful.

# 3.
There is a fountain for thee who thirsts,
For thee who hungers, there is bread;
There is a refuge, so safe and secure,
For thee who art pursued by sin and death.

# 4.
O, come to Jesus, and thou shalt find
What thou needest for salvation,
For Jesus, Jesus, yea, He alone
Is sufficient for all in eternity.
