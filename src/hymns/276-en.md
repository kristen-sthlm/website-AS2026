# 1.
O Jesus, our Lord, remain ever near us,
And tend us and bear us, as always Thou hast done!
Yea, amen, Thy faithfulness shall bring us onward.
Praise, thanksgiving, and glory,
Praise, thanksgiving, and glory,
Praise, thanksgiving, and glory
To God’s holy Lamb!
