# 1.
Abba, Father, Thou in love
Hast embraced us in Thy bosom.
Thy children here are gathered
In the name of our Lord Jesus.
Thou Thyself hast chosen us as sons
In Thy great fatherly love,
Taught us to cry, "Abba, Father,"
Brought us where Thou Thyself dost dwell.

# 2.
God our Father, we Thee praise,
Thou hast given Thy Son,
And in Him all Thy love
Now hath been made manifest.
Thy righteousness He hath proclaimed,
All Thy wisdom, power, and grace;
As Father He hath declared Thee,
And announced to us Thy counsel.

# 3.
Abba, Father, we adore Thee,
While angelic hosts
Now in us behold Thy wisdom,
Learn what Thy love is.
Soon gathered forever,
We shall praise in heaven’s haven
Abba’s love, known in Jesus,
And His precious Father-name.
