# 1.
Hail to the Lord’s Anointed,
The great Son of King David,
Who shall accomplish
God’s counsel upon His throne!
Oppression shall He crush,
The captives bring forth,
From sins deliver
His people of Jacob’s stock.

# 2.
God’s counsel in the heights
The heavens still conceal
From our eyes
The long-expected One.
But He, whose gentle hands
Blessed at last,
The same shall return
And richly fill every lack.

# 3.
He shall come like the rain
And water all the earth,
The dry wilderness
Shall blossom as the lily.
From the pure fountain of life
Shall peace for thousands
Like streams of water flow
Over hills and valleys.

# 4.
Behold, kings fall down
With gold and incense!
Behold, the heathen worship
In the temple, adoring!
And over seas and shores
They come to God’s house
From all the lands of the earth
To behold the Lord’s light.

# 5.
His kingdom shall remain forever,
His praise is new each day.
All render Him homage
And do His good pleasure.
No stream of time shall reverse
His covenant of peace,
Whose name without end
Is and remains Love.
