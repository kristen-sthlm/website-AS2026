# 1.
All the Lord’s ways are
Goodness, truth, faithfulness.
Even where we know nothing,
He wills and knows our best.
He who has counted our days
Before any had come,
He who in all distress and danger
Bore us on strong arms,
Oh, He will help henceforth also,
As He hitherto has helped.
Oh, He will help henceforth also,
As He hitherto has helped.

# 2.
Should we cast a glance backward
On the years that have fled, behold,
The same love manifested
In both weal and woe.
The same love, the same faithfulness,
Shown in Jesus Christ;
The same love, grace, and faithfulness,
Despite our unfaithfulness and lack;
And yesterday, today, forever
He the same assuredly is.
And yesterday, today, forever
He the same assuredly is.

# 3.
The same love, when He gave us
What we most of all desired,
As when He took from us
What above all was dear to us;
When in grace He removed
What stood in our way,
As when He gently reached to us
From His hand a good gift,
Yes, in all we see constantly
The same grace and patience.
Yes, in all we see constantly
The same grace and patience.

# 4.
Should we look forward, the same love
Shall henceforth follow us,
Bear us on shepherd-arms
And preserve us from falling.
Not our watchfulness, our faithfulness
Suffices, only His;
In Christ’s power alone
Every victory for us was won.
|: He in us, and we in Him,
Greater bliss was never found. :|
