# 1.
Father, Almighty,
Praise be unto Thee!
Thy love shall be praised
Eternally.
Long ere all the earth
Was overwhelmed in sin’s flood,
Long ere Adam’s fall,
Thou didst choose me.

# 2.
Jesus, Redeemer!
Thou didst die for me,
Great Deliverer,
Thou hast set me free.
Thy hand Thou didst lay on me,
Took me out of Satan’s power,
Sweetly to my heart didst say:
“I love thee.”

# 3.
Heaven’s Highly Praised,
Father so dear!
A portion of Thy fulness now
He bestows on us.
All things Thou hast done well,
Our soul can now rejoice,
Jesus, Immanuel,
Thou art with us.

# 4.
Jesus, God’s Beloved,
Thou didst give Thyself,
The Father’s Chosen One
From eternal ages.
Unspeakable bliss,
Heaven’s glory,
In all eternity
We have in Thee.
