# 1.
Behold, Jesus is a comforting name,
And in all distress our sure haven;
It reveals the Father’s grace,
His wondrous counsel of love.

# 2.
God’s only Son, O gracious Jesus,
Who here has borne all our guilt,
Our distress Thou seest and canst help,
Thou, who art both God and man.

# 3.
Thyself art our righteousness,
Our help, our hope in eternity;
He who in Thy name has all his comfort,
Is delivered from every foe.

# 4.
Praise be to Thee for the word of life,
For the Spirit’s gift, the table of grace!
Increase in us Thy grace and peace,
Thou, who hast given us eternal life!
