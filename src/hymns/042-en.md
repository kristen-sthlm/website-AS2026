# 1.
Not the blood of offerings, which flowed
Upon the Jewish altar stone,
Was sufficient (our conscience knows it)
To cleanse the soul.

# 2.
No, Christ Himself, God’s Lamb,
Has taken away our sin.
One single offering He brought forth;
His blood was sufficient.

# 3.
Our soul looks back
Upon the burden, which He bore.
Once He died, He dies no more,
Upon the tree of the cross it was.

# 4.
We look forward with joy,
For He, who has suffered,
Shall descend from heaven’s height
With wondrous salvation.

# 5.
May eternal thanks and praise
And glory be borne forth
To Him, who stands in the midst of the throne,
The slain Lamb of God!
