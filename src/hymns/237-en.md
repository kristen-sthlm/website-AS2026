# 1.
Lord, now we all gather
Around Thy precious word of grace;
Let it fall upon our heart
Like rain on thirsty ground!
Keep us all still before Thee,
Let nothing lead us astray;
Lord, let us not waste
The hour of grace Thou hast appointed!

# 2.
Teach us faithfully to take heed
Of the word given to us.
Lead us in Thy clear light,
Which is now revealed.
Open Thou the ear of our heart,
That we may hear the Spirit’s voice;
For He will faithfully lead us
Into all truth in Thy word.
