# 1.
Behold the Lamb, crowned in glory,
With honour and all power!
He shall be praised eternally
For the work He has accomplished.

# 2.
He fills the throne, highly exalted,
With righteousness He sits there.
He is the joy of the redeemed,
With the Father chosen, beloved.

# 3.
Though He is on the Father’s throne,
Our song He does not despise,
The weakest, the faintest tone
Reaches His ear on high.

# 4.
With one accord we bring our song
To the glory of His name,
To Him who is upon the throne,
The slain Lamb of God.

# 5.
He, who was here despised by men
And patiently endured scorn,
Now bears the crown of glory
In majesty and power.
