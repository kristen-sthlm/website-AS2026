# 1.
To the land of glory again
We have seen Thee, Jesus, go;
But we on this earth yet
As strangers must remain.
In faith and hope on the desert path
We walk here and await Thee.

# 2.
And soon Thou wilt return,
Our Jesus, back here
To fetch all Thy saints;
May we then ever watch!
No hour, no day now do we know,
But constantly may we await Thee.

# 3.
How blessed to meet Thee then
With the saints on high,
To go with them into the joy
And enjoy the marriage gladness,
And with Thee, Lord, eternally to be;
In this hope we rejoice!
