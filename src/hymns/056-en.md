# 1.
Crucified in weakness, but raised in power,
Thou didst ascend to heaven’s heights,
There Thou reignest, Jesus, God’s only-begotten Son,
God’s source of eternal joys.

# 2.
And heaven’s hosts in worship behold
The glorious victory Thou hast won,
And man is saved from death and from woe,
For God a propitiation hath found.

# 3.
On high in the bliss and rest of the Godhead
Thou wilt our hearts introduce,
Thy presence, O Lord, now our home doth constitute,
For Thee, Thee alone we belong.

# 4.
A holy right now unto us doth pertain,
Entirely delivered we may be,
No longer in Adam, in Thee we now stand,
An offering, priestly company.

# 5.
The glorious testimony written indeed is
By the Spirit’s divine pen.
Highly exalted Jesus, our heart then learns
Thy Father ever better to know.
