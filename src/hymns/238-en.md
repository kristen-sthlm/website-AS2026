# 1.
As a banner God has given us His truth,
Here among the enemy we praise it,
The Hero of Judah has become our chieftain,
He who has redeemed us and still redeems us.

# 2.
Jesus at Golgotha has won the victory,
The powers of the abyss He trod down.
Eternal redemption from death He has found,
When for His own He bled and suffered.

# 3.
Let us then not lose heart in the battle,
The word of the cross has not lost its power.
Christ has won for us the eternal peace,
The kingdom shall the Father surely give us.

# 4.
May we deny ourselves for Christ,
Work diligently for the Master here;
For God’s people there remains a rest,
Eternal the joy that awaits us there!
