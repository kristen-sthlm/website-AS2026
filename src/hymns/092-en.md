# 1.
Praise the Lord, all saints!
Joyfully before Him we bow down
And call upon His precious name,
Who is risen!

# 2.
Jesus, Thou hast become our all,
Thou, who fought the sore conflict,
Won for us the deep peace,
Which we here enjoy.

# 3.
God in Thee has found His rest,
And the precious blood that flowed
Has won for us reconciliation;
To Thee be eternal praise!

# 4.
Lord, keep us by Thy side,
Help us ever to await Thee,
Joyfully in hope to suffer with Thee
During our waiting here!

# 5.
Thou, our life, shalt be manifested,
We shall see Thee and be glorified,
As Thy bride with joy be received
Into Thy Father’s house.
