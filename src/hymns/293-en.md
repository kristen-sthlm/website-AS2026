# 1.
Blessed hope, Thou comest again,
Leaving us not always here.
O what joy, when Thou dost permit us
To come there where Thou Thyself art!
All our longing then is stilled,
When we go to meet Thee,
When we go to meet Thee.

# 2.
Rest shall Thy beloved have,
When united with Thee there;
No sorrow shall the heart consume,
No tear shall flow more,
When we see Thee, our Jesus,
Face to face,
Face to face.

# 3.
The harps of the heavenly host resound
There in song of the redeemed throng,
To Thee, Lamb of God, to bring honour,
Who once died upon the cross,
To reconcile us with God,
And to win Thyself a bride,
And to win Thyself a bride.

# 4.
Only “a little while” it remains,
Then, O Lord, Thou comest,
And until then Thou givest strength
To follow Thee, to serve now.
May we bear the cross then,
Till we receive the crown of life!
Till we receive the crown of life!
