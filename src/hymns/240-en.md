# 1.
To Thee, O Jesus, I would flee,
Thou knowest me;
To Thee, to Thee each morning new,
And Thee alone;
To Thee, when day to me doth smile,
And all is bright,
To Thee, when sun doth sink away,
And night draws nigh.

# 2.
To Thee, O Jesus, first and last,
With all my need;
Thy love doth fill all lack
In overflow;
To Thee, my precious Saviour,
Yea, Thee alone;
Thou, who hast borne all my woe,
Thou lovest me.

# 3.
Thy way, O Lord, teach me to walk,
And not mine own,
However narrow be the path,
If it be Thine.
Yea, if it be Thine, then it is good,
O precious Lord;
Thy love shall strengthen my courage,
Thyself art near.
