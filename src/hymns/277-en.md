# 1.
It is not with uncertain steps
We walk the desert path.
A well-known voice has called us
To eternal joy with Him.

# 2.
It is His voice who alone has gone
A trackless way on earth
And opened the way up to God
For us who went astray.

# 3.
He does not ask us to go alone
In the vast and wide desert,
But for us home in boundless grace,
We hasten where He dwells.

# 4.
Behold, the door of heaven is now open,
Where the radiance shines forth
And lights the path He walked
To glory with God.

# 5.
In patience we must go forward,
Tested in faith
And draw near to that warm love,
Which gives the heart rest.
