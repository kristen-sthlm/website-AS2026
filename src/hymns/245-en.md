# 1.
How mighty and how patient
Is our Defender!
On the Father’s throne He sits,
Our Advocate.
Surrounded here by weakness,
By need and temptation,
To Him we turn ourselves,
That He may give us strength.

# 2.
And if we stumble, fall,
In this land of sin,
He Himself uplifts our soul
With gentle, divine hand.
Then in the Father’s ear
Is heard the voice of Jesus Christ,
And the sorrowing child receives
Forgiveness and comfort.

# 3.
But long before we feel
Our sin’s dreadfulness,
Jesus has faithfully prayed
For our restoration.
Thy precious grace we praise,
Our heart’s praise we give Thee,
In service unwearying,
Our Advocate!
