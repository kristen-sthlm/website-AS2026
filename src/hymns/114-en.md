# 1.
Praise the Lord! He is love,
He never forsakes us.
Though sorrow and reproach may meet us,
Lovingly He ever looks upon us.
Lovingly He ever looks upon us.

# 2.
Praise the Lord! That He came to earth
To bring us peace,
Fought for us the grievous conflict,
Bore on the cross the judgment of sin.
Bore on the cross the judgment of sin.

# 3.
Praise the Lord! Good and gracious,
Full of patience He is.
Though the way may seem long and perilous,
He leads us even there.
He leads us even there.

# 4.
Praise the Lord! In distress
He ever is and remains our Friend;
He loved us unto death
And in life still loves.
And in life still loves.

# 5.
Praise the Father! In love
He gives us an inheritance with the Son,
Teaches us to count the reproach of Christ
|: As the greatest honour here. :|
