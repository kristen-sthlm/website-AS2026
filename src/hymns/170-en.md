# 1.
On God’s Lamb our faith now rests,
Without limit His love is.
All our sins have been
In His blood washed away here.

# 2.
Sweet rest and peace have filled us,
Praise that cannot be uttered.
God is satisfied with Jesus,
And we rejoice as He does.

# 3.
Conscience no longer condemns us,
For His own precious blood
Has cleansed, washed us, and set us
Before God with holy boldness.

# 4.
Ever filled with His peace
We go forward despite toils here,
Until an eternal rest surrounds us
In the Lamb’s dwelling there.
