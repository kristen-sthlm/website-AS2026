# 1.
Lord, Thy countenance Thou dost not hide from me,
Though not clearly through the clouds I see Thee.

# C.
O Thou my faithful, precious Redeemer,
Comest Thou not soon? Yea, Thou comest soon.

# 2.
Through Thy saving grace I have become
Happy to be a stranger on earth.

# 3.
Keep Thou my heart only fast to the one thing,
Content to possess Thee, Lord, alone.
