# 1.
Thy love higher than all knowledge is,
Yet would our heart, beloved Lord, indeed,
Thy love comprehend in its depth and height,
Its breadth and length, its power, its blissful joy,
More and more.

# 2.
Thy love surpasses all praise assuredly;
Yet would we sing, Lord Christ,
That love which so wholly gave itself
And brought us through grace infinite
So near to God.

# 3.
O fill us, Lord, with Thy love so,
That only for Thee our heart may burn;
On Thee, Thou Fountain of life, ever trust,
And not from other fountains here depend,
But only on Thee.

# 4.
And when we Thee, our Lord Jesus, see,
When Thou for us Thy glory shalt display,
Thy love then in all its depth and height,
Its breadth and length and power in eternal joy
We praise may.
