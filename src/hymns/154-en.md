# 1.
Hear, O soul, a voice from on high
Speaks now of grace for thee!
Hearken, believe, take part in the joy,
Boundless grace here is offered thee!
Yea, for thee, yea, for thee!
Boundless grace here is offered thee.
Yea, for thee, yea, for thee!
Boundless grace here is offered thee.

# 2.
Behold God’s Son, His sorrow and pain,
See how He Himself hath offered Himself!
Into death His heart was broken,
Dear soul, it was for thee!
Yea, for thee, yea, for thee!
Dear soul, it was for thee!
Yea, for thee, yea, for thee!
Dear soul, it was for thee!

# 3.
Christ hath brought full atonement,
Won salvation also for thee.
Behold, in heaven there is a dwelling,
Where all bliss awaits thee!
Awaits thee, awaits thee!
Where all bliss awaits thee.
Awaits thee, awaits thee!
Where all bliss awaits thee.

# 4.
We above shall praise
Jesus, who hath offered Himself.
There shall He in glory show
What He hath done for thee and me.
Yea, for thee and for me!
What He hath done for thee and me.
Yea, for thee and for me!
What He hath done for thee and me.
