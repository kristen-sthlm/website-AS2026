# 1.
When peace like a river overflows my path,
When waves of sorrow break over me,
Whatever my lot, I can yet say:
It is well, it is well with my soul.

# C.
It is well with my soul;
It is well, it is well with my soul.

# 2.
Though the enemy afflicts, though I bear the cross,
I am secure in my Saviour,
For He has done all for my eternal well-being
And has shed His blood for my soul.

# 3.
My debt-bond (so the Lord assures me indeed),
My debt-bond of sins and of lack,
Is nailed to the cross, no more am I a slave!
Praise the Lord, my ransomed soul!

# 4.
O Lord, I long to see what I believe,
To see Thee in Thy great glory!
Yea, soon Thou shalt come; then all shall be well with me,
For in grace Thou hast redeemed my soul!
