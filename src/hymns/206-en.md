# 1.
All my need my Father knows,
Therein I rest,
And what is best, He sends me
In His grace each day.
Well known is the hand that orders
All so well for me.
Joy and sorrow, in all together
Love is manifest.

# 2.
Though every earthly hope should sink,
Tears flow here,
Yet He Himself my highest bliss,
All in all is He.
May I more on Him rely,
Walk faithfully here
In the calm, blessed certainty:
The Father loves me.
