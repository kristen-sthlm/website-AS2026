# 1.
O thou that art gone astray, remain no longer afar
Upon the path of ruin, ah, tarry not!
Consider that God may soon cut short thy time,
Consider He hath called thee, yet received no answer.

# 2.
How long wilt thou thus defy His love?
How long shall He depart unheard from thee?
How long shall He endure thy scorn?
Shall not His word once reach thy heart?

# 3.
Perchance today is the last occasion
That thou shalt hear the glad tidings of grace;
Each moment enters into eternity;
How many blessed, God alone doth know.

# 4.
Still is the way free to return,
The door to the Father’s house stands open.
In heaven there is joy when the sinner
Suffers himself to be brought home, like the lost sheep found again.
