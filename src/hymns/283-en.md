# 1.
This world is a wilderness wide,
Without gain is all that it has.
We have nothing to lose therein,
Not a thought to remain there.

# 2.
The Lord Himself has gone the way before;
Now no fear dwells with us any more,
For our path He has clearly marked out;
It is safe as His love is great.

# 3.
Here in the desert there is but one way,
Where His precious footsteps now are,
And we follow with eager steps
To the place where His crown He bears.

# 4.
For the path which our Saviour has trod
Leads up to His Father and God.
There on the throne His place He has taken,
And His power is our support on the way.

# 5.
And with Him our rest shall be
At His side in glory, there
All His love shall fill us with peace
And with joy, which His presence imparts.

# 6.
In His love we have found a treasure,
Which has made us pilgrims here.
But above, yet known, we soon
Shall see Him as He is.

# 7.
Thee we await, our beloved Saviour,
Until Thou dost gather in clouds those
Whom Thine eye has guided here
To Thyself in Thy heavenly home.
