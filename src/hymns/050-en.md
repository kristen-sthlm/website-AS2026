# 1.
Our Lord Jesus has gone
With victory from His grave,
Not death shall be our lot,
He gave life to us.

# C.
The Lord, who died upon the cross,
From death’s domain took captives.

# 2.
Who can accuse us?
O hear, what joyful tidings:
In Jesus’ death has vanished
All that has separated us from God.

# 3.
Who then can accuse
Him whom God has justified,
And who can condemn
Him who has believed Christ’s work?

# 4.
Now Christ has given ransom,
He has completed His work.
He has prepared help for us
And laid the enemy low.
