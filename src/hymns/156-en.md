# 1.
O come to the Lord Jesus,
Fall down before His foot!
For thee He has given Himself,
For thee on the cross suffered.

# C.
The message of grace resounds over the plain;
Hear, O soul, for it kindly calls thee:
Come to Jesus! Come to Jesus!

# 2.
Today Jesus will save
From every sin and sorrow;
He alone can help,
He is our strong tower.

# 3.
For the whole wide world
God’s arms stand open.
He calls: Come all,
Today grace is to be had!

# 4.
O love without bounds
For every slave of sin!
God so loved the world
That He gave His Son.
