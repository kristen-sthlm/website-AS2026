# 1.
Jesus dear, be near to me,
And teach my heart the path of life;
When day dawns, when night descends,
Jesus dear, be near to me!

# 2.
Keep Thou me close to Thee,
And make Thy path smooth for my foot.
In joy and in sorrow alike,
Keep Thou me close to Thee!
