# 1.
Thy love, O Father, mighty, free,
Has sought and found the captives here,
And through the precious work of Jesus,
Thou dost bestow life and liberty upon them.

# 2.
In the house of bondage Thou didst see us,
Tightly bound in sin’s chains;
Though far from Thee, O our God,
We were reached by Thy saving hand.

# 3.
The grace which Thine only Son gave,
As ransom for our guilt,
Has set us free and now teaches us
To love Thee, our gracious Father.

# 4.
And Thou hast given us the Son,
To be brought home, our God, to Thee.
As “sons” and “brethren” now,
We follow in the path of His footsteps.

# 5.
There above, where Thy love’s sun
Shines brightly in righteousness,
There through Jesus’ blood we see
Our title to eternal glory.

# 6.
Partakers of Thy nature,
Even now, though we walk in the wilderness,
With hearts fixed upon Thy Son,
We share in Thy love’s joy.

# 7.
Thou art love, O keep us then
So near, yea so near to Thee,
That we ourselves may disappear,
And Christ be manifested in us.
