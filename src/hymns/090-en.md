# 1.
Let us sing, eternally sing,
Of our Lord’s faithfulness!
No other fountain of comfort,
In all sorrow our heart knows.
He has loved us in death,
Unto the bitter death of the cross,
And in His death redeemed
Us from eternal distress.

# 2.
He has redeemed us to God
With His blood and bought us free,
That we should praise Him,
And eternally be His own;
That we should serve Him
In righteousness and peace,
Then with Him eternally rest
After well-finished strife.

# 3.
Over height and depth He for us
Is faithful, again and again;
Why should we be anxious about the way,
When we know it bears home!
Whether it bears through green meadows
Or through the desert’s sand,
We may alike securely go forward,
The Father has us in His hand.

# 4.
He is faithful, tender, and patient,
Eternally keeps those who believe.
May then His word and love
Richly in our heart dwell!
Gladly we should then constantly
Sing in a higher strain,
Making melody in our heart
To our Father’s praise and glory.
