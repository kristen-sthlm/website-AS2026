# 1.
In Christ Jesus we have received life,
And all He has done well;
How rich, how glorious is our portion,
How fair our inheritance!

# 2.
We know the Father loves us,
And long to apprehend more
His love, which has “wholly apprehended” us,
And gives us daily strength.

# 3.
We know we shall behold God
In glory soon,
And raise the sound of the song of victory,
And see His glory clearly.

# 4.
Our Father! Teach our hearts only
To know Thee better,
To praise more for the grace we have received,
And walk in Thy path.

# 5.
To the Son Thou didst choose a bride,
In Him she is rich;
His fair name, His robe we bear,
Soon we shall be like Him.

# 6.
With joy shall she then praise
Her Bridegroom’s faithfulness,
With Him share the throne of glory,
And eternal glory.

# 7.
Praise be to Thy mercy,
That Thou hast ordained it thus,
And praise be to Thee for ever,
That we have seen it!
