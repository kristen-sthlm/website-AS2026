# 1.
O God, Thy Son’s atoning work,
Thy grace’s perfection,
Hath made that we may approach Thee
In full righteousness.

# 2.
As Thine own beloved children
We rejoice before Thee,
Since Jesus, Thine elect,
Himself offered on the cross.

# 3.
Sweet the confidence, peace, and joy
Thou dost impart to our heart;
Our spirit hath its refuge
In the light, where Thou art.

# 4.
There in the rich grace of Thy purpose
The sons Thou hast appointed,
May share as joint-heirs
All Thou hast given to the Son.
