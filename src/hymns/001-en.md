# 1.
Holy, holy, holy,
Lord God Almighty!
When the new day dawns, our song of praise to Thee ascends.
Holy, holy, holy, gracious and mighty,
Thee we worship, God and Father ours.

# 2.
Holy, holy, holy, sing all saints,
Cast down their golden crowns before Thy glory.
Before Thee cherubim and seraphim fall.
Thou wast, and art, and shalt be in eternity.

# 3.
Holy, holy, holy, high and inaccessible
Is Thy brightness clear, which no sinful eye beholds.
Eternal is Thy grace, Thy love imperishable.
All-good, unto the children of dust Thou lookest down.

# 4.
Holy, holy, holy, Lord God Almighty!
Over heaven, earth, and seas Thy reign extends.
Holy, holy, holy, gracious and mighty,
Thee we worship, God and Father ours.
