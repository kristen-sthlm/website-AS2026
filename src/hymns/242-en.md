# 1.
God is faithful, O my soul,
Rejoice, for He never fails thee!
God is faithful, all is well,
His grace from thee does not depart.
Though all here may change,
The Lord remains the same
Throughout all the shifts of time.

# 2.
God is faithful, He cannot
In anything retract His word.
What He has promised, He keeps,
On His promises I may build.
Mountains and hills may fall,
Earth and heaven wholly pass away,
The Lord’s word shall yet remain.

# 3.
God is faithful, His covenant
He keeps in all ages.
Never does the foundation of the word fail,
It stands firm in all conflicts.
The Lord’s grace and faithfulness
Endure into all eternity.
Therefore I am calm and blessed.

# 4.
God is faithful, and I may
Eternally trust in that word.
However it may go with me,
Nothing shall sever me from God.
With His faithful Father’s hand
He leads me to the land of peace,
Where I shall sing His praise.
