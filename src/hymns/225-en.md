# 1.
Our God is light. As here we walk
In the wilderness we have Thee,
O Jesus, Thou who hast prepared
A path for every child.

# 2.
At every step we ever see
How secure Thy guidance is,
In faithful, patient love, which
Never fails us here.

# 3.
O Lord, Thou dost not weary,
Though we may grow weary,
In the right moment Thou givest
A word for the heart’s peace.

# 4.
Thou hast made death’s bitter waters
Sweetness for our good,
And shown us a refuge
With refreshment for our soul.

# 5.
For every day there is manna enough,
And the well flows here.
Behold, Eshcol’s grapes testify of
The land where bliss is.
