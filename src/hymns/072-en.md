# 1.
Praise be to Thee, O Jesus Christ,
Who took my guilt upon Thee;
Yea, all my judgment, my sin and lack,
And bore the punishment for me!
A sacrifice lamb, Thou wast led forth;
Now there is no judgment for me.

# 2.
The bitter cup of judgment and death
Was drained by Thee;
Thy love, Jesus, moved Thee thereto,
Now it is empty for me;
A cup of rich blessing
Is now held out to me.

# 3.
For me, O Jesus Christ, Thou didst die,
And I have died with Thee;
From the grave Thou didst take me with Thee,
Now Thou livest in me,
And soon in Thy glory
I shall live with Thee.
