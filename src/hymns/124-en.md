# 1.
The pearl Christ has found,
Which was His soul’s desire,
For which He sacrificed all
And died on the cross here.

# 2.
He sought and with joy found
His pearl, yes, His bride;
All that He possessed He gave up
And purchased her for God.

# 3.
A pearl rare, precious, fair
She was in His eyes;
She was redeemed with His blood,
His beauty now she has.

# 4.
His beloved for evermore
He bears upon His heart
And cleanses in the washing of water by the word
On the wilderness path here.

# 5.
Soon without spot or wrinkle
He will present to Himself
His costly bride in snow-white attire,
Pure and blameless.

# 6.
Our Lord, Thee we now praise,
Teach us to esteem more
Thy love for the assembly,
Which Thou didst dearly purchase here.
