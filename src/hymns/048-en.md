# 1.
The redemption is won:
God’s Son from eternity,
By the Father begotten
For the world’s salvation.
He is the morning star,
Who can shine and shelter
In darkness and in anguish.

# 2.
He has become man;
A promised time of grace
Has dawned over the earth
With light and hope and peace.
Now death’s power is crushed,
And sin’s bond is loosed,
And heaven’s gate is opened.

# 3.
O Christ, Thee we praise,
Our precious Saviour,
That Thou hast deigned to show us
Such rich blessing.
To Thee our heart longs,
From the desert’s strife and straitness,
To Thee in glory.

# 4.
Thy Father alone
Has given all into Thy hand.
Thou didst die to eternally unite
God’s children from every land,
And thus to acquire for Thyself
A people, who joyfully shall inherit
The kingdom Thou hast prepared.
