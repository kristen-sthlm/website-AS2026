# 1.
Holy, holy Lord God!
Earth and heavens at Thy command
Out of darkness’ secrecy
Were brought forth in glory;
Fair, perfect, pure and good
Creation before Thee stood,
Singing with sweet sound:
Holy, holy Lord God!

# 2.
Holy, holy Lord God!
Though in sin against Thy commands
Man has corrupted himself,
Thou yet remainest holy,
And in Thy mercy
Thy glory shines most brightly;
Therefore the bride of the Lamb sings:
Holy, holy Lord God!

# 3.
Holy, holy Lord God!
Creation in festal array
Shall at last be adorned
And raise the song of victory.
All the redeemed gathering
Shall sing throughout eternity
To the sound of the golden harps:
Holy, holy Lord God!
