# 1.
Wait only, wait only!
All shall be made good through the Lord’s goodness:
The tear-sown seed shall be a joyful harvest,
The prayer of faith shall surely be heard.
Ah, for a heart established in faith,
What is this time’s brief sorrow?
Wait only, wait only!

# 2.
Conflict and strife, conflict and strife
Shall soon be exchanged for victory’s sweet peace.
What pressed thee most here
Shall from the harp draw forth there
The purest and highest note of praise,
When at last thou standest glorified before the throne,
Wait only, wait only!

# 3.
Joyfully onward, joyfully onward!
Soon shall all thy weeping be stilled forever.
A few steps more, and then
Thou shalt behold Jesus.
Home in heaven’s blissful heights
Soon thou shalt share thy Lord’s joys,
Wait only, wait only!
