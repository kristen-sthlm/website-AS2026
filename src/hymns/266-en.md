# 1.
In this desolate world
I see the footsteps of Jesus.
All distress He relieves on my journey
And gives me abundant consolation.
Behold, up to the Father there
My heart He leads.
All the world is empty and desolate,
It cannot entice me.

# 2.
My rest is Jesus Christ,
I will follow Him.
My soul knows no sorrow or lack,
For I belong to Him.
Though the way appears hard, yet
The eye of faith reaches the goal.
All trial is turned to my good,
And the soul receives salvation.

# 3.
Yes, happy, glad, and free
I walk a heavenly path,
My soul has but one melody,
O God, it is for Thee!
To Thee I flee in distress,
When the soul is cast down.
Thy rod and staff are my support,
Which bring comfort to me.

# 4.
O what grace so great
To know Thee thus here,
To trace the way where Thou dost dwell
And go onward, upward!
Soon I shall behold Thee,
O great Saviour!
In Thee I gain all victory
And reach Thy glory.
