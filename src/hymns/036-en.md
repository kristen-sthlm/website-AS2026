# 1.
Our Lord Jesus, gracious and good,
This meal Thou hast given us;
It speaks of Thy body, Thy blood,
And that Thou hast suffered death.
Now on God’s throne Thou art exalted
And lovest Thy gathering here.

# 2.
In this bread we see Thy body,
Which Thou hast given as an offering.
Thou hast taken away sin and distance,
Thou hast become our peace, our life.
Thy death and Thy resurrection
Have perfected us before God.

# 3.
And the cup testifies of Thy blood,
It speaks of God’s love;
Therein we receive a holy boldness
To dwell in God’s presence.
A new covenant Thou hast given us,
Wherein God’s law of the heart is seen.
