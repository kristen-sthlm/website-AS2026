# 1.
Though still by the world rejected Thou art,
Despised is Thy blood,
We rejoice in the hope that the day is near,
Our gracious Saviour good,
When the heavens shall declare Thy glory,
And jubilant hosts Thy praise shall bear home
From hearts filled with Thy love so great.

# 2.
And though here below in the wilderness we go,
Amid mist and dust,
With Thee yet in life’s domain we stand
In purity and light.
With us Thou wilt share the joy Thou hast won;
For eternally Thou lovest the treasure Thou hast found
In the field and hidden, while away Thou art.

# 3.
And soon for Thyself shalt Thou present fair
Thy costly-purchased bride,
The precious pearl, Thy labour’s reward,
Given Thee by God;
With praise and adoration then the heavens shall be filled,
When Christ our life as King shall be hailed,
And all things by God unto Him subjected are.
