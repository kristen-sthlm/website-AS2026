# 1.
Now we part, yet ever remain
We in Christ always one.
May we the Lord faithfully follow
On the path He has prepared for us,
In communion with our Father
And His Son, our beloved Lord,
And with all who in the light
Walk here to His glory!

# 2.
Thus in the Spirit may we walk
Continually in God’s truth-light,
Until we one day see each other
In our Father’s blessed house.
Shining in Christ’s image,
Praising His love there,
We shall at home with the Lord,
Never, never part again.
