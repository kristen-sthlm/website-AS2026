# 1.
How sweet Thy name, O Jesus, is
To him who believes in Thee!
It gives comfort in sorrow and care
And grants me health.

# 2.
To the wounded soul it bears balm
And calms the anxious breast,
The hungry receives manna there,
The weary rest and solace.

# 3.
O name, my rock, where I dwell,
My shield, my hiding place, Thou!
My treasure, of which with great joy
I already now partake.

# 4.
My Lord, Thou who art for me
My God, my Saviour,
My help, my Priest, my beloved Shepherd,
And my Defender!

# 5.
I will praise Thy precious name
With every breath
Now and hereafter in Thy embrace
In the day of eternity.
