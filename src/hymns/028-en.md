# 1.
"Man of Sorrows!" O what a name
For the Son of God, who went here before!
What a Saviour, God's Lamb!
Hallelujah, He is worthy!

# 2.
Here He stood in our place
And bought us with His blood.
As our Saviour He died.
Hallelujah, He is worthy!

# 3.
We bore the burden of sin,
The Lamb without blemish He was.
What a Saviour we have!
Hallelujah, He is worthy!

# 4.
He bore the cross's shame and woe,
The work there He perfected.
The Saviour we see exalted.
Hallelujah, He is worthy!

# 5.
Lord He is on the throne,
And we worship Him there.
What a Saviour He is!
Hallelujah, He is worthy!
