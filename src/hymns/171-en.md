# 1.
My God, when I consider
What Thou hast done for me,
What grace Thou ever grantest,
I receive all from Thee!
Then I become heartily glad,
Then praise is drawn forth:
Have thanks, my gracious Father!
Have thanks, God’s precious Lamb!

# 2.
Thou hast tasted death for me,
Thou hast borne my sins,
And over me Thou hast watched
In all my days.
What would have become of me
Hadst Thou not acted thus?
Hadst Thou not granted me grace,
I must have gone to death.

# 3.
Now I am already blessed
And go to heaven.
I enjoy unnumbered joys
In God, my best Friend.
He Himself shall preserve me,
His grace is ever new,
And if any danger threatens,
To Him I may flee.

# 4.
O, should I then grieve,
Who have such a God?
Nay, rather may I begin
To raise the sound of praise.
Yea, the Lord will I praise;
His grace and faithfulness
Shall be my new song
Throughout all eternity.
