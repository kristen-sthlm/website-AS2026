# 1.
Now all is ready! Come!
Come to my meal, come!
Come, rich and poor, old, young,
To be richly filled! Come!

# 2.
Now all is ready! Come!
Let nothing hinder you,
Neither the yoke of oxen, nor house and home,
God calls you to Himself!

# 3.
Now all is ready! Come!
O, what joyful tidings
From Jesus, who is in heaven
In glory with God!

# 4.
Now all is ready! Come!
See, heaven is opened,
God’s love you may enjoy,
For Christ died for you.

# 5.
Now all is ready! Come!
Every hindrance is gone.
His love God to men
In Christ has shown here.

# 6.
Now all is ready! Come!
Before the door is shut, O come!
O sinner, your Saviour
Welcomes you, O come!
