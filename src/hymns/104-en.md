# 1.
Our Father, in Thy beloved Son
Thy love has reached us;
Since He was exalted on Thy throne
An eternal day has dawned.

# 2.
In Him, who died and now lives,
We live before Thee.
And together we will praise highly
Thy immeasurable grace.

# 3.
Thou rejoicest when as sons we
Raise the sound of praise,
For this song of praise is led by
Thine own Son, O God.

# 4.
Thy love, Father, is our portion,
Thou hast foreseen us
And for Thine own glory’s sake
Made one with Christ.
