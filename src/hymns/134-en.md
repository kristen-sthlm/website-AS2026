# 1.
Now is the time of favour!
Behold, now is the day of salvation!
O sinner, seek your soul’s peace
And take Jesus’ redemption.

# 2.
Now is the time of favour!
Behold, Jesus invites you now!
Tomorrow it may be too late,
O, why dost thou delay?

# 3.
Now is the time of favour!
A gracious "year of the Lord";
Shall He call thee in vain,
And thou dost despise His grace?

# 4.
Now is the time of favour!
Hear Jesus calls: Come!
And Christ Himself proclaims to thee,
That there is still room.
