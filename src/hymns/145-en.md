# 1.
Who knocks so softly in the evening’s peace
Upon thy heart?
Who draws near with healing so sweet and gentle
For the soul’s hidden sorrow?

# 2.
Perchance thou hast heard that knocking before
And that voice?
Why dost thou still delay to open thy door
To life, to heavenly comfort?

# 3.
Thou knowest it oft so desolate and cold
Amidst the world,
Thou feelest it almost as though all were lacking
Despite the gleam of fleeting vanity.

# 4.
Each rose here on earth its thorn indeed hath,
But that peace,
Which Jesus thee giveth, no thorn retaineth,
When once the day hath passed away.

# 5.
What is all earth’s glory and splendour,
And all its joy,
Compared to the bliss and the honour to be wholly His,
And to let Him adorn thee?

# 6.
So open this day to the Friend, who so gracious
Thee endoweth
With treasures far better than finest gold,
And crown and kingdom thee promiseth.
