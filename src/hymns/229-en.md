# 1.
May the grace and love of our Lord
Fill the heart night and day,
And His Holy Spirit guide
Thoughts, words, and deeds!

# 2.
May we wholly trust in God,
Forsake all our own wisdom,
Find all our rest in Christ,
Until with joy we see Him!
