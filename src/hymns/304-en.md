# 1.
In hope our longing glances we lift,
Soon the Morning Star kindly toward us smiles;
How joyful, how sweet shall His coming be,
Ere the Sun in its majesty appears!

# 2.
What joy to behold His countenance,
Which in the night’s darkness has granted us comfort;
To hear His voice, which has so encouraged us,
Whose slightest whisper has filled our breast with peace!

# 3.
If the thought of our Lord’s love here
Already can lift above earth’s dust;
If heaven’s foretaste teaches the weary wanderer
To sing gladly on the way to the Father’s house—

# 4.
What then shall the radiance of His glory,
The greatness of His love from us call forth,
If not praise and worship in eternity,
A deep, united homage to God’s Lamb!
