# 1.
O Christ, Thou art the fountain,
God’s fountain deep and clear,
Of love, grace, and wisdom,
Which eternally is and was.
Its streams we only taste
On this earthly shore,
In deep draughts we drink
In Immanuel’s land.

# 2.
“My Beloved owns me,
And He is eternally mine.”
Well may His beauty fill
Our heart, soul, and mind.
Entirely secure in Him
And in His Father’s hand,
To glory He will bring us
In Immanuel’s land.

# 3.
Our life’s path He directed
In His counsel of love,
And even midst sorrow
Shone the brightness of His grace.
Soon we shall behold Him,
Who has led us by His hand,
And see how the Lamb shines
In Immanuel’s land.

# 4.
As the bride on her bridegroom,
Not on her adornment sees,
So we gaze upon Jesus,
Not on the reward He gives,
Not merely on the glory,
But on His pierced hand.
God’s Lamb is the very brightness
In Immanuel’s land.
