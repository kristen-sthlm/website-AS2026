# 1.
In Jesus’ name there is a fountain
Of grace and joy and blessedness,
Not even angels grasp its depth,
They only marvel at its glory.
In Thee all the fulness of the Godhead dwells,
In Thee the believer is made complete.

# 2.
Thy love, stronger than death,
Fills every lack for us here,
And beneath all the storms of time
It gives the soul strength and peace.
All that we have is from Thee;
Praise be to Thee eternally!

# 3.
Let earth’s empty joy vanish!
The world’s happiness gives no peace;
We can find nothing therein
To compare with Thy glory.
What riches we possess in Thee!
Thou lovest us eternally.
