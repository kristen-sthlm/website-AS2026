# 1.
No condemnation is there now
For us, thus saith God.
In Christ we pure are found,
And stand in holy garb.

# 2.
With the Father speaks Jesus’ blood
For those He has redeemed;
Them as jewels now He bears
Continually upon His breast.

# 3.
O, what inexpressible grace:
No more condemnation;
For through Jesus’ precious wounds
We are healed.

# 4.
Ah, teach us, Lord, more to see
Upon Thee, God’s sacrifice Lamb,
That we Thy love may comprehend
And praise Thy name on high.
