# 1.
How good is our faithful God,
Our only and eternal Friend.
His love is immeasurable,
His power as boundless as it.

# 2.
Our Saviour, Thou who art able
To keep Thine own from falling,
We thank Thee that Thou art the One
Who has saved us and wilt save us.
