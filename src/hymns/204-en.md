# 1.
God in love encompasses us,
Strengthens our soul.
Freely His favour we enjoy,
All, all is well.
He heals us and guides us,
And in grace preserves us here,
With His hand He defends us,
All shall be well.

# 2.
We in sorrow have perceived
Peace for our soul,
And perfect salvation found,
For all is well.
God never forsakes us,
In Christ we abide,
The Spirit’s guidance helps us,
All shall be well.

# 3.
Awaiting a glorious morning,
Light for our soul,
We sing here beneath the sorrow:
All, all is well.
The Father’s love is our source,
Jesus fully can satisfy,
And in life as in death shall prevail,
All shall be well.
