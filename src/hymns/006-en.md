# 1.
In deep and eternal counsel,
Ere the world was brought forth,
Before its deep foundation
Was laid upon nothing,
God chose us for bliss,
In the Son took us up,
To be like Him,
When our course here is ended.

# 2.
We are now accepted
In Him who came down
To die, but is now exalted
With God in glory.
In grace that changeth not,
He bears us day by day,
His goodness we experience
On the pilgrim path here.

# 3.
When the morning of glory
One day breaks in here,
It shall scatter all darkness
That is in men’s minds.
He shall then come in brightness,
Whom all shall see,
In glory and honor
With us He shall appear.

# 4.
For He who came from heaven
Down here to the cross’s woe,
Shall soon perfect all things,
We shall see Him!
And, like Him, known.
We shall then know as we are known.
We eagerly await Him,
Who won our hearts.
