# 1.
Behold, the glory shines
Down on my path of pilgrimage,
It speaks of His love,
Who gave Himself for me.
Though here in a wilderness
A little time I walk,
The Lord Jesus strengthens me
With grace that all avails.

# 2.
Behold, the door of heaven stands open,
I see my Father’s home,
And the journey grows shorter,
I have not far again.
I travel to the good land,
Which already in faith I have reached,
To behold Him who with me
Has gone the whole way.

# 3.
His sweet voice calls me
To come where He is;
My soul hastens toward the goal,
I will not rest here.
My spirit’s wings lift themselves,
My homeland I shall soon reach,
And the glory shines
From the Father’s house brightly.
