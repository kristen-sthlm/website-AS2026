# 1.
Jesus, our ascended Lord,
In Thy name we break the bread
And bring forth our heart’s thanksgiving
For Thy wondrous death.

# 2.
This cup, which we bless,
Witnesses of Thy precious blood,
Which speaks in the sanctuary
And gives us holy boldness.

# 3.
Within “the doors being shut”
We may remember Thee in peace;
Without, all care is left,
The heart’s unrest, the earth’s strife.

# 4.
To the place which Thou hast chosen,
Thy Spirit hath directed our path.
In the fellowship of eternal life
We worship, praise Thee.

# 5.
As “brethren” gathered together,
Brethren, O Son of God, with Thee,
To Thy voice we would hearken,
The voice which hath life in itself.

# 6.
Never more, O Lord Jesus,
Shalt Thou taste sorrow and death.
In Thy victory Thou hast won for us
Peace and life in abundance.

# 7.
And the life Thou didst win in death
Shall fill a world of joy.
The fountain with its rich streams
Flows forth from Zion’s height.
