# 1.
Thanks, Lord Jesus Christ,
That all our sin and lack
Hast Thou, God’s Lamb,
Removed on that day,
When in our stead Thou stoodst
And didst let Thy blood flow
Upon the cross’s stem!

# 2.
Thou wast the offering Lamb
For Jews, Gentiles
Upon this earth.
That offering shall avail
Before God in eternity
For the salvation
Of those who believe God’s word.

# 3.
In heaven itself
For us Thou dost appear
Before Thy God;
And in His sanctuary
In assurance of faith we may
Proceed in worship
In holy attire.
