# 1.
O what a blessed world,
Where sin cannot reach!
Our soul is now taught by the Spirit
To eagerly long for it.

# 2.
And this world of light,
Far above the earth raised,
There is our home, our Father’s house
Of blessedness and joy.

# 3.
Our soul shall enjoy
In eternity long
A sweet rest, for God Himself is
Its joy and song of gladness.

# 4.
Our God fills all,
Where all is measureless.
A host of saints without number
Bears forth His praise and worship.

# 5.
In glory so clear
As precious jasper stone
His love yet more wondrous
Far surpasses its radiance.

# 6.
Our God, whom we have learned
In Jesus to know here,
Content with all His own work
In love rests there.

# 7.
And Jesus Christ, God’s Son,
Firstborn among many brethren,
He, who has suffered the cross’s woe,
Is all heaven’s song.

# 8.
Thy love, O our God,
Can fill every soul
And gives now in heavenly light
That which soon shall be our portion.
