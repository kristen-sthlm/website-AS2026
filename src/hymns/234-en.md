# 1.
Abide with me; behold, the day’s close is nigh.
Abide, O Lord, soon night is here.
When all else fails and deceives,
Thou only faithful Comforter, abide!

# 2.
As dreams flee, so hasten our years.
All earth’s joy like a breath passes away.
All hurries on to its transformation soon.
Thou art the same, abide Thou with me!

# 3.
Not merely a glance, a word I ask,
Nay, as to Emmaus Thou didst of old come
And bore the heavy burden of sorrowing brethren,
Come, not to visit only, abide with me!

# 4.
Thou alone canst subdue the threat of darkness
And give strength to stand against the tempter.
Each moment I have need of Thy presence;
In cold, in sun abide, Lord, with me!

# 5.
As an infant I was laid in Thy love’s bosom
And was blessed in Thy precious name.
Thou hast not left me, though on paths of error
I often failed Thee. O, abide with me!

# 6.
I fear not, when Thou art near in grace;
Then pain departs, counsel is never lacking,
Of the grave’s terror not a trace remains,
And death has lost its sting, when I have Thee.

# 7.
Thy cross shall shine before my gaze, when last
I fall asleep, O Lord Jesus Christ.
Then night departs, morning breaks clear.
In life, in death abide, Lord, with me!
