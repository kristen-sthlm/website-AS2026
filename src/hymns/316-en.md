# 1.
What it will be with God one day
To behold the countenance of Jesus,
Our Saviour, our Priest who here
Became so precious and so near to us!

# 2.
Soon we shall stand at Christ’s judgment seat,
With Him look back upon
The path where, secure by His hand,
We walked through the land of death.

# 3.
See how He, so tender and good,
Imparted to us here new strength and courage,
And with His gaze restored
The one who had gone astray from Him.

# 4.
There, more clearly than ever before, we shall see
The grace which God our Saviour
Has shown us in the wilderness
With faithfulness so wondrous.

# 5.
May we, on the way to the Father’s house,
Be preserved in His peace and light,
Walk onward in joyful expectation,
With our gaze fixed on the Lamb of God.
