# 1.
Once on the path of death I was,
But the way of life I have found,
And beyond the wilderness now I see
The land which God gives to His own.
Here I have not my home,
A wilderness is to me this world,
Here I have not my home.

# 2.
As a stranger only I wander here,
My blessed home above is there.
With Christ first I shall remain,
Not this land has my love.
Here I have not my home,
A wilderness is to me this world,
Here I have not my home.

# 3.
Praise be to God in heaven’s height,
I shall share my Lord’s joy
In Jerusalem in rest,
The heavenly, where I may dwell!
O there, there is my home,
A wilderness is to me this world,
Here I have not my home.
