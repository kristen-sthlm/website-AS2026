# 1.
Thou art worthy of praise and thanksgiving,
Of honour and of glory.
Of Thee, O Lamb of God, who wast slain,
Our song shall be unto eternity.

# 2.
Thou sittest now upon the Father’s throne
In the light and splendour of glory;
There upon Thy heart Thou dost bear us
In the power of love and life.

# 3.
To Thee we now raise our song.
Though the tribute be small, indeed.
No mortal tongue can express
Thy full worth, Jesus Christ.

# 4.
Yet shalt Thou have a higher praise,
When once in the clouds Thou shalt appear;
Then shall we in perfect measure
Render Thee, O Christ, our homage.
