# 1.
In hope my redeemed soul is content,
In faith that God to eternal good exalts me,
When I consider that death’s chains
Christ has broken and bestowed life upon me.

# 2.
In Him the path of bliss I have found,
And the heavenly inheritance He has secured for me;
With shepherd arms He embraces me
And thus bears me until I reach heaven.

# 3.
An eternal, imperishable joy it shall be,
Which the God of peace in heaven’s height gives us,
Where Jesus’ friends from all lands
Praise Him, whom they know as their own.

# 4.
Soon shall we come to the glory
And dwell with Thee and all Thy godly;
For Thou wilt turn for the afflicted
All sorrow and need into joy without end.

# 5.
Keep us prepared, Jesus Christ, to await
Thy sweet coming, when Thou Thyself shalt fetch
From the vale of tears, from earth’s sorrows,
Thy bride to Thee in the bright hall of joy.
