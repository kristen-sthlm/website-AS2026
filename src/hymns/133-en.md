# 1.
Now the day of grace is come,
In faith go to the cross,
For the best of works
Cannot save thee,
For the best of works
Cannot save thee.

# 2.
In the Lord Jesus Christ,
Who died on the cross here,
Perfect salvation is found,
His work is finished,
Perfect salvation is found,
His work is finished.

# 3.
And God looks upon Christ,
His blood’s outpouring,
For the sinner has brought forth
A full forgiveness,
For the sinner has brought forth
A full forgiveness.

# 4.
And still Jesus says,
What He has said before:
Behold, him that cometh unto Me,
I will in no wise cast out,
Behold, him that cometh unto Me,
I will in no wise cast out.

# 5.
For him who works not,
But believes on Christ alone,
God has prepared a salvation
And bliss without measure,
God has prepared a salvation
And bliss without measure.
