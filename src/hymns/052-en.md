# 1.
The Lord has risen! The Red Sea’s flood
For us He passed through, and with His blood
We were dearly bought, He bore our judgment
Death is conquered! Jesus’ grave is empty!

# 2.
Thou hast risen, and in Thy dark grave
Our mass of sin was buried, Thy life Thou gavest
And didst resume; us judgment can never reach,
In life and resurrection we stand.

# 3.
Thou hast risen, we now Thy life have received
And walk the desert path Thy foot has trod.
With Thee now one, in the holiness’ rest
We may worship, in God’s favour abide.

# 4.
Thou hast risen, our Redeemer!
Severe was the conflict Thou didst endure.
Thou didst gain the victory, in glory Thou art,
By the Father crowned, we see Thee, Jesus, there!

# 5.
Our Lord Christ! As Forerunner
Thou hast entered, we long to see Thee,
Behold all Thy grace and sin no more
And eternally, eternally be where Thou art.
