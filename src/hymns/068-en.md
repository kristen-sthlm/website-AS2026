# 1.
Our Lord Jesus Christ,
Who once died for us,
What joy to see Thee now exalted
And praise Thee with song.

# 2.
In love was Thy path,
Which God alone understood;
Ever the word of grace from Thy mouth
Gave sorrowing hearts courage.

# 3.
O love, which so brightly
Despite sorrow and opposition burned,
A love which has purchased us for God
And won full atonement.

# 4.
A love which for us
Into death’s valley descended,
And the light of God’s heart-law
In the world’s darkness spread.

# 5.
And now with glory crowned,
High above the heavens,
Thou sittest on Thy Father’s throne,
His heart’s Beloved.

# 6.
Soon we in glory
With Thee shall reign,
Thy love to Thine own then
Shall be seen by all the world.

# 7.
Thee, Jesus, we await,
Thou art our soul’s desire.
Our joy it shall be eternally
To be where Thou art.
