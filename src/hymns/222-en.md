# 1.
From pain and sorrow
I flee to my stronghold,
To the Father’s merciful heart.

# 2.
In weakness and need
With Him a support
I find, which never fails me.

# 3.
He gives me comfort
At a fatherly breast
And helps, when none can help.

# 4.
He knows what I am,
Yet He loves me,
My Father, forever the same.

# 5.
Ah, who is like Thee,
So good and so rich,
In heaven or on earth!
