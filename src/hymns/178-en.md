# 1.
Precious name, that name Jesus!
God’s, the Most High’s, Son,
Thou for us in love didst leave
Heaven’s throne.

# 2.
Jesus, name that for us speaks
Of Thy lowliness here.
Thou, who above all kings,
Art King.

# 3.
Jesus! Thou, who to us didst say
On Thy desert path:
“I am meek, of heart lowly,
Learn of Me!”

# 4.
Thou art He who for us didst suffer
Distress and poverty,
And on the cross’s tree hast borne
All our judgment.

# 5.
Jesus! Thou for us didst let flow
Here Thy precious blood,
Which now proclaims to all:
God is good.

# 6.
Jesus, name in glory
Above all exalted,
Name by angels highly admired,
Heaven’s joy!
