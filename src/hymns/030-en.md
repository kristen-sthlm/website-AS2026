# 1.
It was the night, O Lord,
When all strove
To cast the shadows of darkness
Upon Thy holy mind,
Thy voice of love we hear:
Do this in remembrance of Me!
With thankful hearts’ response
We now may remember Thee.

# 2.
We think upon the darkness
That closed around Thy spirit,
Upon those deep waves
That then broke over Thee.
O, there Thy grace immeasurable,
Thy love’s depth we see,
And give thankful response
In Thy remembrance.

# 3.
Until Thou, O Lord, comest
And callest us to Thyself,
To rest in Thy presence,
A cloudless day with Thee,
We may proclaim Thy death
With longing inward,
That we may become more like it,
In that we remember Thee.
