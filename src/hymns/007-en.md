# 1.
In the depth of God’s heart
There lay a great thought;
That thought was
So wondrous,
A counsel of the Most High,
Where pure love dwells.
In the depth of God’s heart
Lay hidden a great thought.

# 2.
From eternity God thought
Of a chosen bride,
In Christ’s beauty,
The reward of His toil,
Who, before the world was created,
Was foreseen by God.
From eternity God thought
Of Christ and His bride.

# 3.
He is the bright shining
Of all God’s glory.
In Him was
God manifest,
And the bride shall proclaim
His praise in eternity,
He who is the reflection
Of all God’s glory.
