# 1.
Thee, O Jesus, now our song we raise,
Thou who hast loved us without cause,
And for Thee now our knees we bow;
Thou hast redeemed us and done it well.

# 2.
All our sins Thou hast put away,
Thou Lamb of God without spot or blemish,
With Thy precious blood hast cleansed us;
Lord Jesus, all Thou hast done well.

# 3.
Whatsoever befalls here on the pilgrim path,
We may praise Thee, Immanuel,
Sing in joy, beneath the tears:
All Thou doest, Thou doest, Lord, well!

# 4.
Thou hast gone away to prepare us a place
In Thy Father’s blissful home, yet still
Thy Spirit shall lead us so surely;
Lord, Thou shalt make all things well.

# 5.
And when time’s toil is past,
We shall as with one soul
To Thy glory sing this song:
Lord Jesus, all Thou hast done well!
