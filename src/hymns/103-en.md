# 1.
To Thee, our God and gracious Father,
We bring our praise and worship home,
And with a holy boldness now
We sing unto Thy glory.
Thy Son Thyself revealed here,
And made that we may draw near
To Thee Thyself within the sanctuary.

# 2.
Our God, we heartily thank Thee,
Who didst Thine only Son send,
He, who is God, became like men,
O love without an end!
He was obedient unto death upon the cross,
Thou hast given Him in abundance
Life, glory, and honour.

# 3.
We praise and worship Thee,
Thy name be ever praised,
O Father, Thou who in grace
Hast with all good endowed us!
All things Thou holdest in Thy power,
Before Thee not a sigh is hid,
Thou knowest every heart.

# 4.
We now Thy eternal purpose
With thankfulness receive.
Thine only Son Thou gavest forth
To gain many sons.
Thou hast brought us into Thy house,
Where Thou Thyself dost dwell in holy light
And makest Thyself known as Father.
