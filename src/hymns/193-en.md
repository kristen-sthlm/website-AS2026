# 1.
O Lord, when we reflect on
How great Thy love is,
We long to behold Thee
And to be where Thou art.

# 2.
Yet though we walk here in the world
As in a wilderness land,
Where dangers we ever meet,
Still Thy hand shields us.

# 3.
And in Thy love we find
Such abundant consolation,
That each day we must sing
Thy praise with joyful voice.

# 4.
Our life, our light, our beloved Lord,
Our rock and our rest!
May every thought of Thee grant us
Renewed consolation and faith.

# 5.
Our Saviour, ah, help us Thou
To follow Thee faithfully here
The little time, until we Thee
May behold as Thou art.
