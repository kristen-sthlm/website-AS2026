# 1.
The Victor-Prince Himself a company
Leads onward to the glory,
With the wondrous battle-cry:
"Glory to God’s ascended Lamb!"

# 2.
He gave His blood to make peace for us,
God in grace looks down upon us;
Clouds may seem to spread abroad,
Nothing changes Him there.

# 3.
When our foe reveals
Our sins like a flood,
Jesus Himself defends us,
Points to His own blood.

# 4.
Oh, how secure a redeemed company
Moves forward to the haven of peace!
God in Christ preserves us,
Life we have received in His name.

# 5.
Soon in the glory
We shall sing a well-known song,
New yet through all eternity:
"Worthy is the Lamb, who once died!"
