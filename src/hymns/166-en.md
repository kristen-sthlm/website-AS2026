# 1.
I have a Friend, O what a Friend,
Ere I Him knew,
He drew me with such tender bonds of love,
And turned me here to Himself.
The bonds which never can be broken,
He has bound around my heart,
For I am His and He is mine
Forever and eternally!

# 2.
I have a Friend, O what a Friend!
He has saved me from distress.
He gave not only His precious life,
Himself He gave in death.
Not anything of what I have
Do I now call my own,
My heart and my life, my all
Is His, yes His eternally!

# 3.
And Jesus is the name of this Friend,
So good, so true, so worthy,
My Leader, so wise in counsel,
Defender so mighty!
What can separate me from Him,
Who loves me so faithfully?
Neither life, nor death, nor depth, nor height,
For I am His eternally!
