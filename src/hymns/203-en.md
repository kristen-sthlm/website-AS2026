# 1.
By wondrous ways He goes,
In secret walks our God.
In ocean’s depths His footprints are,
And storm bears forth His word.

# 2.
With inexhaustible power’s might
And wisdom deep beside,
He brings to light from hidden veins,
Almighty, what He wills.

# 3.
Ye saints, now take courage!
The cloud your fear imparts
Is filled with grace, and soon its flood
Shall stream down over you.

# 4.
God’s purpose soon shall be revealed,
And grace alone is there.
Though bud’s taste bitter be,
The fruit shall sweet yet be.

# 5.
Blind unbelief ever errs,
When bold it would control God;
But He His way shall make clear,
And Himself interpret His word.
