# 1.
Thou gentle, patient One,
Thy meekness teaches me,
That I Thy yoke may take upon me
And find rest there.

# 2.
O Jesus, Thou art sufficient
To fill soul and mind:
Before Thee all sorrow and anxiety flee,
And Thou Thyself art now mine.

# 3.
Ah, fix my heart’s gaze
Upon Thee more and more,
That, fully satisfied with Thy beauty,
I may see no other.
