# 1.
Our great Salvation Captain, Thou
Art crowned with highest glory.
To Thee, exalted, our song with joy
Ascends, we praise to render.
We magnify and worship Thee,
Thou now our all art become.
To Thee who diedst, O gracious Lord,
The glory, power are given.

# 2.
Thou the cross’s woe didst endure
In boundless love’s endeavour.
Thy blood there flowed, Thou victory gained,
Didst recompense receive for all Thy scorn.
We see Thee as God’s Offering Lamb,
The many sins didst Thou bear.
Lo, dead Thou wast but risen art,
Now the crown of glory Thou hast.

# 3.
As Head of God’s creation,
To heaven ascended,
We now Thee see, Highly Praised,
With glory surrounded.
We rejoice, Lord, beholding
Thee crowned with the Father’s glory.
Thus do we long, with Thee to be
And to Thee our praise to bring home!
