# 1.
This is the great day,
Which Christ has made for us;
The prince of darkness is now defeated,
The gate of light opened for us;
The darkness of the grave has vanished,
The sun of life has now risen.

# 2.
Blessed and glorious was the moment,
When our Saviour rose.
His resurrection is the foundation
For our hope, and with His blood
Christ has secured victory for us
Over all that has ruined us.

# 3.
The Prince of life has now brought forth
Life and incorruptibility.
All is laid at His feet,
He is crowned with glory;
God in Christ is glorified,
The Father’s love revealed.

# 4.
Raise praise with soul and tongue,
Lift up our heart and our voice
To praise and sing in worship
Jesus, who has redeemed us!
God our Father, Thee we honour,
The fruit of our lips we bring home.
