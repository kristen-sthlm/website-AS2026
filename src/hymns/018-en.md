# 1.
Ere a particle of dust was laid
Of this creation,
Thou wast, O Christ, in presence,
Thy Father’s Beloved.
Then it was God chose us in Thee,
The Firstborn,
In Thee, O Jesus Christ,
He blessed us with grace.

# 2.
We see Thee, Lord Jesus,
On earth walking,
A holy, solitary stranger,
Toward heaven gazing;
For us Thou didst suffer pain,
Sorrow, need, and poverty,
That we might possess
Thy joy, Thy riches.

# 3.
We see Thee with Thy crown
Of sharp thorns here,
How obedient unto death
Thy cross Thou humbly bearest;
Despising all shame,
Thyself Thou didst offer,
When outside the city’s gates
They crucified Thee.

# 4.
We see Thee, Lord Jesus,
With the Father’s glory crowned;
For all Thy soul’s travail
He hath well rewarded Thee.
The pearl, which Thou didst seek,
Didst find and dearly purchase here,
Shall to Thine honour shine
In eternal beauty there.

# 5.
Thy rich grace we praise
Now and evermore.
In Thee God hath His rest,
We rest also in Thee.
With our lips’ sacrifice,
Thy name hymning,
Now unto our God we go forth
With joy worshipping.
