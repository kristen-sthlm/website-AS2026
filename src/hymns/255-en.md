# 1.
Thanks that Thou meetest me, dear Lord,
Just where I am,
Thou knowest every heart’s concern.
Thanks that Thou fillest for each one
The vessel he has,
Whether it hold more or less.

# 2.
Thanks that Thou carest for the weak and small,
Them Thou dost feed,
That strengthened on the way they may hasten,
Glad in the hope soon to see
Their Saviour,
And enjoy His undisturbed rest.

# 3.
Though but so little I comprehend here
Of what Thou art,
And all Thy love to me hath given,
I know, O Lord, that I am Thine,
And Thou art mine;
Thyself art the eternal life.
