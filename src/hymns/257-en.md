# 1.
Whom have we but Thee
Our thirst to quench here?
Where every earthly spring runs dry,
Thou alone art eternal.

# 2.
Our heart Thou dost fasten
On heaven’s things with Thee;
Ah, should the bride then let herself
Be ensnared by the earth!

# 3.
Thou dost abundantly give us all,
Not as the world gives.
Thou who, to purchase us for God,
Didst shed Thy blood here.

# 4.
None like God
Can esteem that blood,
Which once for our salvation
Flowed for us on the cross.

# 5.
Ever more we enjoy,
On the ground of this blood,
Our Father’s tender love now—
So rich, so great, so good.
