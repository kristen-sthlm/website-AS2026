# 1.
O God, grant us grace in faith to stand,
Despite every foe’s threat,
And with Thy joy within the heart to go
Toward every suffering!

# 2.
Grant us a faith that bows itself
Silently beneath chastening’s stroke,
And rests content, our God, in Thee
Within the dark day.

# 3.
A faith that in the night of distress
Shows itself yet stronger,
And the bow which Thou hast set in the sky
Clearly through the storm sees.

# 4.
Increase, Lord, thus our faith,
The faith that on Thee looks,
Which over sin and world prevails
In the conflict here.

# 5.
When faith and hope and prayer are done,
Thou, Lord, hast promised to give
The crown of righteousness, fair,
To the overcomers.
