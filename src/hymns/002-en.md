# 1.
Thou art great, our God and Father,
In Thy love’s power,
In the eternal atonement,
Which the Son hath accomplished.

# C.
Holy, holy, holy is Thy name;
A sacrifice of praise unto Thine honour
May we bring forth.

# 2.
Christ, Thou our salvation’s Captain,
Thine are we,
And Thou art our life, our Head,
Thus shall it be eternally.

# 3.
Unto Thy wondrous name
May we gather here,
Teach us to know what the precious
Name doth signify.

# 4.
Through grace may we now behold
Thy glory,
Into the sanctuary to enter
With boldness.
