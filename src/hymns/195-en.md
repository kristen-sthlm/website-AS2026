# 1.
Thou, who in love didst die,
Jesus, for me,
Nothing I keep then
Back from Thee;
Nay, take me, Lord, hold fast
So with Thy love, that
My heart may gladly offer
All for Thee.

# 2.
Thou, who to the throne of grace
Wast given for me,
Lord, of grace give me
Strength from Thee,
Content my cross to bear,
Praising Thee, and so
Doing, though feebly, yet
Something for Thee.

# 3.
Lord, renew continually
Thy strength in me,
That I may every moment
Live for Thee,
Winning some soul may gain,
Healing some wound,
Doing, wherever I go,
All for Thee.

# 4.
Thou hast with mighty hand
Wholly laid hold of me;
Drawn by bonds of love,
I follow Thee;
And when in heaven’s height
I shall Thee see with joy,
I shall enjoy, eternally satisfied,
All with Thee.
