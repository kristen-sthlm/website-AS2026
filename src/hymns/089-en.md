# 1.
Let us sing,
Sing of Jesus’ grace;
Old and young,
Praise His counsel of love,
Praise His precious grace!

# 2.
Heaven and earth
Are full of His glory;
Yet He has become
Poor for mankind’s sake,
The children of dust and clay.

# 3.
Willingly He took
Sickness and need upon Him,
Patiently endured
All that may press me
Here on my wilderness path.

# 4.
The judgment He bore,
Tasted also the death of the cross,
To redeem us
From our sin and need,
Yes, from an eternal death.

# 5.
All to win
Himself a chosen bride,
Who might find
Grace and favour before God
Only in His own robe.

# 6.
Let us sing
Eternally of this Friend!
Heart and tongue
Praise His love still
Here and in heaven!
