# 1.
O man, who walkest in the world
And seekest in sin thy joy,
Yet in the whirl of pleasures thou
Canst not feel happy and satisfied.

# C.
Arise, be free; the world cannot make thee satisfied,
Jesus, Christ, is He who can give thee joy.

# 2.
Thou hast drunk thy fill from the wells
Which the world to youth doth provide,
But found that remorse and shame
Were at the bottom of the cup.

# 3.
Thou hast found in solitary moments
God spoken so mightily to thee,
And said with a moving voice:
O man, give thy heart to Me!

# 4.
Say, dost thou not now feel a calling
To turn back unto God,
And become a living Christian,
And have thy joy in His commandments?

# 5.
Why dost thou still hesitate to seek
Thy gladness in the beloved Saviour?
He alone can give that bliss
Which eternally abiding is.
Arise, be free,
etc.
