# 1.
While time swiftly hastens,
Hark at the heart’s door!
Jesus knocks, waits, tarries,
Knocks now as before.
Knowest thou what it is that moveth
Within thy soul?
Jesus knocks, the voice is heard:
Open, let Me in!

# 2.
Say, O soul, wilt thou not hearken
To Jesus’ voice today?
Open now thine heart’s ear,
Receive salvation!
Then with joy shalt thou perceive
That He Himself is thine,
Heaven’s door for thee is opened,
Thou mayest enter in.
