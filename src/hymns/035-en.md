# 1.
Thy supper, Lord, so good,
Speaks of Thee,
Thou gavest Thy body, Thy blood,
Established peace.
A Man of sorrows Thou wast,
When here Thy cross Thou didst bear;
Glory now Thou hast
For everlasting time.

# 2.
Thanks for Thy word of grace,
Light on our path!
Thanks for Thy rich table,
Spread by Thee!
With this wine and bread
We now commemorate Thy death;
As Thou Thyself didst bid us,
We remember Thee.
