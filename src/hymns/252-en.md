# 1.
O Jesus, how sweetly our breast is filled
At the thought of Thee and Thy name!
Yet sweeter it becomes to hear Thy voice
And rest in peace in Thy bosom.

# 2.
No clearest voices, no ringing brass
Can give a more beautiful sound,
Than when we hear Thy hallowed name,
O Saviour of men, God!

# 3.
To Thee the burdened hearts turn,
Thou art the joy of the humble.
Thy gentleness toward all who pray we have known,
Thy goodness we have experienced here.

# 4.
Thy love, O Lord, all joy it can give,
No tongue can express here,
No pen can describe, only they know it,
Who belong to Thee, dear Lord.

# 5.
O Jesus, our eternal joy Thou art,
Our victory reward Thou shalt be!
May Thou be our glory on the journey here
And then in eternity all!
