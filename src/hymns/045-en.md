# 1.
What hast Thou, Jesus, done for me?
Thou hast Thyself given up.
My sin was great, Thy grace was free,
A sacrificial Lamb Thou hast become.
Now exalted on Thy Father’s throne,
His glory crowns Thee,
For all Thy soul hath suffered here,
He richly there rewards Thee.
Jesus, my Lord,
How sweet Thy name, O Jesus!
When shall I see Thy countenance,
My precious Lord Jesus?

# 2.
Thou shalt come, Thou comest soon,
So hath Thy Spirit written,
To fetch the children home to God,
Whom He to Thee hath given.
Then shall I behold Thee
With joy, my Lord Jesus,
And through unutterable grace
I shall be like Thee, O Jesus!
Jesus, my Lord,
How sweet Thy name, O Jesus!
Soon shall I dwell with Thee forever,
My precious Lord Jesus!
