# 1.
Of all Thou in Thy love dost give,
Our God, infinitely good,
No greater gift the heavens see
Than Christ’s precious blood.

# 2.
And may the selfsame love us move
To trust that blood alone,
For without it to seek repose
Would then be all in vain.

# 3.
We therefore would extol Thee
For all to us bestowed,
For the Saviour, who gave Himself,
Thy Son, who is Thy beloved.
