# 1.
Only a day, a moment at a time;
O, what comfort whatever may come!
All indeed rests in our Father’s hands,
Should we as children then be anxious?
He, who has a father’s heart for us,
O, He gives for each day
Its small portion of joy and sorrow,
Both of toil and of delight.

# 2.
Himself He is near to us every day
With special grace for each particular time.
Each day’s care He will bear,
He who is called both Strength and Counsel.
To preserve His precious possession,
This care He has taken upon Himself.
“As thy day, so shall thy strength be,”
This promise He gave to me.

# 3.
Help us then to rest securely and quietly
Only with Thee, our beloved Lord Jesus,
And not to waste the precious comfort of faith,
Which is preserved for us in the word!
Help us, Lord, that whatever may happen
Always to take from Thy gentle hand
Only a day, a moment at a time,
Until we reach the good land.
