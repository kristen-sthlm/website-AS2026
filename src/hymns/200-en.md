# 1.
"Not of this world," thus sounded the Lord's word
To the few who followed Him on our earth;
Now to us they resound on our pilgrim journey,
Drawing our hearts to God's purposeful world.

# 2.
Not of this world! From it He bought us for His good Father
With His precious blood.
Not of this world, now we may sing,
As we walk through the wilderness in His footsteps.

# 3.
Not of this world, oh what sweet rest!
Peace, strength, and longing dwell in those words.
We have our citizenship in heaven,
May this certainty mark us in every way.

# 4.
Father! We pray to Thee, teach our hearts
To expect nothing where Christ is not;
But to sing from the heart on our desert path:
"All our springs are in Thee."
