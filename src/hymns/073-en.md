# 1.
Let us praise the love of the Lord Jesus,
Who faithfully unto the death of the cross,
For us has endured scorn and mockery,
And drank the cup of judgment and distress.
Our Lord, to Thee we now bring home
Praise, thanksgiving, and eternal glory!

# 2.
Behold, this flame of divine love,
Which no power can extinguish,
That even in the agony of death
Burned so heavenly high and faithful.
Our Lord, to Thee we now bring home
Praise, thanksgiving, and eternal glory!

# 3.
Soon shall the new song be heard to ring
To the sound of harps in heaven’s hall,
Where eternally hallelujah sing
The ransomed hosts without number.
We also before the Lord deeply bow
And here exalt the power of His love.
