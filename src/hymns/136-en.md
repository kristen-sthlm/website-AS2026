# 1.
A glance at the Crucified gives thee life,
Yes, even now there is life for thee also!
O look, dear soul, and thus thou shalt receive life,
Look upon Him who offered Himself!

# C.
Look, look, look and live!
A glance at the Crucified gives thee life,
Yes, even now there is life for thee also.

# 2.
Why did He come here to bear our sin,
If He did not take thy burden also?
Why from His side came the cleansing blood,
If it is not sufficient for thee also?

# 3.
Not with thy tears, thy repentance, and prayer,
Only in the blood dost thou now receive cleansing.
And blessed shalt thou be, if thou at once with thy weight
Of sins goest to the Saviour.

# 4.
Then come to thy Saviour, Jesus, with joy,
Take the eternal life which He gives!
Since He, thy righteousness, lives, then know,
That in eternity thou shalt die no more.
