# 1.
O Thou holy Lamb of God,
Who hast given Thyself!
With Thy blood on the cross’s tree
Thou hast written our charter of freedom;
All that here was against us,
In Thy death hath vanished.

# 2.
Before Thee, O Christ, now
We gratefully fall down,
Hidden in Thee are found
All the treasures of wisdom;
Thou art God’s only-begotten Son,
Who hast declared the Father here.

# 3.
Ransomed company, praise God,
Not held captive by the earth,
Let every soul with joyful sound
Take part in the song!
Thee, who didst drink the cup for us,
Lord Jesus, eternal thanks!
