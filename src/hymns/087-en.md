# 1.
Come, let us in childlike wise
Raise the praise of the Lord Jesus!
But say, who is sufficient for this?
Alas, no words here suffice.

# 2.
Behold Jesus, crowned in heaven’s height!
Extol His majesty with joy!
He is the King of kings
And bears the crown of glory.
