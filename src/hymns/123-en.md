# 1.
What grace, O Christ, it is indeed,
That now we are one
With Thee, who such great love hast prepared
For Thine own.

# 2.
By grace alone for our sake
Thou didst come down to earth,
Becamest partaker of flesh and blood,
And for our sin didst suffer here.

# 3.
Our bill of debt Thou didst in love take
And upon the cross didst bear;
Thou didst drain the cup of wrath for us,
And thus hast set us free.

# 4.
Our Head now in heaven
For us Thou sittest there;
Neither life, nor death, nor depth, nor height
Shall ever separate us from Thee.

# 5.
Teach us to understand and testify here
Of the wonder that has come to pass,
That we on earth yet with Thee
In heaven are one.

# 6.
Soon comes the day of glory
With all Thou hast prepared for us,
When Thou for all wilt show
That we with Thee are one!
