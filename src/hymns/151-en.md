# 1.
Wilt thou not hearken to love’s entreaty,
Cease to live unto thyself at pleasure,
Far from the God who hath seen how thou hast wandered,
A stranger to Him day after day?

# 2.
Feelest thou not how long thou hast erred,
And how thou hast ever despised His goodness?
Yet still resounds the gracious message,
Which many a time already thine ear hath reached.

# 3.
O, how He longeth to set thee free,
To lift off the burden of sins so heavy!
In His tenderness He will now enfold thee
In a love that eternally is young.

# 4.
No reproach, no anger
Meeteth thee, for He took the judgment upon Himself!
All God’s righteousness’ holy demand
He fulfilled here on the cross for thee.

# 5.
And when thy journey here below is ended,
Thou shalt before Christ above stand forth
As an eternal witness of His victory,
The victory His love once for us won.
