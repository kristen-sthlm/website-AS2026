# 1.
Hast thou peace? O soul, consider
What that word doth signify!
Hast thou let God find thee,
While He yet is nigh?

# 2.
Hast thou peace with God on high,
Hath He drawn thee unto Himself?
Hath a drop of that joy,
Which He giveth, found place in thee?

# 3.
Or dost thou live in the world,
Still a stranger unto God,
Hast thou yet thy delight in vanity,
Forgetful of thy Lord’s commandments?

# 4.
Dost thou stand hesitating and delay?
O, how canst thou hesitate still?
The world doth not fully satisfy thee,
Yet thou wilt not leave it.

# 5.
Peace He is, who hath broken down
The enmity upon His cross.
He our debt on the cross hath borne,
And He now will save us.

# 6.
O, waste not the hour of grace,
O, consider in time!
If thou believest, then shalt thou find
Jesus becomes thy joy, thy peace.
