# 1.
Not distant is the moment,
When the Lord shall come
In glory in the clouds
With all His saints.

# 2.
The dark night flees,
Soon morning shall appear,
When every pilgrim’s eye shall see
Its Lord’s countenance.

# 3.
In His Father’s house
Our dwelling He has prepared,
Where light and love shall reign
In eternal glory.

# 4.
And without hindrance there
In blessed rest with God
The redeemed hosts shall raise clearly
An eternal song of praise.

# 5.
Come then, O Lord, soon,
Bid the dead arise.
That we also, transformed,
May go to meet Thee!
