# 1.
Our time is in Thy hand,
How sweet it is, Father,
That Thou our life, our soul, our all
Dost bear in Thy hands.

# 2.
Our time is in Thy hand,
Whatever it may give us
Of sorrow, of darkness, joy and light,
In all Thy grace we see.

# 3.
Our time is in Thy hand,
We fear no more.
The Father who chastens, loves,
What comfort it gives the heart!

# 4.
Our time is in Thy hand,
Behold, Jesus, once dead,
He lives now and leads us
With the Shepherd’s tender support.

# 5.
Our time is in Thy hand,
And our Advocate
Is Christ. None can separate
Us from Thy love any more.

# 6.
Our time is in Thy hand,
May we not forget it!
Soon we shall leave the land of sorrow
And see Thy glory.
