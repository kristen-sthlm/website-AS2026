# 1.
Lift the gaze upward and look toward the height,
Soon the morning star rises, bright and clear!
In glory we now behold Him,
Who here on the cross bore our sins.

# 2.
O blessed moment, when the Bridegroom shall come
And fetch home His own precious bride
To the Father’s house with its many dwellings,
To praise Him, clothed in snow-white raiment!

# 3.
He shall present His assembly glorious
Without any wrinkle or spot,
That there forever she may shine unto His glory
In His own beauty, pure and comely.

# 4.
O Lord, kindle in us a holy longing,
Like that which Thou didst formerly find in Thy people here,
When, in all their waiting to behold Thee,
Thy love burned clearly in their hearts!
